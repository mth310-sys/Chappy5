Research labels remain separated as `SOURCED FACT / INTERPRETATION / REUSABLE PRINCIPLE / UNRESOLVED`.

# 1. What the five-Director study now establishes

## SOURCED FACT / RECORDED BASELINE
The current main research shows multiple fundamentally different real pachislot products can remain recognizably pachislot while assigning very different jobs to the same lever, physical reels and STOP buttons.

- Normal/reach-eye machines can make ordinary play itself the product through reel arrangement, stopping control, slip, reach eyes, forecast sounds, lamps and accumulated player knowledge.
- Skill machines can convert STOP into an accuracy input whose success changes efficiency, payout or later game value.
- AT/ST/CZ machines can change the value of the same role or same reel result according to state, navigation and remaining opportunity.
- Game-number/mode machines can create expectation through time/state landmarks, but risk turning reels into transport between landmarks.
- Current smart-slot products do not converge on one design. In 2026, smart-slot normal/BT machines still explicitly sell reach-eye discovery and slip information rather than only high-output upper-state structures.

Sources already recorded on main include official Universal product pages, manufacturer/developer interviews and specialist pachislot analysis. Additional current verification this run:
- Universal `スマスロ サンダーV` (released March 2026) explicitly positions a normal/BT smart-slot around hit frequency, reach-eye collection and slip-count display: https://www.universal-777.com/product/slot/thunder_v_2026/
- Universal `スマスロ ハナビ` (released February 2026) explicitly retains the series game feel while adding long-term discovery/collection features: https://www.universal-777.com/product/slot/hanabi_2026/
- A developer interview on a current smart-slot example describes intentionally suppressing an obvious flash in a high-value state so that the reel result itself becomes the surprise, confirming that modern presentation can deliberately get quieter to preserve reel meaning: https://pachiseven.jp/articles/detail/22757

## INTERPRETATION
The strongest correction to the original Chappy5 planning is now clear:

**Pachislot reality does not come from decorating three reels with a theme. It comes from a repeated contract between internal lottery, reel control, player attention, physical input, state value and presentation restraint.**

The five Directors have learned many ingredients. What remains weak is not another list of machine types; it is the quantitative and temporal relationship between those ingredients during ordinary repeated play.

# 2. Three remaining fatal Reality gaps before concept work may resume

## GAP A — `NORMAL-PLAY ECONOMY`
### Question
What exactly sustains 100G–500G of ordinary play in each major machine family, and how often must meaningful information/reward conversion occur before the loop becomes dead waiting?

### Why this is fatal
All five research lines independently converge here:
- Game & Reel: reel meaning differs by machine family.
- Sound: recurring base events dominate long-session fatigue.
- Visual: the machine spends most of its life in REST / ordinary state.
- QA: a proposal must survive ordinary repetitions, not just rare peaks.
- Machine: product identity changes according to what players are waiting for or reading.

We currently understand the categories qualitatively, but not enough of their **cadence**.

### Research required next
For at least 2–3 concrete real machines per family, document a representative ordinary-play timeline and identify:
- What the player is watching each game.
- What can materially change on an ordinary lever-on.
- Frequency and purpose of rare roles / precursor / navigation / reach-eye opportunities.
- Whether an unsuccessful event leaves information, state residue or nothing.
- How a 100G block is segmented.
- What a 500G dry spell actually consists of.
- Which interactions are optional discovery versus mandatory procedure.

Families: reach-eye/normal, skill, AT/ST, CZ-driven, mode/game-number, current smart-slot.

### Exit condition
Machine Director can describe each family’s 100G survival principle without relying on vague terms such as “期待感”, “演出”, “ポイント” or “出目が楽しい”.

---

## GAP B — `REWARD LADDER & FAILURE TRUTH`
### Question
How do successful real machines preserve the value of ordinary hits and failed routes when stronger upper states exist?

### Why this is fatal
Current Chappy5 research already identifies a major modern-product risk: if upper state owns nearly all emotional and payout value, normal BONUS/AT becomes a failed attempt rather than a reward.

Similarly, CZ/precursor failure can either:
- change future state/information,
- provide a credible clean loss,
- or become repeated fake “almost” presentation.

We have the principle but not enough cross-machine evidence.

### Research required next
Across real machines with different architectures, document:
- First-hit routes and what an ordinary hit independently pays/feels like.
- What upper state adds that ordinary reward does not.
- Whether the player can have a satisfying 3-hour session without upper entry.
- What remains after CZ/AT/ST failure: state, mode, accumulated value, information, or true reset.
- Post-end return windows and their actual gameplay purpose.
- How end-state truth is communicated so the next BET or stop decision is credible.

### Exit condition
We can distinguish a **reward ladder** from an **upper-state funnel**, and a **true failed route** from theatrical false residue.

---

## GAP C — `PHYSICAL / SENSORY BASELINE OVER TIME`
### Question
What does an actual player repeatedly see, touch and hear during ordinary play, and which parts of cabinet/audio/mechanism are intentionally inactive most of the time?

### Why this is fatal
Visual and Sound studies corrected many previous misconceptions, but the evidence is still weighted toward feature descriptions and individual examples. Chappy5 must eventually compress a physical cabinet into iPhone presentation without turning the machine into a flat UI or permanently “on” spectacle.

### Research required next
Cross-era/cross-cabinet study should quantify or closely document:
- Seated eyeline relative to reels/LCD/operation deck.
- Reel-window proportion and actual visual dominance during normal play.
- Lever/MAX BET/STOP reach and repetition burden.
- Base illumination versus chance/hit illumination.
- Speaker/source locations and which sounds are mechanical versus authored.
- Mechanism REST position and actual activation frequency.
- Reward feedback differences between medal and medal-less/smart-slot play.
- What disappears physically in smart-slot operation and what other feedback takes over.
- Long-session sensory fatigue: repeated clicks, high-frequency cues, low-frequency vibration, forced screen attention.

### Exit condition
The team can define an evidence-based `ORDINARY MACHINE STATE` before defining any rare presentation state.

# 3. Machine-family map — what makes ordinary 100G survive

This is an integration model, not a concept recommendation.

| Family | Ordinary 100G survival principle | Lever/Reel/STOP role | Main danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal / reach-eye | Discovery, contradiction, slip, role denial, hit inference | Observe and reveal internal change through stopping outcome | Too opaque for newcomers or reduced to memorization |
| Skill / technical | Player execution and improvement | STOP becomes accuracy/knowledge input at selected moments | Fatigue and accessibility if demanded too often |
| ART / AT / ST | Same role/game changes value by state and remaining opportunity | Execute navigation, recognize rare-role opportunity, read state | Normal state becomes transport to AT |
| CZ-driven | Repeated entry opportunities segment time and convert roles into challenge value | Roles/STOPs matter differently in challenge state | Repeated failure becomes fake “almost” loop |
| Mode / game-number | Hidden-state inference and temporal landmarks create continuation logic | Reels may be secondary unless role/state relationship stays alive | Dead waiting between zones |
| Current smart-slot | Not one family: medal-less platform supports both reel-centric normal/BT and high-output AT designs | Depends on chosen product identity | Treating “smart-slot” itself as game design |

## REUSABLE PRINCIPLE
A future Chappy5 concept must state **which ordinary-play economy it belongs to**, or explicitly define a new one. It may combine families, but the combination must explain how player attention changes over time instead of merely stacking systems.

# 4. New research conclusion for Machine Director

## INTERPRETATION
The team is not ready to resume original concept ranking.

The first research loop is sufficient to reject several old assumptions, but insufficient to answer three product-level questions with evidence:
1. the cadence of ordinary 100G–500G,
2. the relationship between ordinary reward, failure and upper reward,
3. the physical/sensory baseline of the machine during the 90% of time when nothing spectacular is happening.

## REUSABLE PRINCIPLE
Before any new original proposal, require a `REALITY FOUNDATION` containing:
- ordinary-play economy,
- attention contract,
- reward ladder,
- failure truth,
- ordinary physical state,
- 30-minute and 3-hour rhythm.

Only after those are grounded should theme, originality, flagship spectacle, signature mechanism and signature sound be layered on.
