## Integrated machine loop

The four specialist passes converge on this repeated real-machine loop:

`LOW-COST PHYSICAL HOME → WAIT / OPTIONAL INFERENCE → DIRECT TRUTH SIGNAL → BIG OR REG LOCAL REWARD → SHORT POST-BONUS RESOLUTION HORIZON → CHAIN OR CLOSURE → RETURN TO HOME`

The machine does not require a visible CZ→AT→upper-state ladder to create a large experiential state change. Its strongest transition is temporal: the player moves from a potentially long unresolved ordinary horizon to a sharply compressed post-BONUS horizon while the physical reel/STOP HOME remains stable.

## Integrated findings

### 1. Physical HOME and expectation state are separable

Game/Reel and Visual agree that the player can keep essentially the same physical action/attention coordinates while the expected time-to-answer changes radically. This is a different state architecture from changing the player's entire interaction grammar.

**REUSABLE PRINCIPLE — STATE VALUE CAN CHANGE THROUGH RESOLUTION HORIZON WITHOUT RELOCATING THE PLAYER'S PHYSICAL HOME.**

### 2. Direct notification is valuable because it closes debt

Visual and Sound converge on a stable primary truth surface plus a trusted baseline. A direct signal has strength not merely because it is salient, but because it sharply reduces interpretive uncertainty when it arrives. Silence, timing deviation, lamp cadence, selective blackout, voice/music changes and STOP-linked persistence can deepen optional prediction around that core without replacing the core truth contract.

**REUSABLE PRINCIPLE — A STRONG TRUTH SIGNAL SHOULD PAY DOWN INTERPRETIVE DEBT AT THE MOMENT IT CLAIMS ATTENTION.**

### 3. Restraint creates signal headroom

The machine can preserve identity during REST while leaving unused contrast in light and sound. Specialist evidence supports subtraction as well as addition: expected sound can disappear; competing light can be reduced. Salience therefore does not require continuous escalation.

**REUSABLE PRINCIPLE — RESERVE CONTRAST DURING REST SO IMPORTANT DEVIATIONS CAN BE LEGIBLE WITHOUT CONSTANT MAXIMUM OUTPUT.**

### 4. The same three STOPs can deepen without increasing mandatory precision

The normal motor contract is comparatively low-cost, with precision raised conditionally. During BONUS, the same STOP sequence can also become an information clock through cue persistence. Expert play can therefore reveal more from the same actions without making expert decoding mandatory for beginner-safe operation.

**REUSABLE PRINCIPLE — MASTERY MAY INCREASE INFORMATION YIELD FROM A STABLE INPUT CONTRACT INSTEAD OF INCREASING BASIC INPUT BURDEN.**

### 5. Long-session risk moves from motor fatigue to vigilance

Playtest/QA and Sound agree that sparse presentation is not automatically low-cost. A player who continuously audits micro-deviations, waits for a direct signal, tracks mode implications, or becomes committed by elapsed games can accumulate vigilance and relief debt even though the STOP routine itself is easy.

**REUSABLE PRINCIPLE — LOW MOTOR COST CAN COEXIST WITH HIGH VIGILANCE COST; TEST THEM SEPARATELY.**

### 6. A hit after a dry block cannot retroactively validate the dry block

The eventual notification may provide anticipation payoff, local BONUS reward, relief, and future 32G expectation at once. QA must keep those components separate. A strong light after a long wait does not prove that the preceding wait had intrinsic quality.

**REUSABLE PRINCIPLE — DO NOT CREDIT OUTCOME RELIEF TO THE QUALITY OF THE BASELINE LOOP.**

### 7. Positive chains also need release

Short repeated BONUS cycles can produce their own fatigue if every event remains foregrounded. The integrated healthy chain rhythm is closer to:

`PEAK → LOCAL REWARD ROUTINE → RELEASE → SHORT-HORIZON TENSION → NEXT PEAK OR CLOSURE`

not uninterrupted peak stacking.

**REUSABLE PRINCIPLE — REWARD DENSITY DOES NOT REMOVE THE NEED FOR ATTENTION RELEASE.**

## Shared hypothesis verdict

Supported with qualifications.

A simple direct-notification machine can plausibly create strong repeated value without a visible AT upper-state ladder by combining:
- a low-cost stable physical HOME;
- a highly trusted direct truth signal;
- optional expert inference around a beginner-safe contract;
- local BONUS completion;
- strong compression of the next-resolution horizon after BONUS;
- fixed signal surfaces with temporal vocabulary rather than constant physical transformation.

The principal failure boundary is not insufficient spectacle. It is the possibility that long ordinary exposure becomes vigilance, relief-seeking, mode-accounting or commitment pressure rather than intrinsically sustainable play.
