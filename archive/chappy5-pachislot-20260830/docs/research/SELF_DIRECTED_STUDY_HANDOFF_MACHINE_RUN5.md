The key longitudinal distinction is:

`CAUSAL TRUST != LEARNED TOLERANCE`

- causal trust means stable action/effect law supports prediction and diagnostic surprise;
- learned tolerance means criterion, pace or compensatory behavior widened around degraded feedback without better discrimination.
