This run answers Game & Reel Run 5's handoff question:

> What visual/mechanical evidence lets a player preserve learned reel invariants when reel motion, partial stops, symbol landmarks and finger occlusion compete on a small iPhone display—so simplification lowers monitoring cost without erasing the causal information that mastery depends on?

---

# 1. Topic reason

Game & Reel Run 5 reframed mastery as `candidate-set compression`: the same observable evidence should leave fewer plausible role / route / causal hypotheses as the player learns the machine.

Visual & Mechanism therefore should not ask only whether reels are readable or attractive. The stronger question is whether the rendered machine preserves the **evidence needed to eliminate possibilities** across STOP1 → STOP2 → STOP3.

If visual simplification removes the evidence that distinguishes routes, slip families, role denial, or partial-stop history, the interaction can remain clean and responsive while destroying the very mechanism that makes reel mastery possible.

---

# 2. Prior unknown

Previous Visual runs established:

- reel / information / control ownership should survive touch and interruption;
- a three-stop game should accumulate physical evidence rather than restart its scene after each STOP;
- cabinet geometry acts as a causal reference frame;
- platform discontinuity must not impersonate machine law.

Still unresolved:

1. Which visual information must remain continuously legible for candidate-set elimination?
2. What can safely be simplified without turning physical reels into abstract UI labels?
3. How should partial stops survive finger occlusion and small-screen clutter?
4. Does reducing decoration always improve reel reading, or can over-simplification destroy symbol grouping and reel identity?
5. How should a skilled player's lower monitoring cost differ from a novice simply missing evidence?

# 3. Investigation

## 3.1 Real-machine anchor — 新ハナビ cabinet and reel work surface

### SOURCED FACT
P-WORLD's 2021 industry coverage reports that 新ハナビ preserved the 5号機ハナビ game flow while adding level-based technical intervention, new effects, changed blackout rules, and new small-role aiming points created by reel-array changes. It also links Universal's official PV, explanation videos and test-play material.

Source:
https://news.p-world.co.jp/articles/17137/nippon

### DIRECT OBSERVATION
Front views of the real cabinet show a visually dense but spatially stable composition:

- a large fixed identity area above the reels;
- a bright, bounded three-reel aperture in the central working zone;
- a narrow information/display band below the reels;
- an operation deck beneath that band;
- three STOP buttons arranged in a fixed horizontal row;
- a large lower identity panel that carries hall-distance presence without occupying the reel-reading rectangle.

Representative photographed machine front:
https://auctions.yahoo.co.jp/jp/auction/g1085111359

### INTERPRETATION
The cabinet is not visually empty. Its restraint is **topological**: strong identity mass and decoration exist, but the precision work surface remains geometrically bounded.

Reusable distinction:

`GLOBAL VISUAL DENSITY != LOCAL DECISION-SURFACE DENSITY`

A cabinet may be recognizable and decorative at hall distance while preserving a comparatively protected near-field precision zone around the reels.

### UNRESOLVED
Exact physical reel-window dimensions, actual reel-cylinder depth, glass spacing, glare, luminance ratios, material chemistry, button travel and lamp electronics remain unverified without physical measurement.

## 3.2 Visual crowding changes identification before detection

### SOURCED FACT
Visual-crowding research distinguishes detection from identification: an object can remain visibly present while nearby clutter makes its identity difficult to recognize. Reviews describe crowding as a major limit on object recognition, especially outside direct fixation, and show that nearby contours/background structure can impair discrimination even when the target itself is detectable.

Sources:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3070834/
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3413380/

### INTERPRETATION
This maps directly onto reel reading on a small display.

The failure mode is not necessarily “I cannot see the reel.” A player may clearly see that three reels and symbols are present but fail to discriminate the specific symbol landmark or relationship needed to eliminate a role candidate.

Therefore Visual QA should separate:

1. `REEL DETECTION` — can the player see the reel and stopped symbol group?
2. `LANDMARK IDENTIFICATION` — can the intended symbol landmark be discriminated quickly?
3. `RELATIONAL READING` — can the player compare positions across reels?
4. `STATE HISTORY` — can STOP1 evidence remain readable while attention moves to STOP2/3?

New principle:

`A REEL CAN BE VISIBLE YET FUNCTIONALLY ILLEGIBLE.`

---

## 3.3 Candidate-set compression needs persistent relational evidence

### INTERPRETATION grounded in completed 新ハナビ research
The closed Case Study 02 material established that reach-eye / role-denial meaning can depend on stop order, aimed region, slip, intermediate stop form and contradictions across reels. That means a final static three-reel screenshot is not always the entire informational object.

For learned mastery, the visual system must preserve at least the evidence class required by the rule being learned:

`WHERE I AIMED / WHICH ROUTE → WHAT STOPPED FIRST → WHAT REMAINED POSSIBLE → WHAT STOPPED NEXT → WHAT WAS DENIED / CONFIRMED`

Not every game needs every field displayed as UI. The key is that the **physical-looking reel state itself remains a reliable memory surface**.

New principle:

`VISUAL COMPRESSION MAY REMOVE REDUNDANCY; IT MUST NOT REMOVE THE EVIDENCE THAT PERFORMS HYPOTHESIS ELIMINATION.`

This is different from “make important things bigger.” A larger symbol that loses its positional relationship to neighboring symbols can become easier to identify but worse for reel grammar.

---

## 3.4 Partial stops should function as external memory

### INTERPRETATION
On a three-stop machine, STOP1 and STOP2 reduce what the player must remember internally if the resulting stopped reels remain stable and readable.

The stopped reel is therefore more than an animation result. It is **externalized working memory**.

After STOP1:

`settled reel A + spinning B/C = preserved evidence + unresolved space`

After STOP2:

`settled A/B + spinning C = stronger constraint set`

If stopped reels dim excessively, reanimate, become covered by state overlays, shift scale, or lose contrast when the next STOP occurs, the player must reconstruct prior evidence from memory. That increases vigilance cost and undermines Game & Reel's mastery compression.

New principles:

- `A STOPPED REEL IS AN EXTERNAL MEMORY SURFACE.`
- `DO NOT MAKE THE PLAYER RE-MEMORIZE EVIDENCE THE MACHINE CAN KEEP VISIBLE.`
- `PARTIAL-STOP PERSISTENCE IS A MASTERY AID, NOT ONLY A CONTINUITY EFFECT.`

---

## 3.5 Finger occlusion: hide the control, not the evidence hierarchy

### INTERPRETATION
A finger naturally occludes part of the hand plane during touch. The higher-risk failure is when touch feedback expands or relocates so far that it covers the reel/information plane or shifts the machine map.

The real cabinet anchor suggests a useful separation:

`HAND PLANE BELOW` / `REEL WORK SURFACE ABOVE`

This means ordinary hand occlusion can be tolerated because the precision evidence remains spatially above the primary touch zone.

For a small iPhone representation, preserving this relationship is more important than preserving exact real-cabinet pixels.

New principle:

`LET THE FINGER OCCLUDE THE ACTION SURFACE BEFORE IT OCCLUDES THE EVIDENCE SURFACE.`

This is a relational principle only. It does not prescribe exact button position, scale, or proprietary cabinet geometry.

---

## 3.6 Simplification should protect grouping, not flatten physical identity

### SOURCED FACT
Crowding literature also shows that grouping relationships affect recognition in clutter; target/flanker organization can alter whether nearby elements interfere with identification.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2857404/

### INTERPRETATION
Removing all boundaries, shading and reel-window structure is not automatically beneficial. Some physical segmentation helps the eye parse:

- reel A vs reel B vs reel C;
- stopped vs spinning;
- reel content vs cabinet decoration;
- information band vs reel work surface;
- action surface vs evidence surface.

Therefore the goal is not minimum visual structure. It is **task-supporting segmentation with low irrelevant competition**.

New principle:

`SIMPLIFY COMPETING DETAIL; PRESERVE STRUCTURE THAT EXPLAINS WHAT BELONGS TO WHAT.`

This also protects the previous physical-layer requirement: reels should not become three floating UI columns detached from a cabinet body.

---

## 3.7 Slip legibility is relational, not a demand for frame-by-frame exposure

### INTERPRETATION
Game & Reel's model allows slip / stop response to carry information where documented. Visual & Mechanism should not infer or expose proprietary internal control. The visual responsibility is narrower: if the game claims that observed reel response matters, the motion/result transition must be consistent enough that the player can relate aim, motion and settlement.

This does not require showing every intermediate symbol clearly during rotation.

What must survive is:

`KNOWN AIM REGION + TRUSTED MOTION CONTINUITY + LEGIBLE FINAL LANDING = BOUNDED INFERENCE`

New principle:

`SLIP LEGIBILITY REQUIRES A TRUSTED BEFORE/AFTER RELATION, NOT A SLOW-MOTION EXPLANATION.`

If a small display cannot support trustworthy micro-motion discrimination, the correct research response is to mark that limit unresolved—not to invent oversized digital slip indicators that replace physical reel reading.

# 4. New learning

## 4.1 Visual readability should be measured as evidence preservation

A generic readability score is too coarse.

Research-only decomposition:

- `TARGET LEGIBILITY` — can a symbol/landmark be identified?
- `REEL SEGMENTATION` — is reel ownership clear?
- `RELATIONAL LEGIBILITY` — are symbol positions across reels comparable?
- `PARTIAL-STOP PERSISTENCE` — does prior evidence stay available?
- `ACTION/EVIDENCE SEPARATION` — does touch avoid consuming the precision surface?
- `MOTION-TO-SETTLE TRUST` — does the final landing belong to the visible reel motion?
- `CLUTTER RESISTANCE` — do lamps/panels/overlays leave the precision surface interpretable?

This yields the central Run 5 model:

`STABLE SPATIAL SEGMENTATION + LEGIBLE LANDMARKS + PERSISTENT PARTIAL STOPS + LOW COMPETING CLUTTER -> VISUAL EVIDENCE PRESERVATION -> CANDIDATE-SET COMPRESSION`

---

## 4.2 Expert efficiency should look like fewer necessary glances, not less available evidence

A skilled player may not inspect every reel region every game. That does not mean the machine should remove those regions or automatically summarize them.

The machine should keep the evidence available while learned routines let the Human sample it selectively.

New principle:

`MASTERY MAY REDUCE SAMPLING; THE MACHINE SHOULD NOT REDUCE EVIDENCE JUST BECAUSE AN EXPERT SAMPLES LESS.`

This protects beginner access too: the same stable physical map supports slower learning without requiring a separate visual grammar.

---

## 4.3 Quiet precision zones create more value than globally quiet cabinets

The 新ハナビ anchor reinforces that a recognizable cabinet can carry strong static identity outside the reel zone. What matters for reel-centered play is that attention claims do not continuously invade the precision surface.

Refined principle:

`HALL IDENTITY CAN BE LOUD IN MASSING; REEL DECISION SPACE SHOULD BE QUIET IN COMPETING INFORMATION.`

This is not permission to copy 新ハナビ's artwork, panel shape, color scheme, lamp topology or lettering.

---

# 5. Changed understanding

Before this run, “small-screen reel readability” could be framed too narrowly as symbol size, brightness and contrast.

The stronger model is:

`READABILITY = CAN THE PLAYER RETAIN AND USE THE RELATIONAL EVIDENCE NEEDED TO UPDATE THEIR HYPOTHESIS SET?`

A giant isolated symbol can be readable but information-poor. A physically structured reel field can be smaller yet preserve more useful relationships if clutter, ownership and partial-stop continuity are controlled.

Visual simplification is therefore not a cosmetic reduction process. It is an **evidence-budget decision**.

# 7. Limits / misuse warnings

Do not misuse this research to conclude:

- all decoration should be removed;
- real cabinets are visually minimal;
- larger symbols are always better;
- exact 新ハナビ dimensions/materials are known;
- every slip must be visually exposed frame-by-frame;
- a UI overlay is an acceptable substitute for documented physical-reel evidence;
- experts need less underlying information because they look less often;
- crowding research provides a direct pixel-spacing threshold for iPhone pachislot.

No perceptual laboratory result here establishes Chappy5-specific size, spacing, contrast or timing thresholds. Those remain future Human-test questions after production is authorized.

# 8. Remaining unknowns

1. Minimum symbol landmark size for reliable iPhone identification at actual play distance.
2. How much reel-border / cabinet structure helps segmentation before it becomes clutter.
3. Whether dynamic lamps adjacent to the reel aperture measurably impair role/reach-eye recognition.
4. How much finger coverage occurs in the actual intended iPhone grip during three repeated STOPs.
5. Whether stopped-reel brightness should remain identical to spinning-reel brightness for best relational reading.
6. What rendering frame drops do to perceived slip magnitude on real target hardware.
7. Whether novice and expert users rely on different visual landmarks for the same stop route.
8. How long partial-stop evidence must remain visually dominant before attention can safely move elsewhere.
9. Exact real-machine glare/depth/material cues for 新ハナビ remain unresolved without physical inspection.

Highest-value next question:

**If Visual mastery depends on keeping partial-stop evidence available while skilled players reduce how often they inspect it, what auditory structure allows the player to keep eyes on the reel evidence surface—using routine sound as low-cost action/state confirmation—without audio becoming a substitute for reel reading or creating a second mandatory monitoring channel?**

Suggested boundaries:

- real-machine study only;
- closed 新ハナビ material may be used as a reality anchor, not reopened or redesigned;
- distinguish sounds that permit visual attention to remain on reels from sounds that demand gaze switching;
- separate routine motor confirmation, information-bearing deviation, technical-intervention feedback and state continuity;
- examine whether audio can reduce unnecessary visual checking without creating defensive listening;
- no original concepts, audio assets, prototype or code.

# 10. Possible future Chappy5 value

If eventually authorized, this research could help prevent a common browser-pachislot failure: shrinking a cabinet onto a phone until the reels remain technically visible but no longer carry enough relational evidence for real reel play.

The transferable target is not 新ハナビ's protected design. It is the general condition in which:

`THE MACHINE CAN BE VISUALLY PRESENT WHILE THE REELS REMAIN AN INFORMATION-BEARING PHYSICAL WORK SURFACE.`

That could preserve beginner legibility, expert evidence compression, physical-machine identity and long-session attention efficiency at the same time.
