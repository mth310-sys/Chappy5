## Central answer

Sound Run 8 established:

`AUDITORY DUTY CLOSED != SEMANTIC SCENE CLOSED.`

QA answer:

`TEST WHAT THE PLAYER STOPPED DOING AND WHAT THEY KEPT KNOWING.`

Healthy selective closure cannot be scored from calm behavior, routine pace or one verbal report. It requires separate evidence that:
1. the obsolete action/listening duty actually retired;
2. the still-live inference remained usable;
3. attention ownership transferred to the correct remaining evidence surface;
4. ordinary monitoring burden recovered where appropriate;
5. a later legitimate duty can still reopen attention;
6. the old duty does not resurrect from a similar cue.

Research abstraction:

`SELECTIVE CLOSURE = RETIRE OBSOLETE ACTION + PRESERVE LIVE INFERENCE + TRANSFER OWNERSHIP + RECOVER BASELINE COST + PRESERVE FUTURE REOPENING`

This is not an implementation formula.
