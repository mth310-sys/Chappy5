## 1. Why this topic now

Machine Director Run 5 formally separated `CAUSAL TRUST` from `LEARNED TOLERANCE` and handed Game & Reel the next boundary question:

**How can repeated reel/action grammar expose stable invariants strongly enough that mastery narrows causal uncertainty rather than merely teaching the player to accept ambiguous outcomes—especially when complete proprietary stop-control tables are unavailable?**

Game & Reel Run 4 already separated execution error / route effect / machine-law effect / platform artifact / interpretation error. The remaining problem is longitudinal: after 100G, 500G or several hours, what has the player actually learned?

No Case Study is reopened. 新ハナビ remains a completed real-machine anchor only.

## 2. Prior unknown

Earlier work established:

- the player does not need the complete hidden stop-control table;
- partial stops and remembered aim can act as causal breadcrumbs;
- valid reach-eye interpretation may depend on BET / stop order / procedure conditions;
- technical intervention creates real execution responsibility;
- repeated experience can produce both genuine skill and mere adaptation to degraded feedback.

What was still missing was a testable distinction between:

`MASTERY = uncertainty becomes structurally smaller`

and

`TOLERANCE = uncertainty stays broad, but the player stops objecting to it`.

## 3. Investigation

### SOURCED FACT — 新ハナビ exposes procedure-conditioned observable laws

P-WORLD documents a normal-play procedure where the player first aims a cherry-covering landmark on the left reel, right reel can be stopped freely, and the middle reel only needs an ice aim when the partial result leaves ice relevant. It also notes that shown reach-eye examples are valid under stated conditions such as 3-credit play and specified stop-order families. It further documents red-7-route laws in which the amount of slip relative to the aimed red 7 changes the meaning of a later small-role miss.

Source:
- https://www.p-world.co.jp/machine/database/9401

### SOURCED FACT — multiple aiming routes expose different candidate sets

Nana-Press documents the red-7 route as several first-stop classes with different surviving candidates, including wind-bell/replay, wind-bell/ice/bonus, replay/miss, and cherry-substitute outcomes. The completed Case Study also preserves BAR/暖簾 and single-Don routes as alternative observation lenses.

Sources:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3867/
- `docs/research/CASE_STUDY_02_SHIN_HANABI_GAME_REEL_RUN1.md`

### INTERPRETATION — an invariant is not an identical result

The stable law available to the player is rarely:

`same press -> same final three-reel picture`.

That would be incompatible with different internal roles and bonus states.

A more realistic observable invariant is relational:

`same action class + same visible preconditions -> same family of legal consequences`.

Examples of action classes include:

- left-first cherry-covering route;
- a particular red-7 first-stop class;
- right-reel free stop while ice remains impossible;
- middle-reel ice duty only after an ice-preserving partial stop;
- a technical-intervention attempt under a documented bonus procedure.

Mastery can therefore improve without the player knowing the hidden flag. The player learns which **families of consequences remain possible** after each action.

### SOURCED FACT — internal role names are not the normal player-facing grammar

Published analysis separates internal small-role categories such as wind-bell variants, cherry variants and ice variants for probability analysis, while normal-play instructions teach visible stop forms and conditional follow-up actions instead of exposing the internal category every game.

### INTERPRETATION — mastery compresses at the level of candidate sets

A beginner may represent a first stop as:

`many symbols / maybe something happened / check everything`.

A practiced player can represent the same stop as:

`ice dead -> middle free`

or

`ice alive -> middle aim required`

or

`this miss would contradict the ordinary role family`.

The number of physical reels did not change. The hidden control table did not become known. What changed is the **size and organization of the live hypothesis set**.

This gives a stronger definition:

`CAUSAL MASTERY = fewer plausible causal hypotheses after the same observable evidence, while preserving accuracy.`

### SOURCED FACT — bonus discovery can precede explicit notification

P-WORLD describes approximately 3,500 reach-eye patterns and normal play in which a reach eye can establish bonus before a later explicit notification. It also documents role/presentation relations such as delayed start corresponding to cherry or bonus and flash/role contradictions that can imply bonus.

Source:
- https://www.p-world.co.jp/machine/database/9401

### INTERPRETATION — contradiction is a high-value invariant

A mature reel grammar is not learned only as `if A then B`.

It also contains negative knowledge:

`if ordinary role X were the explanation, this visible relation should not be absent / contradicted`.

This is important because role denial reduces causal uncertainty without revealing hidden state directly.

The player can move from:

`something unusual happened`

to

`ordinary wind-bell / ice / replay explanation is no longer sufficient`

before knowing the exact bonus flag.

### SOURCED FACT — BIG/REG technical procedures preserve explicit action responsibility

P-WORLD documents BIG technical intervention and REG procedures whose difficulty depends on the chosen method. Published full-strategy return is materially higher than nominal return. After BIG, the player also operates JAC-IN avoidance / acceptance across 花火チャレンジ and 花火GAME rather than entering a passive reward film.

Sources:
- https://www.p-world.co.jp/machine/database/9401
- https://www.universal-777.com/product/slot/shin_hanabi/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3868/

### INTERPRETATION — stable invariants must survive state changes

A player who mastered only the visual picture of normal play has not mastered the whole machine. State changes alter responsibility:

`NORMAL -> small-role coverage / reach-eye inference`

`BIG -> one documented payout-optimization action, then simplified consumption`

`HANABI CHALLENGE -> preserve RT until transition should be accepted`

`HANABI GAME -> continue reading while RT rules differ`

`REG -> selected technical method changes precision responsibility`

The reusable law is therefore not `always press the same thing`. It is:

`recognize state -> recognize current responsibility -> execute known action class -> interpret consequence within that state's grammar`.

### SOURCED FACT — repetition alone does not guarantee optimal learning

Motor-learning literature supports separating performance improvement from what was actually learned. Practice can improve execution, but sequence knowledge, chunking and motor execution are partially separable; repeated practice can also preserve suboptimal planning. Practice consistency can strengthen use-dependent learning, while decision uncertainty itself can act as a context for motor memory.

Sources:
- Wong et al. / sequence-learning discussion: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12352532/
- Verstynen et al., chunking and motor learning: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4138363/
- The consistency of prior movements shapes use-dependent learning: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8431821/
- Decision uncertainty as a context for motor memory: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11420082/
- Sub-optimality retained through extended practice: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5116677/

### INTERPRETATION

These are not pachislot studies and do not establish reel timing thresholds. They support one narrow warning:

`repetition + smoother behavior != proof that the player's causal model became more accurate`.

A player may simply repeat a safe routine, slow down, or stop checking anomalies.

## 4. New learning

### INTERPRETATION — distinguish five kinds of compression

A mature repeated reel grammar can compress uncertainty in at least five different ways:

1. `ACTION COMPRESSION`
   - several raw visual states map to one safe action class.
   - Example form: `no ice duty -> free stop`.

2. `ROLE COMPRESSION`
   - visible partial stops eliminate whole role families.

3. `ROUTE COMPRESSION`
   - the player knows which consequences are only meaningful under the route actually used.

4. `COUNTERFACTUAL COMPRESSION`
   - the player can bound what a different aim / stop order could plausibly have changed without claiming exact hidden control.

5. `SOURCE COMPRESSION`
   - after a surprising result, fewer causal sources remain plausible: execution / route / machine law / platform / misread.

`TRUE MASTERY` should improve several of these together. Faster pressing alone proves none of them.

### INTERPRETATION — invariants should be relational and falsifiable

Useful learned laws need an observable way to be wrong.

Weak rule:
`weird stops happen sometimes`.

Strong rule:
`under this route and partial-stop condition, role family X remains possible; after this contradiction, X is no longer a sufficient explanation`.

The second rule reduces uncertainty because future observations can support or reject it.

### INTERPRETATION — “I don't know” can be expert-calibrated

Mastery is not perfect identification of hidden state.

A strong expert answer can be:

`I cannot identify the proprietary hidden control cause from public evidence, but I can rule out input failure, wrong stop order and the ordinary small-role explanation; this belongs to a legitimate unresolved machine-law class.`

This is better calibrated than inventing an exact flag or control table.

### INTERPRETATION — predictive compression should reduce defensive checking

If learning is real, routine states become cheaper to monitor because the player knows what can safely be ignored and when conditional duty opens.

If the player still needs to verify every reel, every button acknowledgement, every flash and every payout after hundreds of games, the system may be producing vigilance rather than mastery.

Thus:

`CAUSAL UNCERTAINTY ↓ + REQUIRED MONITORING ↓ + ACCURACY MAINTAINED/↑`

is stronger evidence of mastery than:

`PLAY SPEED ↑`.

### INTERPRETATION — repetition should sharpen surprise, not normalize arbitrariness

When ordinary action classes are stable, a rare contradiction becomes more informative with experience.

When ordinary relations are unstable, the same rare contradiction becomes less informative because too many causal explanations remain alive.

Therefore:

`MASTERED BASELINE -> RARE DEVIATION GAINS INFORMATION VALUE`

whereas

`UNSTABLE BASELINE -> RARE DEVIATION BECOMES ONE MORE AMBIGUITY`.

## 5. Changed understanding

Previous model from Run 4:

`trustworthy STOP = meaningful consequence + causal source usually diagnosable`.

Updated model:

`mastered reel grammar = repeated observable invariants progressively reduce the number of plausible action / role / route / source hypotheses, while unknown proprietary machine law remains explicitly bounded rather than guessed`.

This makes **uncertainty compression** the key longitudinal Game & Reel acceptance dimension.

## 6. Reusable principles

- `MASTERY SHOULD SHRINK THE LIVE HYPOTHESIS SET, NOT MERELY INCREASE PLAY SPEED.`
- `LEARN THE FAMILY OF LEGAL CONSEQUENCES; DO NOT REQUIRE IDENTICAL RESULTS.`
- `OBSERVABLE INVARIANTS SHOULD BE RELATIONAL, STATE-AWARE AND FALSIFIABLE.`
- `A PARTIAL STOP IS VALUABLE WHEN IT ELIMINATES POSSIBILITIES OR CHANGES RESPONSIBILITY.`
- `NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE — ROLE DENIAL / CONTRADICTION — IS PART OF REEL MASTERY.`
- `ROUTE-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE MUST NOT BE GENERALIZED OUTSIDE THE ROUTE THAT EARNED IT.`
- `TRUE SKILL CAN INCLUDE A CALIBRATED 'UNKNOWN' ABOUT HIDDEN CONTROL.`
- `DO NOT FILL PROPRIETARY CONTROL GAPS WITH CONFIDENT COUNTERFACTUALS.`
- `A SAFE DEFAULT ROUTE CAN TEACH STABLE ACTION CLASSES BEFORE OPTIONAL ROUTES REOPEN UNCERTAINTY.`
- `SKILL REWARD IS LEGIBLE ONLY WHEN STATE, RESPONSIBILITY, ACTION AND CONSEQUENCE REMAIN LINKED.`
- `REPETITION SHOULD SHARPEN SURPRISE AUTHORITY; IT SHOULD NOT NORMALIZE ARBITRARINESS.`
- `LOWER MONITORING WITH PRESERVED DETECTION ACCURACY IS STRONGER MASTERY EVIDENCE THAN FASTER PLAY ALONE.`
- `CAUSAL TRUST GROWS WHEN ORDINARY INVARIANTS KEEP THEIR PROMISES ACROSS MANY GAMES.`

## 7. Limits / misuse

- No undisclosed 新ハナビ stop-control table is reconstructed or inferred as fact.
- Public examples for one route / press position / state must not be generalized to every flag or stop order.
- The motor-learning studies are conceptual support only; they do not define pachislot timing windows or payout design.
- Faster or more automatic pressing may reflect habit, not better causal discrimination.
- A player's verbal confidence is not proof that their internal model is correct.
- A rare correct prediction can be luck; mastery must be evaluated across repeated action classes and normal events.
- This document does not authorize implementation, simulated stop tables, assets, browser prototypes or original-machine design.

## 8. Remaining unknowns

- What minimum set of observable action classes is sufficient for a novice to form a trustworthy first causal model?
- How many repetitions are needed before a routine action class becomes safely compressible without reducing small-role coverage accuracy?
- Which is learned first in real play: aim landmarks, role candidate sets, stop-order conditions, or contradiction families?
- How should a Human trial distinguish `I knew what could happen` from retrospective explanation after seeing the result?
- Can counterfactual belief be tested without disclosing the hidden table by asking only bounded questions such as `could changing stop order have changed this pattern's interpretability?` rather than `what exact symbol would have stopped?`
- How robustly does mastered grammar recover after hours/days away when state-specific responsibilities are involved?
- How should platform anomalies temporarily invalidate a learned invariant without permanently damaging trust in the machine law?

## 9. Next worthwhile Game & Reel study

**Counterfactual calibration under incomplete machine knowledge:** what kinds of counterfactual question can a skilled player answer legitimately (`this route would change the information available`) versus illegitimately (`this exact hidden flag would have produced this exact stop`) when the proprietary control table is unavailable?

This should preserve the boundary between useful skill knowledge and reverse-engineered fiction.

## 10. Possible future Chappy5 value

If Human Gate later authorizes production, a research-only Human trial could compare early and practiced play on the same action classes and measure:

- number of causal hypotheses the player reports after STOP1 / STOP2 / STOP3;
- prediction accuracy for whether conditional aiming duty remains;
- correct rejection of route-inapplicable reach-eye rules;
- ability to identify role denial without overclaiming hidden state;
- execution/route/platform attribution precision;
- monitoring burden and unnecessary re-checks;
- confidence calibration, including legitimate `unknown` responses.

A successful learning curve would not require the Human to recite a stop table. It would show:

`FEWER PLAUSIBLE HYPOTHESES + BETTER BOUNDED PREDICTION + LESS DEFENSIVE MONITORING + NO FALSE CERTAINTY ABOUT HIDDEN CONTROL`.
