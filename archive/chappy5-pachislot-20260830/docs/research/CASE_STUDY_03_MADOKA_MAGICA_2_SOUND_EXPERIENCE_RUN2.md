## 1. Run 2 question: what repeats, what temporarily owns attention, and what gives it back?

Run 1 established two reward clocks: BIG as a short foreground reward and ART as a persistent future-game resource. Run 2 focuses on the acoustic hand-offs around that structure.

The useful model is not simply:

`NORMAL < CZ < BIG < ART < SPECIAL`

but:

`ROUTINE ACTION AUDIO -> LOCAL DEVIATION -> TEMPORARY ATTENTION OWNER -> RESOLUTION -> AUDIBLE RETURN ADDRESS`

The same cabinet must survive hundreds or thousands of repeated BET/lever/STOP cycles, so the most important audio question is not peak spectacle. It is whether routine sound remains trustworthy enough that deviations retain meaning.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`THE VALUE OF A RARE CUE DEPENDS ON THE STABILITY OF THE ROUTINE IT INTERRUPTS.`

## 3. BIG notification modes explicitly move certainty to different action boundaries

### SOURCED FACT

BIG is a real bonus with approximately 150-coin acquisition. Major references document three notification types and selectable BGM.

Sources:
- HAZUSE bonus summary: https://hazuse.com/machine/pachislot/SX0136/
- NanaPress bonus overview: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/50/918/

NanaPress additionally documents notification customization:
- chance notification can couple a cut-in with the ART notification lamp;
- complete notification can make the lower panel and LCD-side frame go dark at **lever ON**;
- post notification can signal at **lever ON, reel start, or after payout finishes** through cabinet UI/indicator behavior.

Source:
https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/50/2452/

### INTERPRETATION

This is strong evidence that notification design is partly a **timeline design**. The player is not only choosing what the bonus sounds/looks like; the machine can place certainty against different motor boundaries.

For Sound study, the important abstraction is:

`INPUT BOUNDARY -> EXPECTATION GAP -> NOTICE -> NEXT DUTY`

Complete notification pulls certainty toward the first action boundary. Post notification can defer certainty until after reel motion or even payout settlement. That changes the felt length of uncertainty without changing the underlying bonus reward.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`CERTAINTY TIMING IS AN EXPERIENCE PARAMETER.`

and

`A CUE'S MEANING INCLUDES WHICH MOTOR BOUNDARY IT INTERRUPTS.`

## 4. Memorable impact and startle cost must remain separate scores

### FIELD REPORT retained from Run 1

A player field report described the complete-notification event as sufficiently strong to cause a physical startle and attract attention from an adjacent seat, while the same player still valued the reward/visual moment.

Source retained from Run 1:
https://noriko-slot.com/zissen-madomagi22567

### INTERPRETATION

The event can be memorable and undesirable in sensory cost at the same time. That matters especially because the player can repeatedly choose this notification style across multiple BIGs.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`MEMORABILITY, INFORMATION VALUE, REWARD VALUE AND STARTLE COST ARE FOUR DIFFERENT QA SCORES.`

A peak does not pass merely because it is remembered.

## 5. Bonus end uses sound as a deliberate information query, not only a celebration tail

### SOURCED FACT

At bonus end, touching the LCD or using PUSH can trigger a voice response. Published analysis documents multiple responses with setting implications, including base, odd/even tendency and higher-setting tendency categories. A special end image can also carry setting information.

Sources:
- NanaPress: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/50/922/
- HAZUSE: https://hazuse.com/machine/pachislot/SX0136/

### INTERPRETATION

This is a materially different audio duty from reward music. The player intentionally asks the machine for information **after** the short reward has ended. Audio therefore extends the bonus into a post-reward analytical boundary without needing to make the bonus itself longer.

The sequence is:

`BIG REWARD -> END -> PLAYER QUERY -> VOICE INFORMATION -> NEXT-SESSION DECISION`

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`POST-REWARD AUDIO CAN SERVE DECISION QUALITY RATHER THAN EXTEND CELEBRATION.`

For long-session design research, optional/queried information is less fatiguing than forcing every informational layer into the reward climax.

## 6. ART is a persistent audio home; action cues must temporarily outrank atmosphere

### SOURCED FACT

`マギカ☆ラッシュ` is documented as a 50G+α, approximately 1.5 coins/G ART. Published play instructions keep the normal physical stopping routine but require navigation compliance when navigation appears.

Sources:
- HAZUSE ART: https://hazuse.com/machine/pachislot/SX0136/genre/209/
- Ichigeki machine summary: https://1geki.jp/slot/s_madomagi2/

### INTERPRETATION

ART sound has two simultaneous jobs:
1. establish a persistent state identity over many games;
2. yield foreground priority when a time-critical navigation/action cue appears.

If state BGM permanently owns maximum attention, the machine makes its own motor instructions compete with its atmosphere. If navigation is over-emphasized on every game, the long state becomes tiring.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`PERSISTENT BGM OWNS CONTEXT; INTERMITTENT ACTION CUES OWN THE MOMENT OF REQUIRED INPUT.`

This does not require every action cue to be louder; it requires clear semantic priority.

## 7. BIG during ART is an audible interruption-and-return problem

### SOURCED FACT

Published game analysis documents that bonus value changes by current state. A bonus during ART/related phases can preserve or add future ART-related value rather than erasing the persistent state.

Sources:
- NanaPress bonus detail: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/50/918/
- HAZUSE: https://hazuse.com/machine/pachislot/SX0136/

Game & Reel Run 2 further records ART-end preparation and bonus-in-ART as playable/value-bearing boundaries.

Repository source:
`docs/research/CASE_STUDY_03_MADOKA_MAGICA_2_GAME_REEL_RUN2.md`

### INTERPRETATION

Therefore the correct audio question is not “is BIG bigger than ART?” It is whether the player can hear a clean ownership transfer:

`ART HOME -> BIG FOREGROUND -> BIG RESOLUTION -> ART HOME RESTORED`

The return must restore not only music/state identity, but the expected action duty.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`A PERSISTENT STATE NEEDS AN AUDIBLE RETURN ADDRESS AFTER A TEMPORARY REWARD TAKES THE FOREGROUND.`

## 8. Special ART zones may spend local intensity but must return it

### SOURCED FACT

`マギカ☆クエスト` and `ワルプルギスの夜` are temporary high-consequence structures attached to ART. Public analysis documents different extension/continuation mechanics rather than a permanent replacement of all following ART play.

Sources:
- HAZUSE ART: https://hazuse.com/machine/pachislot/SX0136/genre/209/
- one public strategy summary: https://xn--mckza4ard4ttb2d.com/madokamagica2-bonus

The latter also documents that a particular BGM state during Walpurgis carries continuation information. This research records only the information relationship, not the song itself.

### INTERPRETATION

A music change can therefore be both atmosphere and machine truth. Its remembered value is partly the guarantee/expectation it carries, not merely the musical composition.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`MUSIC BECOMES GAME INFORMATION WHEN ITS ENTRY CHANGES WHAT THE PLAYER KNOWS.`

and

`LOCAL HIGH-CONSEQUENCE STATES SHOULD RETURN ACOUSTIC HEADROOM WHEN THEIR QUESTION ENDS.`

## 9. Negative-result memory: 穢れ is an example of presentation carrying history forward

### SOURCED FACT

The machine's `穢れ` system accumulates under several negative/undesirable conditions, including CZ failure and some prolonged/failed situations. Public sources document visible accumulation indication and later bonus-related consequences.

Sources:
- Pachiseven normal analysis: https://pachiseven.jp/machines/4939/cutout/4
- Ichigeki: https://1geki.jp/slot/s_madomagi2/45/

### UNRESOLVED

This pass does not claim the exact sound map, rate or acoustic signature of every accumulation size/event.

### INTERPRETATION

For experience analysis, the important structure is that failure need not acoustically end as pure deletion. A later memory layer can make some bad outcomes remain part of the session model.

However, if every negative event screamed its retained value, failure would cease to feel like failure and long-session audio would become saturated.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`FAILURE MEMORY CAN BE AUDIBLE WITHOUT TURNING EVERY FAILURE INTO A CELEBRATION.`

## 10. Long-form play: state topology is established; raw acoustic density remains unresolved

### SOURCED FACT / VIDEO SOURCE

Long-form real-play material exists for the machine, including:
- DMM Pachitown's “new machine REAL recording” program, published 2016-10-14, described as a one-day practical play session: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BkBa5TGXHek

- long-session/home-machine streams also exist publicly.

These sources are appropriate for repeated-state topology and future timestamp audit.

### DIRECT OBSERVATION LIMITATION

In this run, indexed access was sufficient to verify the existence/context of long-form sessions but not to perform a clean, uninterrupted, microphone-controlled audio audit of every game. Therefore this document does **not** invent:
- “X cues per 100G”;
- exact routine SPL;
- exact cue duration;
- exact frequency balance;
- exact fatigue threshold.

### INTERPRETATION

A+ART long-session fatigue should be measured by **attention claims per unit time**, not only average loudness. A quiet voice cue, persistent BGM layer and uncertain consequence can collectively demand more cognition than one short loud impact.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`AUDIO FATIGUE LOAD = REPETITION COST + CONCURRENT CLAIMS + UNCERTAINTY DURATION + STARTLE COST + RE-ANCHOR COST.`

This is a QA model, not a physical acoustic formula.
