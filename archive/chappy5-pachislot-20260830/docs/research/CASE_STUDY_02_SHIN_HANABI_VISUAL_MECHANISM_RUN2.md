## Purpose of this run

Run 1 established the broad visual grammar: reel-first workspace, static identity, blackout/flash as state syntax, shallow visual competition around the reels, and no evidence that a large moving mechanism is necessary to make the cabinet physically present.

Run 2 does not repeat those conclusions. It focuses on what front photography hides: **front-shell versus cabinet-body depth, seated hand/reel geometry, visible speaker architecture, state-light ambiguity, and how a cabinet can be visually loud at aisle distance while keeping the repeated reel-reading plane stable.**

No original Chappy5 visual or mechanism is designed here. Protected artwork, logo, exact ornament shape, proprietary flash pattern and signature presentation are not reusable assets.

## 1. Front shell and rear body are visually different jobs

### Direct observation

The oblique photographs make a distinction that the official front image flattens:

- a relatively plain, dark rectangular cabinet body continues rearward;
- the saturated red crown and reflective / translucent side pieces are concentrated on the front shell;
- speaker cavities, upper marquee and reel-facing fascia sit forward of the rear box;
- the operation deck projects materially further toward the seated player;
- the lower decorative panel remains behind the STOP touch plane rather than sharing its front-most plane.

### Interpretation

The machine's *identity volume* is mostly concentrated toward the player's side of the box. The rear cabinet is not required to carry equal sculptural complexity.

This yields a reusable physical-reading principle:

`FRONT THEATRE / BACK BOX SEPARATION`

A cabinet can have strong sculptural presence by concentrating form, reflection and light in the front-facing depth layers while keeping the structural body visually quieter. This is not permission to copy Archer geometry; it is a depth-allocation principle.

### Caution

No exact shell projection, wall thickness or cabinet depth was measured. A seller page gives roughly `H810 × W475 × D400 mm` and `30–60 kg` only as a **generic pachislot-machine shipping guide**, not a manufacturer-confirmed 新ハナビ specification. These numbers must not be promoted to exact machine dimensions.

## 2. The reel window is physically recessed, not merely graphically framed

### Direct observation

Oblique photography shows a clear front cover / bezel plane ahead of the visible reel-cylinder surface. The reel symbols therefore occupy a genuinely deeper plane than the surrounding art and reflective trim.

The three reel strips are bright, continuous vertical cylinders viewed through a rectangular aperture. The player reads them through a front plane rather than seeing a flat image printed on the fascia.

### Interpretation

This physical separation does two jobs simultaneously:

1. produces object presence through parallax/reflection;
2. protects the reel field as a coherent work surface, because decoration terminates at the aperture rather than visually entering the symbol plane.

Reusable principle:

`DEPTH SHOULD CLARIFY INFORMATION, NOT ONLY ADD LUXURY`

A deep reel window is not valuable merely because it looks expensive. Its useful role is to make the moving/stopped reel plane unmistakably separate from bezel, panel, lamp and control planes.

### Unresolved

- visible reel aperture dimensions;
- cylinder diameter;
- strip pitch;
- exact distance from transparent cover to reel face;
- optical treatment / anti-reflection properties of the front cover.

## 3. Seated gaze and seated hand geometry are deliberately split

### Direct observation

The player-facing stack is physically divided:

`REEL READING PLANE`
→ `NARROW INFORMATION / MINI-DISPLAY BAND`
→ `TOP SURFACE OF OPERATION DECK`
→ `FORWARD STOP-BUTTON FASCIA`

The oblique view shows that the STOP buttons are not coplanar with the reels and are much closer to the seated player. The lever projects from the player's left side of the control area. The central controls occupy the deck surface, while the three repeated STOP targets form a fixed horizontal row on the nearer fascia.

### Interpretation

The machine permits the eyes to remain high on the reel result while the hands operate lower, spatially predictable controls. Repeated operation does not require the control target itself to become a large visual event every game.

Reusable principle:

`HAND PLANE CAN BE LOUD TACTILELY WHILE GAZE PLANE STAYS QUIET`

The most frequently touched surface and the most frequently inspected surface need not compete for visual dominance.

This aligns with Game & Reel's `AIM → CLASSIFY → RESOLVE` loop: the hand can execute a stable motor routine while perception remains anchored to symbol movement / stopped relationships.

### Unresolved

Lever throw, return force, switch force, STOP travel, deck angle and seated anthropometric distances remain undocumented.

## 4. Speaker architecture: visible upper stereo-like symmetry, hidden low-frequency responsibility unresolved

### Direct observation

Front and oblique images consistently show large dark grille cavities in the upper left and upper right of the face, flanking the upper bonus/reference panel. They are among the strongest non-reel physical features in the upper half of the cabinet.

### Sourced field evidence

Used-machine sellers describe volume modifications across `all speakers`; one real-unit seller separately states that a volume control was added to a `woofer`.

### Interpretation

The upper grille pair is visually compatible with left/right acoustic emission near head/ear height, while low-frequency reproduction may use another transducer elsewhere in the body. **This is an interpretation, not a confirmed channel map.**

Important Visual & Mechanism consequence:

`SPEAKER OPENINGS ARE PART OF SILHOUETTE EVEN WHEN SOUND IS NOT PLAYING`

Acoustic hardware contributes to cabinet identity and material depth at rest. Speaker placement therefore cannot be treated as an invisible Sound-only afterthought.

### Unresolved

- OEM speaker count;
- which visible grille maps to which channel;
- woofer OEM location and dimensions;
- crossover / amplification topology;
- whether resale units preserve original drivers.

No audio-production inference should be made from seller modifications.

## 5. Tall side-light surfaces create a peripheral event channel without occupying reel center

### Direct observation

Powered real-machine photographs show tall translucent / luminous surfaces running along the left and right sides of the central face. Different photos show substantially different apparent colors/intensities in these same areas.

Because photos were taken in unknown machine states and exposures, **the color difference itself is not treated as a documented state law**.

What can be safely observed is geometric:

- a large portion of luminous area exists in the player's peripheral field;
- that area frames rather than covers the reels;
- the central reel symbols remain readable even when the outer frame is brightly illuminated.

### Interpretation

This supports a two-zone light architecture:

`CENTRAL PRECISION LIGHT` — reel illumination / blackout / flash that must remain rule-readable

`PERIPHERAL PRESENCE LIGHT` — large framing surfaces that can make the machine feel alive or visible from aisle distance without drawing a new opaque layer over the reels.

Reusable principle:

`PERIPHERAL ENERGY, CENTRAL PRECISION`

When the primary game is fine reel discrimination, high-area lighting is safer at the periphery; rule-bearing changes should remain discriminable in the exact work surface.

### Important caution

Run 2 does **not** assign specific colors, pulse patterns or states to the side lamps. Resale photo lighting is insufficient evidence for that mapping.

## 6. Aisle identity works even before event animation

### Direct observation across powered and unpowered/front and oblique images

The machine remains identifiable through large static masses:

- saturated crown / shoulder color block;
- bilateral dark speaker openings;
- reflective vertical framing;
- large upper identity field;
- central white/bright reel rectangle;
- broad lower illuminated/graphic panel;
- low horizontal STOP row.

The exact licensed artwork is not reusable. The spatial lesson is.

### Interpretation

At aisle distance, fine reel symbols and mini-LCD content disappear first. The remaining readable structure is **large-area silhouette, luminance blocks and material contrast**. Once seated, the hierarchy reverses: fine reel relationships become primary and the same large identity masses become peripheral context.

This strengthens Run 1's principle with a more operational test:

`TWO-RESOLUTION CABINET TEST`

Evaluate a machine at two minimum visual resolutions:

1. **coarse / aisle resolution** — if symbols/text disappear, is the cabinet still one identifiable object?
2. **fine / seated resolution** — if branding is ignored, is the game-critical surface still calm and discriminable?

A strong hall cabinet does not require the same element to win at both distances.

## 7. Visual escalation is not equivalent to spatial expansion

### Manufacturer fact + direct observation

The official page identifies changed blackout rules and mini-LCD `たまやチャレンジ`, while BIG→RT still retains the physical reels and same core controls. Run 1 already established that blackout/flash can carry meaning.

Run 2 adds the mechanical observation that the cabinet itself offers many existing planes — reel light, side light, small display, upper/lower panels, STOP/control illumination — **without needing a moving structure to invade more space during reward states**.

### Interpretation

The product can escalate by changing:

- which existing plane carries attention;
- luminance contrast;
- temporal sequencing;
- information density;
- control responsibility;

rather than by physically expanding the machine toward the player.

Reusable principle:

`ESCALATE RESPONSIBILITY BEFORE ESCALATING VOLUME`

Here `volume` means visual/physical occupied space, not audio loudness.

This is especially relevant to a reel-reading machine: large spatial motion could destroy the visual constancy that makes slip, stopped form and contradiction learnable.

## 8. What appears deliberately still — refined

Run 1 concluded that no major moving役物 is evidenced as the machine's core presentation device. Run 2's oblique views clarify why stillness does not make it visually flat:

- real reel cylinders move every game;
- lever and buttons provide mechanical interaction;
- recessed grille cavities produce static depth;
- clear cover, reflective trim and translucent lamps react differently to ambient light;
- shell/deck/front-body depth creates parallax as the viewer shifts position;
- illumination changes can move perceptually without moving geometry.

Reusable principle:

`STATIC GEOMETRY CAN STILL HAVE DYNAMIC OPTICS`

Mechanism study should not equate `mechanism` with `large actuator`. Material reflection, parallax, reel-cylinder motion, button actuation and lamp transmission are also machine behavior.

## 9. State communication versus decoration — refined allocation

### Strong evidence of state / play communication
- physical reel result itself;
- reel blackout / flash grammar described by manufacturer/analysis sources;
- mini-LCD `たまやチャレンジ` information;
- bonus / RT instructions and changed reel responsibilities.

### Strong evidence of identity / hall-presence responsibility
- overall red / reflective front shell massing;
- fixed large branding panels;
- speaker grille geometry as static silhouette;
- broad lower panel identity.

### Shared hardware, state assignment not fully documented
- tall side translucent/light areas;
- some control illumination;
- upper/lower panel illumination intensity.

The study must not force every lit part into a probability hierarchy merely because it can emit light.

Reusable principle:

`NOT EVERY LAMP NEEDS TO BE A WORD`

Some light may establish presence/identity; game-critical lamps need a stable syntax. Mixing every surface into state communication can make the machine louder but less readable.

## 10. New cross-discipline handoff

### To Sound & Experience

The visible bilateral upper grille architecture and field evidence of multiple speakers / a reported woofer make spatial sound a legitimate physical question, but exact channels remain unresolved. Sound should avoid claiming a confirmed stereo/woofer topology until primary or service evidence exists.

Visual's important synchronization question is instead:

**When a reel blackout/flash/STOP result carries the rule, does Sound reinforce that same gaze event or drag attention to a different surface?**

### To Playtest & QA

Future human-machine testing should separate:

- aisle recognition;
- seated reel readability;
- peripheral lamp distraction;
- hand finding of lever/STOP without gaze relocation;
- long-duration reflection/glare fatigue on the reel cover;
- whether strong side illumination degrades symbol discrimination in a dark hall.

These are not verifiable from web photography alone.

## 11. Reusable principles added in Run 2

- `FRONT THEATRE / BACK BOX SEPARATION` — concentrate identity depth where the player/aisle sees it; structural volume need not be equally ornate.
- `DEPTH SHOULD CLARIFY INFORMATION` — use physical planes to separate reel, bezel, lamp, panel and controls rather than adding depth only as decoration.
- `HAND PLANE != GAZE PLANE` — frequent controls can be spatially stable and tactile while the eye stays on the game surface.
- `SPEAKER OPENINGS ARE PART OF SILHOUETTE` — acoustic hardware has a static visual/mechanical responsibility.
- `PERIPHERAL ENERGY, CENTRAL PRECISION` — large-area light can live around the work surface while rule-bearing light remains discriminable at the center.
- `TWO-RESOLUTION CABINET TEST` — judge aisle/coarse identity separately from seated/fine game readability.
- `ESCALATE RESPONSIBILITY BEFORE ESCALATING VOLUME` — change meaning/timing/attention before adding more occupied physical space.
- `STATIC GEOMETRY CAN HAVE DYNAMIC OPTICS` — parallax, reflection, transmission, reel motion and lamp change can create machine life without a giant actuator.
- `NOT EVERY LAMP NEEDS TO BE A WORD` — preserve a distinction between presence lighting and rule-bearing lighting.

## 12. Contradictions / unresolved retained

1. Official product material confirms the Archer cabinet name but does not provide the exact external dimensions, weight, reel diameter, reel-window depth, deck angle, switch forces or material chemistry in the material opened here.
2. Generic seller shipping dimensions must not be promoted to exact 新ハナビ specifications.
3. Used-machine photos can reveal geometry but cannot establish OEM lamp color/state tables because exposure, service state and modifications are uncontrolled.
4. Seller statements about `all speakers` and `woofer` are useful physical clues, not manufacturer-confirmed acoustic architecture.
5. Chrome-look, glass-look and resin-look remain appearance descriptions unless primary material specifications are found.
6. No source opened supports inventing actuator mechanisms, LED counts, PWM behavior, optical diffusion construction or hidden stop-switch engineering.
7. Long-duration glare, eye fatigue and peripheral-light distraction remain `HUMAN / CONTROLLED TEST REQUIRED`.

## 13. Run 2 conclusion

新ハナビ's restraint should not be summarized as `a simple cabinet`. The oblique physical evidence shows a highly layered object whose **front shell, grille cavities, recessed reel plane, translucent side light, projecting deck and STOP fascia all create machine presence while the central repeated work surface remains stable.**

The deeper Visual & Mechanism lesson is:

`A CABINET CAN SPEND PHYSICAL COMPLEXITY ON PRESENCE WITHOUT SPENDING PERCEPTUAL COMPLEXITY ON EVERY GAME.`

That distinction helps explain how a machine can remain visible in a noisy hall and still support hours of fine reel reading.
