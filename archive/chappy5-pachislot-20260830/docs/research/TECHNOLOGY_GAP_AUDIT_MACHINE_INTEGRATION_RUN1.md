## Scope
Machine Director integrated the four specialist Run 1 audits plus the existing Machine framework. This is a readiness diagnosis, not production authorization and not an original-concept selection.

## Integrated verdict
The accumulated Hokuto / Shin Hanabi / Madoka 2 / Oki Doki GOLD studies have produced sufficient broad pachislot literacy to stop treating “study more famous machines” as the default answer. The dominant remaining deficits are not machine-tourism deficits. They are the ability to author, synchronize, translate to iPhone, measure and Human-validate an original machine as one causal system.

### LEARNED
- broad state/reward architectures and the distinction between vertical and lateral depth;
- repeated 3-STOP play can derive value from prediction, classification, technique and learned attention rather than constant escalation;
- REST, attention headroom, return architecture and question closure are part of long-session quality;
- real→phone translation must separate logical state, action ownership, wall-clock exposure and physical/sensory embodiment;
- visual hierarchy, reel physicality, causal audio timing and sparse presentation principles are understood well enough for the present research decision;
- Browser correctness is not Human playability or reality presence.

### KNOWLEDGE GAP
High-value unknowns are now narrower and question-led: how cross-domain timing and causal coherence make separately-correct reel, light, mechanism and sound events feel like one physical machine; what tolerances remain perceptually stable over repeated play; and which unknowns truly require commercial-machine evidence versus general HCI/perception evidence. Proprietary commercial stop tables, exact material recipes and mastering chains are not prerequisites and should not be reverse-engineered by default.

### TECHNOLOGY GAP
- generalized original reel-stop/control law and canonical complete-spin event trace;
- deterministic presentation/audio scheduling driven by that same event truth;
- coherent cabinet depth/material/light/reel/mechanism rendering on target iPhone;
- state-aware audio priority/mix and original machine-body substitution;
- cross-domain event contract preventing private subsystem clocks from drifting apart.

### MEASUREMENT GAP
- authored math targets → long-run statistical acceptance;
- touch/event → visual/audio response distributions and inter-STOP jitter;
- visual reality acceptance for depth, emissive hierarchy, REST and motion continuity;
- long-session workload/fatigue, novelty decay, intrinsic loop value and HOME-return evidence;
- a versioned Human Experience protocol that separates pre-reward loop quality from reward relief.

### PLATFORM GAP
- actual iPhone touch timestamp/aiming/hitbox/finger-occlusion behavior;
- Safari viewport, audio unlock, interruption, route change, suspend/resume and recovery;
- device-speaker/headphone hierarchy;
- thermal/frame/battery behavior over long sessions;
- translating multiple physical materials, acoustic body and controls onto one emissive touch surface.

## Machine decision
Do not open another broad Case Study merely because Stage 1 remains open. The next learning should be specialist self-directed research aimed at the gaps above. A new real-machine comparison is justified only when a Director can state a precise unanswered question that existing evidence cannot resolve.

Reality Baseline remains incomplete because understanding is not yet demonstrated as measurable, target-device, Human-validated capability.
