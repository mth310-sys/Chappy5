# 1. Why this topic is next

Visual & Mechanism Run 6 established that responsibility, not novelty, should earn interruption authority. A stopped reel can itself reopen attention because it changes the next legal/valuable action or inference. The unresolved Sound question is whether audio can perform the same boundary work without becoming a second permanent inspection channel.

The target distinction is:

`RESPONSIBILITY-BEARING AUDIO`
= changes or confirms what the player must now do, infer, or attribute.

versus

`DECORATIVE AUDIO INTERRUPTION`
= captures attention without changing responsibility.

This is especially important in reel-centered normal-type play because audio must coexist with an already rich physical evidence surface rather than replace it.

---

# 2. Prior unknown

Sound Run 5 established:

- good auditory offloading removes a check rather than adding one;
- routine audio should protect reel reading rather than duplicate it;
- reliable silence can be informative;
- audio teaches a monitoring policy;
- false orienting can create defensive listening.

What remained unresolved was **how audio should reopen responsibility after monitoring has been safely compressed**, and how quickly it should release the player back to low-duty reel-centered play once the temporary responsibility is resolved.

# 3. Real-machine anchor — 新ハナビ retained, not reopened

## 3.1 Start-timing deviation can reopen classification duty

### SOURCED FACT

Major strategy documentation for 新ハナビ identifies `リール始動音遅れ` as a normal-play deviation associated with cherry or bonus possibility.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/

### OBSERVATION

The value of this cue is not that it is louder or more spectacular than routine operation. Its meaning comes from violating the learned baseline at the start boundary of a game and causing the player to reclassify an otherwise ordinary spin.

### INTERPRETATION

This is an example of **rule-bearing timing deviation**. The audio does not need to tell the player the final answer. It reopens attention early enough for the player to preserve reel-reading responsibility.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`A RESPONSIBILITY CUE SHOULD REOPEN THE QUESTION BEFORE THE PLAYER MUST ANSWER IT.`

`TIMING DEVIATION CAN CARRY RULE VALUE WITHOUT ESCALATING SPECTACLE.`

## 3.2 RT uses audio to change procedure, not merely atmosphere

### SOURCED FACT

Published 新ハナビ RT procedures distinguish moments where a predictive cue changes the required stopping procedure. During 花火チャレンジ, a prediction sound is associated with reverse-order technical intervention, while a separate ordered instruction is used for JAC IN. Published material also documents BGM cessation as a bonus indication during 花火チャレンジ.

Sources:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3869/
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/64/

Published 花火GAME documentation likewise identifies a prediction sound as one of the events that changes the player's small-role handling requirement relative to otherwise free play.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3873/

### OBSERVATION

The same physical lever and three STOP buttons remain in use while responsibility changes. The machine does not need a new interaction map; it temporarily changes **what those controls mean now**.

### INTERPRETATION

Audio is strongest here when it acts as a **procedure gate**: routine can stay compressed until a specific cue reopens technical or role-handling duty. Once that duty is resolved, the audio should not continue demanding inspection merely because the state remains special.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`AUDIO MAY REOPEN PROCEDURE WITHOUT RELOCATING MOTOR OWNERSHIP.`

`STATE AUDIO EARNS AUTHORITY WHEN IT CHANGES THE CURRENT ACTION LAW.`

`DO NOT KEEP RESPONSIBILITY OPEN AFTER THE PROCEDURAL QUESTION IS RESOLVED.`

## 3.3 Continuity and omission can be state evidence

### SOURCED FACT

Published 新ハナビ material documents BGM cessation during 花火チャレンジ as a bonus indication.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/64/

Auditory predictive-processing research shows that an expected sound's omission can itself produce a measurable prediction-error response, particularly when the expected event is predictable and behaviorally relevant.

Sources:
- `The sound of silence: Omission responses and how the brain predicts in the absence of sound`, Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 2025. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41354114/
- `Silent Expectations: Dynamic Causal Modeling of Cortical Prediction and Attention to Sounds That Weren't`, Journal of Neuroscience, 2016. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27511005/
- `Suppression and omission effects in auditory predictive processing—Two of the same?`, European Journal of Neuroscience, 2024. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38764129/

### INTERPRETATION

A stable BGM or repeating machine-audio bed can become a learned expectation. Under those conditions, **stopping** can function as a stronger information event than adding another effect. But omission only works if the baseline was reliable enough to be predicted.

This means silence is not inherently subtle, tasteful, or meaningful. It becomes meaningful only when the omitted event had acquired stable expectation and the player is not required to monitor every possible dropout defensively.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`OMISSION HAS AUTHORITY ONLY AFTER CONTINUITY HAS EARNED PREDICTABILITY.`

`A MISSING EXPECTED SOUND CAN REOPEN RESPONSIBILITY WITHOUT ADDING A NEW SOUND.`

`TECHNICAL DROPOUT MUST NOT IMPERSONATE AUTHORED OMISSION.`

# 4. Responsibility reopening is a three-part temporal contract

This run separates audio responsibility handling into three phases.

## 4.1 OPEN

A sound/timing deviation may reopen responsibility when it changes:

- the next required action;
- the current role hypothesis;
- the current state procedure;
- the need to attribute a causal anomaly.

The cue must arrive before the relevant action boundary.

`OPEN = EARLY ENOUGH TO CHANGE WHAT THE PLAYER DOES OR INFERS.`

## 4.2 HOLD

Once responsibility is open, audio should provide only the minimum information required to sustain the temporary task. It should not continuously escalate merely because the state is important.

`HOLD = MAINTAIN THE TEMPORARY RULE, NOT THE EXCITEMENT LEVEL.`

## 4.3 RELEASE

Once the technical/inference duty is resolved, audio should allow monitoring to collapse again. Continuing to issue salient cues after responsibility closes trains unnecessary vigilance.

`RELEASE = RETURN THE PLAYER TO THE LOW-DUTY BASELINE WITHOUT MAKING THEM VERIFY THAT RELEASE.`

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`GOOD AUDIO INTERRUPTION HAS AN EXIT CONDITION.`

`AN INTERRUPT THAT CANNOT RELEASE BECOMES A NEW BASELINE BURDEN.`

# 5. Auditory deviation should map to responsibility magnitude, not spectacle magnitude

### SOURCED FACT

Human-factors research shows that warning acoustics affect perceived urgency, and that people continue to respond to invalid or proximal warnings even when they are imperfectly reliable.

Sources:
- Edworthy, Loxley & Dennis, `Improving auditory warning design: relationship between warning sound parameters and perceived urgency`, Human Factors 33(2), 1991. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1860703/
- Meyer, `Effects of warning validity and proximity on responses to warnings`, Human Factors 43(4), 2001. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12002005/
- Gilson et al., `Behavioral influences of proximal alarms`, Human Factors 43(4), 2001. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12002008/

### INTERPRETATION

For pachislot, these studies do not authorize alarm-style design or numeric urgency thresholds. They support a narrower conclusion: **salience itself changes behavior**. Therefore using highly interruptive audio for low-responsibility events can distort the player's learned monitoring policy.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`AUDIO SALIENCE SHOULD TRACK RESPONSIBILITY CHANGE MORE CLOSELY THAN CELEBRATION INTENSITY.`

`FALSE URGENCY TRAINS FALSE RESPONSIBILITY.`

`NOT EVERY MEMORABLE SOUND DESERVES RE-ORIENTING AUTHORITY.`

# 6. The reel remains the proof surface

### SOURCED FACT / CLOSED CASE ANCHOR

新ハナビ's published normal/RT procedures repeatedly combine audio or flash cues with reel handling rather than replacing the reel result itself. A start-delay can reclassify the spin; prediction sounds can reopen role/technical duty; stopped reels and resulting forms still determine what the player has actually observed.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3869/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3873/

### INTERPRETATION

In a reel-reading machine, audio should most often answer one of these questions:

- `Did my action register?`
- `Did the rule/state that governs the next STOP change?`
- `Did an expected state continue or terminate?`
- `Do I need to inspect the reel evidence more carefully now?`

It should not routinely answer:

- `What exact stopped-reel evidence means`, when the reel itself is the skill surface.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`AUDIO MAY REOPEN REEL ATTENTION; IT SHOULD NOT PRE-EMPT REEL INTERPRETATION.`

`THE CUE MAY SAY “LOOK AGAIN”; THE REEL SHOULD STILL SAY “WHY.”`

# 7. Repetition and fatigue

### SOURCED FACT

Auditory-deviance research indicates that regularity violations can be detected even outside the current focus of attention, but high workload can also cause important auditory alerts to be missed (`inattentional deafness`).

Sources:
- `Deviance detection based on regularity encoding along the auditory hierarchy`, Brain Topography, 2013. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24218032/
- Dehais et al., `Failure to detect critical auditory alerts in the cockpit: evidence for inattentional deafness`, Human Factors 56(4), 2014. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25029890/

### INTERPRETATION

Sparse audio does not guarantee a deviation will be detected. Nor does repeated salience guarantee reliability: over-signaling can create fatigue/defensive listening, while an over-compressed baseline can allow a meaningful cue to disappear under reel workload.

Therefore repeated-play quality requires both:

1. low false interruption during routine play;
2. preserved detection of genuinely responsibility-bearing deviations while eyes remain reel-centered.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`SPARSE AUDIO IS USEFUL ONLY IF THE IMPORTANT DEVIATION SURVIVES REAL TASK LOAD.`

`LOW FALSE INTERRUPTION AND HIGH RESPONSIBILITY-DEVIATION DETECTION MUST COEXIST.`

# 8. New integrated model

`SAFE AUDITORY AUTOMATICITY = PREDICTABLE ROUTINE + LOW FALSE INTERRUPTION + TIMELY RESPONSIBILITY OPENING + MINIMAL DUTY HOLD + CLEAN RELEASE + REEL-PRIMACY PRESERVATION`

A practical research-only classification for future Human testing:

### A. ACTION ACCEPTANCE AUDIO
Confirms that a physical action registered. Should usually collapse into background expectation.

### B. RULE-BEARING DEVIATION
Reopens attention because the next action/inference law changed.

### C. TEMPORARY STATE DUTY AUDIO
Holds a changed procedure only while that procedure remains active.

### D. RESOLUTION / RELEASE AUDIO
Confirms that temporary responsibility is resolved and permits return to low monitoring.

### E. DECORATIVE SALIENCE
May be memorable, but if it changes no action/inference/state duty it must not be treated as responsibility-bearing by default.

# 9. Changed understanding

Before this run, the likely formulation was:

`important sound = attract attention`

The stronger formulation is:

`important sound = alter or confirm the current responsibility contract`

This shifts Sound evaluation from loudness/memorability toward whether a cue opens, sustains, or closes a real player duty at the correct boundary.

# 11. Limits / misuse risks

- Human-factors alarm studies are not pachislot specification tables; no urgency values or acoustic parameters are imported.
- Neurophysiological omission/deviance findings do not prove that a specific game cue will be consciously detected.
- Published 新ハナビ pages establish procedures and cue relationships, not proprietary waveform, loudness, speaker tuning, mixing, or exact temporal offsets.
- No conclusion is drawn about undocumented internal stop-control or audio-control tables.
- No melodies, samples, signature sounds or protected arrangement details are reproduced.
