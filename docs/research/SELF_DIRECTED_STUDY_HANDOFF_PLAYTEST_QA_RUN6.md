## Central correction

`LESS CHECKING != LOWER MONITORING COST.`

Reduced checking can reflect:
- healthy skill/trust compression;
- defensive listening changing form;
- habituation/desensitization;
- fatigue;
- abandonment of the audio channel.

Therefore low checking alone cannot PASS auditory offloading.

## Integrated QA model

Useful offloading requires convergence among:

`ROUTINE RE-CHECKS DOWN`

AND

`PRIMARY REEL-TASK ACCURACY PRESERVED`

AND

`MEANINGFUL-DEVIATION DETECTION PRESERVED`

AND

`NORMAL-EVENT FALSE ALARMS CONTROLLED`

AND

`CAUSAL ATTRIBUTION REMAINS COHERENT`

AND

`NO COMPENSATORY SLOWDOWN / WAITING / DOUBLE-CHECK POLICY`

Research target:

`MINIMUM NECESSARY MONITORING AT PRESERVED DISCRIMINATION.`

## Main principles

- `CHECKING DOWN + DETECTION DOWN = DESENSITIZATION, NOT OFFLOADING.`
- `OFFLOADING PASSES ONLY IF MEANINGFUL DEVIATION SURVIVES PRIMARY-TASK ENGAGEMENT.`
- `TEST THE LEARNED MONITORING POLICY, NOT ONLY THE CUE.`
- `WHEN DIRECT GAZE MEASUREMENT IS ABSENT, REQUIRE CONVERGING BEHAVIORAL EVIDENCE.`
- `DO NOT TURN SELF-REPORT INTO FAKE EYE TRACKING.`
- `MEASURE SPONTANEOUS MONITORING BEFORE NAMING THE CHANNEL.`
- `A PROBE THAT TEACHES WHAT TO MONITOR INVALIDATES THE NEXT MONITORING MEASURE.`
- `OFFLOADING REQUIRES CORRECT REJECTION AS WELL AS DETECTION.`
- `A PLAYER WHO FLAGS EVERYTHING HAS NOT LEARNED WHAT CAN BE SAFELY IGNORED.`
- `NEVER ACCEPT LOWER MONITORING COST BY SACRIFICING THE PRIMARY SKILL SURFACE.`
- `MASTERY, FATIGUE AND DESENSITIZATION CAN ALL REDUCE OVERT CHECKING; QA MUST SEPARATE THEM BY PRESERVED DISCRIMINATION.`

## Evidence boundary

Cross-domain evidence supports that:
- high visual/cognitive load can suppress awareness/report of unexpected auditory events;
- auditory cues can reduce visual-task disruption in some conditions;
- unreliable/repetitive alerts can change trust and monitoring policy.

No laboratory numeric thresholds are imported as pachislot criteria.
