## Question received from Playtest & QA Run 5

How should the cross-Director `ATTENTION CONTRACT` and `SOURCE AUTHORITY` models distinguish a machine that teaches a stable causal law from a machine whose users merely learn to tolerate unstable timing/feedback?

## Sourced evidence

### S1 — delayed-feedback adaptation can move the acceptance criterion without improving discrimination
Sugano (2021), *Audiomotor Temporal Recalibration Modulates Decision Criterion of Self-Agency but Not Perceptual Sensitivity*, used signal-detection analysis after delayed auditory action feedback. Repeated exposure changed the decision criterion for self-agency but did not improve perceptual sensitivity for discriminating control.

Source: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.580441
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33981264/

### S2 — action outcome timing can matter more for agency than movement feedback timing
David et al. (2016) manipulated delay of movement-related feedback and action outcome feedback. Agency reports were more sensitive to outcome delay than movement-feedback delay, in both auditory and visual outcome versions.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0161156

### S3 — outcome valence can retrospectively bias agency judgment
Oishi, Tanaka & Watanabe (2018) found higher reported agency for shorter delays and for successful outcomes even when action experiences were otherwise closely controlled. This supports separating causal quality from reward/result evaluation.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0202690

### S4 — delay effects operate at multiple levels
Wen (2019) reviews evidence that delay affects agency through multiple processes and that higher-level causal judgment may not track lower-level timing sensitivity one-to-one.

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31173998/

## Machine interpretation

A player can become behaviorally fluent around a degraded action-effect relation without the machine becoming more causally legible. Therefore long-session smoothness is ambiguous evidence.

Define two different states:

`CAUSAL TRUST`
= the player has learned a sufficiently stable relation between action, machine response, state and resolution that prediction errors remain diagnostic.

`LEARNED TOLERANCE`
= the player has shifted expectation, timing criterion, pace or compensation behavior so an unreliable relation produces less conscious friction, without improved source discrimination.

These must never be merged into one `familiarity` or `comfort` score.

## Attention Contract Run 5

Previous model:

`CLAIM OPENER → RESPONSIBILITY → SOURCE AUTHORITY → CROSS-CHANNEL SUPPORT → RESOLUTION → RELEASE / RE-ANCHOR`

Run 5 adds a longitudinal trust condition:

`STABLE BASELINE → CLAIM OPENER → RESPONSIBILITY → SOURCE AUTHORITY → CROSS-CHANNEL SUPPORT → RESOLUTION → RELEASE → TRUST UPDATE → LOW-MONITORING REST`

`TRUST UPDATE` is not a reward meter. It asks whether the just-completed event strengthened, preserved or weakened the player's predictive model of the machine.

### Trust-positive event

A surprising event may remain trust-positive when:
- the initiating action was acknowledged;
- the event belongs to a learnable machine law;
- cross-channel evidence is coherent rather than merely simultaneous;
- the outcome/resolution explains what responsibility just ended;
- normal operation returns clearly enough that vigilance can be released.

### Tolerance-only event

A session may look smooth while trust is weak when:
- timing or feedback is variable enough that the player stops expecting precision;
- the player slows down or adds checking rituals;
- favorable results retrospectively make poor ownership feel acceptable;
- anomalies cease to be noticed because the criterion widened, not because the source became clearer;
- the player remains in low-level defensive monitoring instead of returning to REST.

## New cross-Director principles

- `TRUST IS PREDICTIVE PRECISION; TOLERANCE IS WIDENED ACCEPTANCE.`
- `A PLAYER GETTING USED TO LATENCY DOES NOT MAKE LATENCY PART OF THE MACHINE LAW.`
- `SMOOTH PERFORMANCE AFTER ADAPTATION IS NOT SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE OF CAUSAL QUALITY.`
- `GOOD OUTCOMES MUST NOT LAUNDER BAD OWNERSHIP.`
- `COMPENSATION BEHAVIOR IS PART OF MACHINE COST.`
- `A TRUSTED MACHINE LETS THE PLAYER LOWER MONITORING AFTER RESOLUTION.`
- `A MACHINE EARNS SURPRISE AUTHORITY BY KEEPING ORDINARY CAUSAL PROMISES.`
- `SOURCE AUTHORITY SHOULD NARROW UNCERTAINTY; LEARNED TOLERANCE MERELY WIDENS WHAT THE PLAYER WILL ACCEPT.`
- `LONG-SESSION REALITY REQUIRES STABLE CAUSAL LAW PLUS RECOVERABLE ATTENTION, NOT HABITUATION TO PLATFORM DEFECTS.`
