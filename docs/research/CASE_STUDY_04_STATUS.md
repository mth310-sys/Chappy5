## Why this lineage now

This case deliberately contrasts with prior studies:
- Hokuto: AT/state/continuation/upper ladder
- New Hanabi: normal/reach-eye/technical intervention/lateral reel depth
- Madoka 2: A+ART with real BONUS and persistent ART layers
