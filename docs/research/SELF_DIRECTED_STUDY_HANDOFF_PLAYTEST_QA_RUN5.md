Question received from Sound & Experience Run 4:

**Can Human attribute a STOP timing anomaly to the correct source—intentional machine behavior, own input, or platform/audio failure—while normal STOPs remain low-monitoring-cost, and can this be measured without first teaching the anomaly categories?**

## Key change

Split STOP anomaly QA into three independent variables:

`ANOMALY DETECTION`
→ `SOURCE ATTRIBUTION`
→ `CAUSAL TRUST`

and add a fourth longitudinal concern:

`LEARNED TOLERANCE` must not be mistaken for better discrimination.

Research evidence indicates that repeated delayed feedback can shift the criterion for accepting an effect as self-caused without improving perceptual sensitivity. Cross-modal cues are integrated according to learned reliability, and favorable task outcome can also mask delay detection or inflate agency judgments.

## Main principles

- `CORRECT SOURCE ATTRIBUTION = RELIABLE CUE INTEGRATION, NOT SINGLE-CUE DETECTION.`
- `LEARNED TOLERANCE != IMPROVED CAUSAL DISCRIMINATION.`
- `COMPENSATION BEHAVIOR IS EVIDENCE OF A PROBLEM, NOT PROOF THAT THE PROBLEM DISAPPEARED.`
- `A GOOD OUTCOME CAN MASK BAD OWNERSHIP; A BAD OUTCOME CAN CONTAMINATE OWNERSHIP JUDGMENT.`
- `DETECTION, ATTRIBUTION AND TRUST ARE THREE DIFFERENT QA VARIABLES.`
- `DO NOT TEACH THE CAUSAL MENU BEFORE MEASURING SPONTANEOUS CAUSAL INFERENCE.`
- `AN ATTRIBUTION TEST WITHOUT NORMAL-EVENT FALSE ALARMS REWARDS PARANOIA.`
- `TRACK CRITERION SHIFT SEPARATELY FROM SENSITIVITY.`
- `LONG-SESSION TRUST IS SELECTIVE COMPRESSION, NOT GLOBAL TOLERANCE.`
- `REEL MASTERY REQUIRES TRUST IN THE CAUSAL CHAIN THAT PRODUCES THE REEL EVIDENCE.`

## Research-only future measurement shape

Without authorizing implementation, a future Human trial should preserve this order:

1. let the Human learn routine action-effect relations without warning about anomalies;
2. use sparse post-event probes rather than continuous self-monitoring;
3. ask whether anything felt different;
4. ask for a free cause attribution;
5. record confidence and next-action change;
6. only then offer standardized categories if needed;
7. include ordinary baseline probes to measure false alarms;
8. compare source discrimination with criterion/tolerance changes across repetition.

No timing threshold is set from laboratory studies.
