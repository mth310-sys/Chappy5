## 1. Why this machine is the next shared object

Case Study 01 studied an AT/state/continuation ladder. Case Study 02 studied normal/reach-eye/technical-intervention play. Case Study 03 studied A+ART with local BONUS and persistent ART layers.

Case Study 04 deliberately moves to a machine whose repeated value is built around a much simpler visible contract:

`LEVER / REELS / HIBISCUS NOTIFICATION / BONUS / 32G POST-BONUS EXPECTATION / MODE CONTINUATION`

The aim is not to copy Okin Doki's protected branding, lamp design, music or exact presentation. The aim is to understand how a machine with comparatively simple visible rules can generate strong identity, tension, repetition and post-reward continuation through timing, mode expectation and a direct physical notification device.

## 4. Phase A — real-machine study questions

### Machine Director
- Why does a player sit down when the visible rule set is so simple?
- Where does the machine's depth live if not in CZ branching or technical reel discovery?
- How do 999G-scale dry possibilities coexist with a 32G high-tension post-BONUS loop?
- Is the local BONUS satisfying on its own or mainly valued as a gateway to continued 32G chains?
- How does a direct lamp notification create trust, anticipation and repeated leverage without constant multi-layer presentation?

### Game & Reel Director
Deep-dive:
- actual reel arrays and symbol roles;
- valid lines and pseudo-BONUS handling where documented;
- ordinary role structure and rare-role responsibilities;
- how much precise aiming is actually required in normal/BONUS play;
- what the three STOPs mean when the primary reward truth is often carried by notification/mode rather than reach-eye discovery;
- mode family, game-number ceiling, 32G chain logic and transitions only where sourced;
- do not infer undisclosed mode-transition rates or hidden control tables;
- player behavior over 100G, 500G, a BONUS, 32G continuation, chain end and 3 hours.

### Visual & Mechanism Director
Deep-dive:
- 30φ cabinet silhouette and proportions;
- physical reel-window size/depth/readability;
- hibiscus lamp placement, translucency, brightness role and visibility in a noisy hall;
- gold cabinet identity versus seated task readability;
- operation deck / lever / MAX BET / STOP ergonomics;
- speaker/lamp locations where documented or visible;
- REST VISUAL before notification;
- what stays quiet so the lamp event has impact;
- BONUS and 32G post-BONUS visual transitions;
- distinguish documented material/device facts from photo-based interpretation.

### Sound & Experience Director
Deep-dive:
- baseline BET/lever/reel/STOP/payout/replay sound routine;
- relationship between quiet routine and direct lamp/notification event;
- exact timing of notification relative to lever/reel/stop where documented;
- BONUS entry, sustain and end audio;
- 32G post-BONUS expectation and how sound changes or stays restrained;
- repeated fatigue across long dry sections;
- memorable cue versus exposure burden;
- speaker/low-frequency behavior only where documented;
- never reproduce signature sounds, melodies or samples.

### Playtest & QA Director
Reconstruct:
- first 5 minutes: what does a beginner learn to watch?
- normal 100G with little happening;
- 500G dry stretch without a major reward;
- first direct notification and BONUS;
- 32G continuation expectation;
- chain continuation versus chain end;
- post-chain return to normal;
- 3-hour fatigue / vigilance / relief seeking;
- beginner versus experienced player's mode inference and stop-duty differences;
- distinguish intrinsic routine satisfaction from relief at finally seeing the lamp light.
