## 1. Reel array / role vocabulary — full array is reference data, not something to copy

### SOURCED FACT

Major strategy material publishes the three real reel arrays and the role construction for 沖ドキ！GOLD. The same source documents the visible role vocabulary as replay, common/press-order bell forms, cherry A/B, middle cherry, watermelon, definite role A and definite role B. Common bell can appear as 3-coin or 8-coin forms depending on the documented stop form.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12849/

### RESEARCH HANDLING

The actual full symbol order is confirmed to be publicly documented in reel-table form, but this study does **not** duplicate the protected full array or artwork. What matters for reusable understanding is the responsibility created by the arrangement:

- BAR is a practical visual reference for ordinary play.
- watermelon is a conditional follow role that can require additional aiming.
- cherry families and definite roles can be distinguished from representative stop forms.
- the same physical reels support a different information route during BONUS when middle-first play is available.

### UNRESOLVED

No undisclosed stop-control table, slip table, internal priority table or exhaustive `press position → stop position` map was found. Representative stop forms must not be expanded into an invented complete control model.

## 2. Normal play: precision duty is conditional, not continuous

### SOURCED FACT

The documented ordinary procedure is left-first play with BAR aimed around the upper frame. If watermelon appears, the middle and right reels also require watermelon follow; otherwise the remaining reels may be stopped freely.

Representative documented branches include:

- a left stop containing BAR/cherry/watermelon corresponding to middle cherry, after which middle/right may be free-stopped;
- a bell/BAR/cherry left stop corresponding to cherry A/B or definite role A;
- a replay/bell/BAR left stop containing miss/replay/common bell/definite role A possibilities;
- upper watermelon on the left requiring middle/right watermelon follow, with a watermelon line versus a small-V definite-role-B form;
- middle watermelon on the left requiring middle/right watermelon follow, with watermelon alignment versus a watermelon-miss definite-role-A form.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12849/

### INTERPRETATION

The ordinary motor loop is therefore not `precision × 3 every game`.

It is closer to:

`LEFT REFERENCE AIM → CLASSIFY LEFT STOP → IF WATERMELON BRANCH, INCREASE PRECISION → OTHERWISE RELEASE PRECISION → COMPLETE 3 STOP`.

This is deliberately different from 新ハナビ. In 新ハナビ, knowledge of stop position, slip and small-role denial can continuously turn the reel surface into bonus evidence. Here, the reels mainly provide **economic role handling and occasional role classification**, while direct notification carries much of the reward certainty.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**PRECISION DUTY CAN BE EVENT-TRIGGERED RATHER THAN GAME-WIDE.**

A three-reel machine can preserve physical participation while keeping ordinary motor cost low by requiring precision only when the first stop exposes a follow branch.

## 3. Internal role versus observed stop form

### SOURCED FACT

The strategy source explicitly presents its stop forms as examples and maps several observed forms to multiple possible established roles. Cherry A/B can also be distinguished by documented stop-pattern rules, and the strategy material uses this distinction because cherry B has a setting-dependent appearance rate.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12849/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/

### INTERPRETATION

The visible result is not the internal role itself. Even on a machine whose famous truth signal is a lamp, the reel surface still performs a smaller but real inference job:

`INTERNAL ROLE → CONTROL + PLAYER STOP INPUT → OBSERVED FORM → ROLE CLASSIFICATION / FOLLOW DUTY`.

However, unlike a reach-eye machine, the study found no basis to claim that ordinary unlit misses form a deep proprietary bonus-detection grammar. That would be importing 新ハナビ logic into the wrong lineage.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**DO NOT REQUIRE THE REELS TO CARRY EVERY KIND OF TRUTH.**

A machine can use physical reels for handling, classification and cadence while assigning final reward certainty to a different physical signal.

## 4. BONUS changes the meaning of stop order

### SOURCED FACT

During normal play the documented procedure requires left-reel first stop. During BONUS, when no navigation is displayed, variable-order play is possible. The published middle-first procedure aims BAR around the upper frame on the middle reel and can identify watermelon / definite-role possibilities earlier.

Documented middle-first branches include:

- middle-reel middle bell → common bell, remaining reels free;
- a middle stop with watermelon/BAR/cherry → middle cherry or definite role, remaining reels free;
- middle-reel middle watermelon → watermelon or definite role, requiring right watermelon follow;
- middle-reel lower watermelon → replay or cherry A/B, then left cherry follow.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12849/

### INTERPRETATION

The same three STOP buttons change responsibility by state:

- `NORMAL`: protect expected value cheaply; first-stop classification decides whether precision escalates.
- `BONUS / no navigation`: optional middle-first information route can classify rare roles earlier.
- `BONUS / navigation`: navigation owns order responsibility; player follows the machine instead of exploring freely.

This is important because the machine is not “three free stops plus a lamp.” Stop order remains part of the game contract even when reach-eye discovery is not the headline feature.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**STOP RESPONSIBILITY MAY CHANGE BY STATE WITHOUT CHANGING THE HARDWARE.**

A stable motor interface can move between low-cost routine, conditional precision, optional information seeking and navigation compliance.

## 5. Small-role and setting relationship

### SOURCED FACT

Published setting-analysis data lists cherry A as 1/52.5 across settings, while cherry B changes from roughly 1/168.0 at setting 1 to 1/128.0 at setting 6. The combined cherry rate changes from roughly 1/40.0 to 1/37.2. Definite role A is listed as 1/8192 across settings, while definite role B changes from roughly 1/16384 at setting 1 to 1/6553.6 at setting 6. The source still marks watermelon and middle-cherry base appearance rates as under investigation, so this study does not fill them in.

The same analysis documents mode-dependent BONUS winning rates for cherry/watermelon and setting differences in some ordinary-mode cases.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/

### INTERPRETATION

Player skill here is not primarily a high-frequency payout technique. A knowledgeable player can extract more information by:

1. avoiding conditional-role loss;
2. distinguishing cherry A/B and definite-role classes where the visible form permits;
3. relating those observations to setting inference over a long sample.

This produces **low motor difficulty but non-zero observation depth**.

### UNRESOLVED

Published pages do not justify converting these setting clues into a deterministic setting-read model. Long-session inference remains statistical and noisy.

## 6. BONUS is a local reward and a transition point

### SOURCED FACT

The machine uses pseudo-BONUS with approximately 3.0 coins/G. Published basic performance is:

- BIG: 70G, about 210 coins;
- REG: 30G, about 90 coins.

The published entry symbol representation is red-7 alignment for BIG and red-7/red-7/BAR for REG. BONUS play also includes a 1G consecutive-BONUS draw; published material states that a 1G consecutive BONUS is BIG.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12853/

### INTERPRETATION

The local reward has a fixed enough shape to be understood immediately, but its experiential value does not end at the payout. BONUS is also the point at which the next 32G becomes a separate high-attention horizon.

Therefore the complete reward loop must not be simplified to `lamp → payout`.

It is:

`DIRECT NOTIFICATION → PSEUDO-BONUS → POSSIBLE 1G EVENT → POST-BONUS 32G QUESTION → CONTINUE OR CLOSE → RETURN TO LOW-COST NORMAL`.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**A REWARD CAN CLOSE ONE QUESTION WHILE OPENING A SHORTER, STRONGER TIME HORIZON.**

The design value comes from changing the expected time-to-next-resolution, not only increasing audiovisual intensity.

## 7. Mode architecture: depth lives in time, not a visible CZ ladder

### SOURCED FACT

Major strategy material documents ten mode/state categories including normal A/B, pullback, chance, guarantee, heaven, doki-doki, super-doki-doki, gold-doki and non-active interval. Published game-number ceilings are:

- normal A/B: up to 999G;
- pullback/chance: up to 200G;
- guarantee / heaven / doki-doki / super-doki-doki / gold-doki: up to 32G.

Normal B is documented as having at least a 50% transition to heaven-or-higher and not downgrading until that class is reached. Doki-doki and super-doki-doki are documented with 80% and 90% loop rates respectively, with guarantee behavior after drop. Gold-doki is tied to a possible re-entry route after the prior high-continuation sequence.

Sources:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12848/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/

### INTERPRETATION

The hidden depth is largely **temporal**:

- hundreds of games can belong to a low-intensity horizon;
- 200G classes shorten that horizon;
- after BONUS, 32G can become a sharply compressed continuation horizon.

There is no need for a visible `CZ → AT → upper AT` ladder to create state value. The player experiences a change in **how soon the next answer must arrive**.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**STATE VALUE CAN BE EXPRESSED AS A CHANGE IN RESOLUTION HORIZON.**

A hidden state can matter because it changes the plausible/guaranteed time to the next reward, even when the visible motor loop remains almost unchanged.

### UNRESOLVED

Do not infer unpublished full mode-transition matrices, reset behavior beyond sourced rules, proprietary selection logic, or per-game internal lottery tables.

## 8. What the player actually does across time

### First 100G — INTERPRETATION anchored to sourced procedure

The safe routine can be learned quickly:

`BAR reference → left stop classification → mostly free middle/right → occasionally follow watermelon → repeat`.

At setting 1, published combined cherry appearance is roughly 1/40, so a 100G window contains only a small number of cherry observations on average; it is not a constant precision exercise. The unresolved watermelon base rate prevents a complete branch-frequency estimate.

The experience question is therefore not “is every STOP interesting?” but “does the low-cost repeated operation remain trustworthy while the player waits for a direct answer?”

### 500G dry section — SOURCED FACT + INTERPRETATION

Published setting-1 first-BONUS rate is around 1/299.6 overall, while normal A/B can extend to a 999G ceiling. A 500G dry stretch is therefore a relevant real-play test case rather than an impossible edge case.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12853/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12848/

Do **not** convert 1/299.6 into a simple geometric “500G probability” here: the machine is mode-based and has ceilings, so a constant independent-rate model would be misleading.

During a long dry section, motor mastery should reduce effort rather than add new discovery. Cognitive depth shifts toward optional role counting, mode inference, game-count context and continue/quit judgement.

### First BONUS → 32G — INTERPRETATION

The biggest change is not hand movement. The same lever/three-STOP body map persists, but the player’s time horizon contracts sharply. Every next game sits inside a short post-reward decision window.

### Chain end → normal — INTERPRETATION

When the short horizon closes, the player must accept a large expansion back toward the normal-mode horizon. That transition is a core emotional discontinuity of this lineage.

### Three hours — INTERPRETATION

Motor fatigue is plausibly low relative to a technical-intervention machine because high precision is conditional. The likely fatigue transfer is instead:

`VIGILANCE FOR DIRECT NOTIFICATION + POST-BONUS 32G ATTENTION + OPTIONAL MODE INFERENCE + CONTINUE/QUIT DECISIONS`.

This must be tested by Playtest & QA; Game & Reel does not declare long-session enjoyment from rules alone.

## 9. Direct contrast with 新ハナビ

This Case Study is useful precisely because the hardware vocabulary overlaps while the information economy is different.

### 新ハナビ

`AIM / STOP / SLIP / REACH-EYE / ROLE DENIAL / TECHNIQUE` can make the reel surface itself the repeated discovery engine.

### 沖ドキ！GOLD

`LOW-COST STOP ROUTINE / CONDITIONAL ROLE FOLLOW / DIRECT NOTIFICATION / PSEUDO-BONUS / 32G HORIZON / MODE INFERENCE` moves much of repeated value away from reel-discovered bonus truth.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**THREE REELS DO NOT FORCE A SINGLE KIND OF PACHISLOT DEPTH.**

The same three-stop hardware can support:
- discovery depth;
- technical input depth;
- navigation duty;
- low-cost physical cadence with reward truth located elsewhere.

A future original machine must choose deliberately rather than automatically turning every reel into a reach-eye system or every state into an AT ladder.

Key findings for the next Director:

1. Normal HOME is low-cost but not zero-information: left-reference classification and conditional watermelon follow preserve physical reel responsibility.
2. BONUS changes stop-order meaning; middle-first play can become an information route when navigation does not own the order.
3. The machine's largest state change is temporal: up to 999G / 200G / 32G horizons rather than a visible CZ→AT ladder.
4. BIG/REG are local rewards, but BONUS also opens the 32G continuation question.
5. Long-session burden is likely to migrate from precision fatigue toward vigilance, mode inference and the emotional expansion from 32G back to ordinary horizons.
