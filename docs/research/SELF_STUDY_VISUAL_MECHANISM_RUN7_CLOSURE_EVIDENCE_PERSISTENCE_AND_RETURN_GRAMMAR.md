This run answers Game & Reel Run 7's handoff:

> What visual/physical persistence must survive STOP input, finger occlusion and state transition so the player can see both that a temporary reel responsibility ended and which action grammar governs next—without adding a redundant “all clear” overlay?

---

# 1. Topic reason

Game & Reel Run 7 established:

`GOOD REEL CLOSURE = SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE THAT THE OLD DUTY NO LONGER APPLIES + LEGIBLE EVIDENCE OF WHAT ACTION LAW APPLIES NEXT`

Visual & Mechanism therefore must not solve closure by placing another message on top of the machine. A new “clear / done / safe” signal can itself become a new attention target and can separate the player's understanding from the reel evidence that actually resolved the question.

The narrower question is:

**Can the machine make a responsibility disappear by preserving the evidence that killed it, while simultaneously restoring the ordinary visual/physical grammar that tells the hand what to do next?**

---

# 2. Prior unknown

Prior Visual runs established:

- the reel aperture is the primary precision/evidence surface;
- stopped reels act as external working memory;
- branch cues should change the status of an existing evidence surface before adding a second evidence surface;
- hand plane and gaze/evidence plane should remain distinct;
- responsibility reopening must occur before the next consequential STOP;
- stable cabinet geometry can support changing responsibility without changing the motor map.

Still unresolved:

1. what must remain visible after STOP so that closure can be inferred rather than announced;
2. whether a brief finger occlusion can be tolerated without breaking ownership of the stopped result;
3. whether state transition should erase the just-resolved evidence immediately or allow a short perceptual handoff;
4. how the next ordinary action law becomes legible without a new overlay;
5. which visual changes indicate `question closed` versus merely `effect ended`.

# 3. Investigation

## 3.1 Closed real-machine anchor — 新ハナビ keeps the reel work surface physically stable

### SOURCED FACT
Universal Entertainment's official 新ハナビ page identifies the machine as an Archer-cabinet bonus+RT machine. It explicitly presents changed reel arrangement, changed blackout rules, a mini-LCD `たまやチャレンジ`, and REG technical intervention as additions while inheriting the 5号機ハナビ game flow.

Sources:
- https://www.universal-777.com/product/slot/shin_hanabi/
- https://www.universal-777.com/product/a_project/2021/

### DIRECT OBSERVATION
Front and oblique real-machine photography used in completed Case Study 02 shows a stable body grammar across normal play and reward states:

- upper identity/illumination mass;
- bounded recessed three-reel aperture;
- compact secondary display/information band;
- projecting operation deck;
- fixed horizontal three-STOP row;
- broad lower identity panel.

The cabinet does not move the STOP row or relocate the reel aperture when the player's temporary duty changes.

### INTERPRETATION
A stable spatial map gives closure somewhere to land. The player can stop monitoring a temporary rule without also needing to relearn where the reel result, next STOP or ordinary control grammar lives.

`CLOSURE IS EASIER WHEN THE MACHINE RETURNS MEANING TO A STABLE PLACE.`

### UNRESOLVED
Exact 新ハナビ reel-window depth, control travel/force, surface chemistry, optical diffusion, glare, speaker topology and lamp electronics remain unverified without physical measurement.

## 3.2 Closure evidence should persist at the surface that resolved the question

### SOURCED FACT
Published 新ハナビ strategy material describes normal play in which the first stopped reel classifies whether an ice-covering duty remains. Published blackout rules also change the candidate set across STOP progression. These are evidence relations, not merely decorative effects.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/
- https://pachiseven.jp/articles/detail/14698

### INTERPRETATION
If STOP1 eliminates a role candidate, the stopped geometry itself is the evidence that the old aiming duty ended. Erasing, dimming, covering or visually demoting that stopped result immediately after the decision would force the player to hold the conclusion internally rather than letting the machine continue to carry it.

The closure surface should therefore obey:

`RESOLUTION EVIDENCE SHOULD OUTLIVE THE MOMENT OF RESOLUTION LONG ENOUGH TO SUPPORT THE NEXT ACTION.`

No duration threshold is asserted. This is a temporal-order principle only.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE
- `THE SURFACE THAT CLOSES THE QUESTION SHOULD REMAIN LEGIBLE UNTIL THE NEXT GRAMMAR IS SECURE.`
- `DO NOT ERASE PROOF MERELY BECAUSE THE INTERNAL FLAG HAS ADVANCED.`
- `CLOSURE EVIDENCE IS PART OF THE NEXT ACTION CONTEXT.`

## 3.3 Finger occlusion is tolerable when ownership survives it

### GENERAL PERCEPTION EVIDENCE
Object-persistence research shows that visual systems use spatiotemporal continuity and surface/context information to maintain correspondence across brief occlusion. Object-file research also reports that coherent, bounded spatial organization helps objects remain represented as the same entities across time/disruption.

Sources:
- Mitroff & Alvarez, “Space and time, not surface features, guide object persistence” — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18229497/
- Hein & Moore et al., “Object Correspondence across Brief Occlusion...” — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2764005/
- Mitroff, Arita & Fleck, “Staying in bounds: Contextual constraints on object-file coherence” — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19498955/

These studies are not pachislot studies and no laboratory threshold is imported.

### INTERPRETATION
A finger briefly crossing the operation area need not destroy machine continuity if:

- the reel aperture remains spatially fixed;
- the stopped reel does not jump, relayout or change scale;
- the STOP button remains owned by the same physical-looking operation deck;
- the finger covers the action surface more than the evidence surface;
- after release, the same stopped evidence reappears in the same place with no unrelated modal replacement.

The relevant Visual/Mechanism target is not “never allow occlusion.” It is:

`PRESERVE OBJECT AND SURFACE OWNERSHIP ACROSS OCCLUSION.`

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE
- `OCCLUSION MAY INTERRUPT VISIBILITY; IT SHOULD NOT INTERRUPT OWNERSHIP.`
- `LET THE HAND CROSS THE CONTROL PLANE WITHOUT REASSIGNING THE EVIDENCE PLANE.`
- `SPATIAL CONTINUITY IS A CLOSURE RESOURCE.`

### MISUSE WARNING
Do not convert this into a claim that any occlusion is harmless. Large, long, repeated or semantically competing occlusion remains untested and may increase memory cost.

## 3.4 A closure effect is not the same as closure evidence

### DIRECT OBSERVATION / SOURCE-BOUNDED ANCHOR
新ハナビ uses flashes, blackouts, mini-LCD information and RT task cues around a stable reel/STOP layout. Completed Case Study 02 already established that presentation priority can change temporarily while the physical work surface remains fixed.

Sources:
- https://www.universal-777.com/product/slot/shin_hanabi/
- https://pachiseven.jp/articles/detail/14698
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3869/

### INTERPRETATION
A flash ending only proves that the flash ended. A lamp returning to its baseline only proves that the lamp state returned. Neither necessarily proves that the old reel duty is over.

Closure evidence must answer at least one of these:

1. `candidate exhausted`;
2. `role fulfilled`;
3. `role denied / contradiction established`;
4. `state transition invalidated the old action law`;
5. `bounded skill attempt completed`;
6. `ordinary next-action grammar has resumed`.

Therefore:

`EFFECT TERMINATION != RESPONSIBILITY TERMINATION.`

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE
- `DO NOT USE VISUAL QUIETING AS A SUBSTITUTE FOR LOGICAL CLOSURE.`
- `A RETURN TO REST SHOULD CONFIRM A CLOSED QUESTION, NOT BE THE ONLY REASON THE PLAYER BELIEVES IT CLOSED.`

## 3.5 The next grammar should be legible from restored invariants

### SOURCED FACT
Published 新ハナビ procedures use the same basic lever and three STOP controls across normal play, BIG-derived RT and technical-intervention contexts, while the temporary stopping responsibility changes by state/cue.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3869/
- https://www.universal-777.com/product/slot/shin_hanabi/

### INTERPRETATION
The machine need not announce “ordinary mode restored” if ordinary mode has strong invariants:

- same reel window;
- same normal illumination hierarchy;
- same operation deck;
- same STOP ownership;
- absence of the temporary task cue;
- ordinary reel evidence once again sufficient to choose the next action.

This is not “absence means everything.” Absence works only after the baseline has been learned and only when the old responsibility has already been logically resolved.

`RETURN GRAMMAR = CLOSED OLD DUTY + RESTORED BASELINE INVARIANTS + LEGIBLE NEXT ACTION.`

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE
- `RESTORE THE GRAMMAR, NOT AN “ALL CLEAR” MESSAGE.`
- `THE NEXT ROUTINE SHOULD BE RECOGNIZABLE FROM INVARIANTS THE PLAYER ALREADY OWNS.`
- `BASELINE RETURN IS STRONGEST WHEN IT REDUCES CHECKS RATHER THAN ADDS ONE.`

## 3.6 One duty can close while another remains visually alive

### SOURCED / EXISTING CASE EVIDENCE
Game & Reel Run 7 already established from closed 新ハナビ evidence that role denial can end a small-role duty while opening a reach-eye / bonus-inference duty.

### INTERPRETATION
Visual closure must therefore be local, not global.

A stopped reel may simultaneously say:

- `you no longer need to cover this small role`, and
- `this contradiction is now worth reading as bonus evidence`.

A global “normal / clear” visual reset at that point would be actively misleading because it would close too much.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE
- `VISUAL CLOSURE MUST BE RESPONSIBILITY-SPECIFIC.`
- `DO NOT FLATTEN A LOCAL RESOLUTION INTO GLOBAL CALM.`
- `THE SAME STOPPED OBJECT MAY CLOSE ONE QUESTION AND OPEN ANOTHER.`

---

## 3.7 State transition should hand off, not hard-cut, the player's model

### SOURCED FACT
新ハナビ's BIG → 花火チャレンジ → 花火GAME structure gives bounded state-specific procedures while retaining the same cabinet and core controls. Official/major strategy sources document that the task changes during RT without changing the physical control map.

Sources:
- https://www.universal-777.com/product/slot/shin_hanabi/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3869/

### GENERAL PERCEPTION EVIDENCE
Research on object continuity across brief occlusion and perceptual persistence indicates that spatiotemporal/context continuity supports tracking the same object across interruption. Again, no numerical threshold is imported to pachislot.

Sources:
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2764005/
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15973783/

### INTERPRETATION
A visual state transition should avoid making the reel/control system appear replaced by a new interface when only the action rule changed. The player should be able to perceive:

`same machine / same controls / changed temporary responsibility`.

When that responsibility closes, the inverse handoff should be possible:

`same machine / same controls / temporary rule no longer active / baseline rule available again`.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE
- `TRANSITION THE RULE BEFORE YOU TRANSFORM THE BODY.`
- `STATE CHANGE MAY CHANGE SURFACE PRIORITY WITHOUT BREAKING SURFACE IDENTITY.`
- `A CLEAN RETURN PRESERVES THE OBJECT MODEL THE PLAYER BUILT BEFORE THE SPECIAL STATE.`

## 3.8 Hall identity and closure use different visual budgets

### SOURCED FACT / DIRECT OBSERVATION
Official and real-machine photography show strong static identity masses and illuminated/translucent-looking zones surrounding a bounded central reel work surface. Universal identifies the cabinet as Archer. The official page emphasizes the mini-LCD and changed reel/blackout systems while the physical reel area remains central.

Sources:
- https://www.universal-777.com/product/slot/shin_hanabi/
- https://slotters.jp/shinhanabi/

### MATERIAL / DEPTH INTERPRETATION
From photography only, reflective frame areas appear metallic/chrome-like and some illuminated areas appear translucent or internally lit. The reel aperture appears physically recessed relative to the front frame and operation deck. These are **interpretations from imagery**, not verified material chemistry or measured depth.

### INTERPRETATION
The machine can remain identifiable in a noisy hall through static silhouette, large upper/lower identity areas, material contrast and persistent light topology while leaving per-STOP closure to the central evidence surface.

`HALL IDENTITY CAN STAY ON; CLOSURE AUTHORITY SHOULD STAY LOCAL.`

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE
- `STATIC IDENTITY SHOULD NOT HAVE TO PULSE TO PROVE THAT A TASK ENDED.`
- `KEEP ROOM-DISTANCE IDENTITY SEPARATE FROM SEATED DECISION-SURFACE AUTHORITY.`

# 4. New learning

The key Visual/Mechanism delta is that **closure is primarily a persistence problem, not an ending-animation problem**.

A robust closure chain is:

`STOP / STATE EVIDENCE → QUESTION RESOLVED → RESOLUTION EVIDENCE PERSISTS → TEMPORARY PRIORITY EXPIRES → BASELINE INVARIANTS REASSERT → NEXT ACTION GRAMMAR BECOMES OBVIOUS`

The bad version is:

`QUESTION RESOLVED INTERNALLY → EVIDENCE DISAPPEARS → “CLEAR” EFFECT PLAYS → PLAYER MUST TRUST THE EFFECT → NEXT ACTION REQUIRES A SECOND CHECK`

Central rule:

`GOOD VISUAL CLOSURE PRESERVES THE PROOF LONG ENOUGH FOR THE NEXT GRAMMAR TO TAKE OWNERSHIP.`

---

# 5. Changed understanding

Previous model:

`temporary visual priority should expire cleanly`.

Updated model:

`temporary priority should expire only after the evidence that resolved it is stable enough for the player's next-action model to reattach to baseline.`

This changes closure from an effect-timing issue into a **surface-ownership transfer**:

`TEMPORARY RESPONSIBILITY SURFACE → RESOLUTION EVIDENCE → BASELINE OWNERSHIP`.

It also clarifies that a blank/quiet scene can still be bad closure if the player must ask, “Am I actually done?”

# 8. Remaining unknowns

- How long resolution evidence must remain visually stable before ordinary play can be re-chunked without rechecking.
- Whether abrupt luminance changes around the reel aperture can erase practical recall of a just-stopped relation even if the symbols remain physically visible.
- How much state-transition animation can occur without making the player treat the reel/control system as a new interface.
- Whether the ordinary REST visual is strong enough to signal baseline return without becoming a monitored cue itself.
- Exact 新ハナビ physical dimensions, reel-window depth, panel angles, lamp topology, material chemistry, glare and control mechanics.
- Human differences between novice and expert closure: experts may infer candidate exhaustion earlier, but may also detect a newly opened inference duty that novices miss.
