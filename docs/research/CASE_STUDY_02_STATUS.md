## Shared questions

1. Why can normal play itself remain the product without CZ/AT milestones?
2. How do stop position, slip, role denial and reach-eye knowledge change the meaning of the same three STOPs?
3. How does technical intervention create agency without becoming only a punishment for weaker players?
4. How do BIG/REG and RT change hand/eye/ear behavior, not merely payout?
5. How does sparse visual/audio presentation preserve sensitivity to small deviations?
6. What sustains 500G without a hit, and what fails under that dry-spell test?
7. Does three-hour mastery deepen perception, or does repeated aiming become fatigue?
8. What is inherited from older HANABI lineage, what was deliberately changed for the 6号機 product, and which lessons are general rather than nostalgia/IP dependent?
