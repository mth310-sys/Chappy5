## 1. Long-session reality makes baseline audio the dominant exposure

### FIELD EVIDENCE

Public hall-result datasets show individual 新ハナビ machines receiving several-thousand-game sessions, including examples around 5,000–8,000G. These datasets do not identify a single player's uninterrupted session and cannot prove subjective fatigue, but they establish that the product is realistically consumed at repetition counts where ordinary BET/lever/reel/STOP/result sounds massively outnumber rare cues.

Examples:
- https://slotoshihall.com/5-7%E6%97%A5-%E3%82%A2%E3%83%97%E3%83%AD7%E7%B5%90%E6%9E%9C%E3%81%BE%E3%81%A8%E3%82%81/
- https://slotoshihall.com/8-3%E5%9C%9F-%E3%83%97%E3%83%A9%E3%82%B63%E7%B5%90%E6%9E%9C%E3%81%BE%E3%81%A8%E3%82%81/
- https://slotoshihall.com/4-26%E6%B0%B4-boom%E5%A4%A9%E7%A5%9E%E6%9C%AC%E5%BA%97%E7%B5%90%E6%9E%9C%E3%81%BE%E3%81%A8%E3%82%81/

### INTERPRETATION

For a reel-centered machine, the acoustically important asset is not the rare reward cue but the thousands of ordinary action confirmations surrounding it. A baseline sound can be individually pleasant yet become irritating through repetition if attack, high-frequency edge, duration or tail is too aggressive.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`EXPOSURE WEIGHT > EVENT PRESTIGE`.

Sound QA should weight an event by expected repetition count, not by dramatic importance. A cue heard thousands of times deserves stricter fatigue review than a premium cue heard once.

## 2. Start-sound delay is information because normal timing is trusted

### SOURCED FACT

Major strategy documentation defines one of 新ハナビ's four principal normal presentations as `リール始動音遅れ`; it indicates cherry or bonus possibility. The same documentation separates delay, blackout, flash and たまやチャレンジ as distinct normal-game presentation families.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/

### INTERPRETATION

Run 1 established `REST AUDIO = SOUND IDENTITY + EXPECTED TIMING`. Run 2 sharpens this: the player must first internalize a stable timing contract before a delayed sound becomes legible. The delay is therefore not merely an effect; it spends accumulated trust in the ordinary loop.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`DEVIATION SPENDS BASELINE TRUST`.

Every special delay, omission or timing displacement consumes the credibility of the normal timing pattern. If exceptions become too frequent or inconsistent, their information value decays.

## 3. Flash cancellation is also an audio-fatigue clue

### SOURCED FACT

新ハナビ's normal flash is tied to payout/result timing, and the machine permits flash cancellation.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/

### INTERPRETATION

This matters beyond visual tempo. If the player can already resolve the result from reels, forcing the full audiovisual tail every time would add dead exposure without adding information. A cancellable presentation protects experienced-player rhythm and reduces repeated sensory tail.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLES

- `INFORMATION COMPLETION SHOULD ALLOW PRESENTATION COMPLETION`.
- `DO NOT REPEAT A CONFIRMATION AFTER THE PLAYER ALREADY KNOWS`.

Sound tails should be evaluated by whether they still carry unresolved information, reward sustain or necessary state feedback.

## 4. RT BGM has three jobs: mark, sustain, become interruptible information

### SOURCED FACT

花火チャレンジ is a maximum-20G RT after BB, extended by JAC-IN avoidance. 花火GAME is a 20G RT after JAC IN. Both strategy pages explicitly state `BGM停止でボーナス`.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/64/
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/65/

The official soundtrack separately lists music for red-7 BB, Don BB, 花火チャレンジ, 花火GAME, RB, and an RB high-skill-completion jingle.

Official source:
- https://www.universal-777.com/fun/music/shin_hanabi/

### INTERPRETATION

State BGM is not only atmosphere. It performs three different tasks:
1. `ENTRY MARKER` — tells the ear that the rules/context changed.
2. `SUSTAIN BED` — maintains state identity while lever/STOP grammar continues.
3. `EXPECTED CONTINUITY` — because it normally persists, its disappearance can become a truthful bonus cue.

The third role is impossible unless the second role is sufficiently stable.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`A LOOP CAN BE BOTH MUSIC AND MEASUREMENT BASELINE`.

When state music also functions as a sensor through interruption, its continuity rules are gameplay rules and must be specified as strictly as a reel/LED condition.

## 5. Reward escalation can occur by role reassignment, not continuous loudness growth

### SOURCED FACT

BB, 花火チャレンジ and 花火GAME have distinct sustained audio identities in official soundtrack material, while the physical lever/reel/STOP interaction continues across these phases.

Source:
- https://www.universal-777.com/fun/music/shin_hanabi/

### INTERPRETATION

Compared with an AT ladder, 新ハナビ does not need every successive reward phase to become acoustically larger. The same controls are reframed by different state context and responsibilities. Audio differentiation can therefore come from phase identity, rhythm, continuity and interruption law rather than an endless volume/complexity climb.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`ESCALATE CONTEXT BEFORE ESCALATING INTENSITY`.

A new state should first justify a different listening mode. Louder, denser and longer should not be automatic consequences of being later in the reward chain.

## 6. Technical intervention feedback must distinguish execution from outcome

### SOURCED FACT

新ハナビ REG supports multiple skill procedures. Strategy data documents different payout results depending on procedure and execution, while the official soundtrack includes a dedicated high-skill-completion jingle.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/4/
- https://www.universal-777.com/fun/music/shin_hanabi/

### INTERPRETATION

A technical-intervention game contains at least four different feedback meanings:
- input was accepted;
- aimed execution succeeded or failed;
- the economic payout was determined;
- a streak/mastery milestone was achieved.

Collapsing them into one triumphant or one punitive sound would blur agency. Especially on failure, an exaggerated negative cue can turn a learnable miss into social/psychological punishment.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`FEEDBACK GRANULARITY SHOULD MATCH AGENCY GRANULARITY`.

The more responsibility the player has, the more carefully sound should separate action acknowledgement, execution judgement and reward consequence.

## 7. Sparse presentation creates acoustic headroom for reel reading

### SOURCED FACT

Published normal presentation families are limited and rule-linked: start-sound delay, blackout, flash, and たまやチャレンジ. They do not describe a continuous voice/cinematic narration layer over ordinary play.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/

### INTERPRETATION

Sparse presentation is not merely aesthetic restraint. It leaves auditory headroom for:
- physical reel motion;
- STOP response timing;
- payout/replay resolution;
- small deviations such as delay;
- the player's own expectation formation.

If every ordinary game carried narration or dense music, reel relations could still exist mathematically but become less perceptually central.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`ACOUSTIC HEADROOM IS GAMEPLAY SPACE`.

Unused sound space can be intentionally functional. Silence/low density is not missing content when the player is actively reading reels.

## 8. Memorable versus fatiguing: frequency and semantic precision matter more than spectacle

### INTERPRETATION

For 新ハナビ, a useful distinction is:

`MEMORABLE = LOW FREQUENCY × HIGH SEMANTIC PRECISION × SYNCHRONY × PLAYER DISCOVERY`

versus

`FATIGUING = HIGH FREQUENCY × SHARP ATTENTION CLAIM × LOW NEW INFORMATION`.

The exact acoustic values are not measured here. This is a reusable analytical model, not a claim about proprietary mix settings.

A delayed start sound, meaningful BGM stop or skill-completion acknowledgement can remain salient because it is rule-specific. Conversely, even a short pleasant confirmation can fatigue if it forcefully claims attention every few seconds while telling the player nothing new.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`ATTENTION CLAIM MUST EARN ITS REPETITION RATE`.

For every recurrent Audio Event, future QA should ask:
- how often can this occur in 100G / 500G / 3h?
- does it change player action or interpretation?
- is its semantic content new at that repetition?
- can its tail be shortened/cancelled?
- does it mask reel/result information?

## 9. Cross-discipline connection to Visual Run 2

Visual Run 2 added `PERIPHERAL ENERGY, CENTRAL PRECISION`, `HAND PLANE != GAZE PLANE`, `NOT EVERY LAMP NEEDS TO BE A WORD`, and the idea that physical complexity need not become perceptual complexity every game.

Sound equivalent:

- `PERIPHERAL ENERGY, CENTRAL PRECISION` → reward/state music may create broad atmosphere while short rule cues preserve precise timing around reels.
- `HAND PLANE != GAZE PLANE` → operation confirmation should support hand rhythm without demanding gaze or narrative attention.
- `NOT EVERY LAMP NEEDS TO BE A WORD` → not every sound layer needs semantic urgency; ambience/state bed and rule-bearing cues should be distinguishable.
- physical complexity without perceptual overload → multi-speaker capability, if present, should not imply that every event needs maximum spatial/low-frequency occupation.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

`NOT EVERY SOUND NEEDS TO BE A MESSAGE`.

Separate:
1. body-rhythm confirmation,
2. state atmosphere,
3. rule-bearing attention cue,
4. reward release.

If all four demand equal conscious attention, the hierarchy collapses.

## 10. What long-form online evidence still cannot establish

### UNRESOLVED

This run did not obtain clean, measurement-grade uninterrupted audio capture suitable for objective fatigue conclusions. Online footage is affected by hall noise, camera AGC/compression, commentary, microphone position, machine-volume setting and playback device.

Therefore the following remain unresolved:
- exact BET/lever/reel/STOP/payout/replay timbre;
- SPL and relative mix hierarchy;
- frequency balance and transient sharpness;
- speaker routing/topology and subwoofer behavior;
- cabinet vibration/low-frequency transfer;
- precise delay duration in milliseconds;
- exact BGM loop length/crossfade behavior;
- measured 3-hour annoyance or habituation thresholds;
- whether novice players reliably notice `遅れ` without prior training.

These must not be guessed from compressed video.
