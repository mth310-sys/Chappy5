## 1. Reel-start delay is meaningful because START normally owns a trusted timestamp

### SOURCED FACT

Major strategy documentation identifies `リール始動音遅れ` as one of the principal normal-play presentation systems. When it occurs, it points to cherry or bonus possibilities.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3870/

### INTERPRETATION

The important property is not merely the existence of a special sound. The player has learned an expected relationship:

`lever/start action → normal reel-start sound/timing → reel motion`

The delay edits the timing relationship before any extra presentation has to dominate the screen.

Reusable principles:

- `A TRUSTED BASELINE TIMESTAMP MAKES SMALL TIMING ERRORS LEGIBLE AS INFORMATION.`
- `TIMING DEVIATION CAN CLAIM ATTENTION WITHOUT ADDING A NEW SOUND LAYER.`
- `AUDIO MEANING CAN COME FROM WHEN A SOUND OCCURS, NOT ONLY FROM WHAT SOUND OCCURS.`

Exact delayed-start milliseconds remain `UNRESOLVED`; no timing table is invented.

## 2. Visual Run 5 implies a sound sequence: audio should follow the player’s unresolved question

Visual Run 5 established:

`READ → STOP → MODIFY CONTEXT → READ AGAIN → STOP → MODIFY CONTEXT → RESOLVE`

### SOURCED FACT

Normal play documents blackout and post-stop flash as rule-bearing presentation, while the reel-start delay acts earlier at START. Flash occurs after the stop sequence / payout relationship and can be cancelled.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/

### INTERPRETATION

Sound therefore has different legal attention windows:

`START WINDOW → STOP-SEQUENCE WINDOW → POST-RESULT WINDOW`

A sound that is useful before STOP1 can be intrusive after STOP3, and a celebratory or interpretive sound that is harmless after the physical result may be destructive if it covers the player’s remaining stop responsibility.

Reusable principles:

- `AUDIO IMPORTANCE = SIGNAL CONTENT × TIMING WITHIN THE PLAYER'S CURRENT QUESTION.`
- `DO NOT USE POST-RESULT AUDIO TO OCCUPY PRE-RESULT ATTENTION.`
- `THE SAME SOUND DENSITY CAN FEEL LIGHT OR HEAVY DEPENDING ON WHEN IT CLAIMS THE PLAYER.`

This is an interpretation of reusable timing architecture, not a claim about proprietary manufacturer intent.

## 3. The soundscape should allow responsibility to close mid-game, not only escalate

### SOURCED FACT

RT play is explicitly conditional:
- 花火チャレンジ: a warning sound can require reverse-order stopping / aiming; JAC IN uses warning sound + normal-order navigation; flash calls for normal small-role handling; otherwise free stopping is acceptable.
- 花火GAME: flash / warning sound raises small-role handling duty; otherwise free stopping is acceptable.

Sources:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3869/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3873/

### INTERPRETATION

Audio does not only say “pay more attention.” Its absence after a known branch can also tell the player “no additional special listening duty is open.”

That means mastery includes learning the negative space of the sound system.

Reusable principles:

- `AUDIO SHOULD BE ABLE TO CLOSE RESPONSIBILITY, NOT ONLY OPEN IT.`
- `LEARNABLE ABSENCE CAN BE AN INFORMATION STATE.`
- `MASTERY INCLUDES KNOWING WHICH SOUNDS DO NOT NEED TO ARRIVE.`
- `A DEEP SOUND SYSTEM SHOULD HELP EXPERTS LISTEN LESS OFTEN, BUT MORE PRECISELY.`

This connects directly to Game & Reel Run 4's finding that the reels should tell the player when it is safe to pay less attention.

## 4. REG demonstrates a difference between execution confirmation and punishment audio

### SOURCED FACT

Published REG documentation states:
- beginner / intermediate / advanced technical-intervention routes exist;
- when advanced/intermediate technical aiming succeeds, a sound occurs;
- successful execution is also shown visually;
- payout differs with success/failure;
- advanced all-success play can achieve the maximum REG acquisition target.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3989/

### INTERPRETATION

This provides a clean three-layer model:

`player action → local success confirmation → economic consequence`

A success cue is useful because it confirms agency immediately. But failure already has a payout consequence, so adding a large negative sound would create redundant punishment unless it adds corrective information.

Reusable principles:

- `EXECUTION CONFIRMATION SHOULD ARRIVE CLOSE TO THE ACTION IT VALIDATES.`
- `LOCAL SUCCESS AUDIO SHOULD CONFIRM CONTROL, NOT OVERSHADOW THE NEXT REQUIRED INPUT.`
- `WHEN THE GAME ALREADY PRICES FAILURE, AUDIO NEED NOT PRICE IT AGAIN.`
- `FEEDBACK SHOULD DISTINGUISH CORRECTION FROM HUMILIATION.`

Exact miss-audio behavior remains `UNRESOLVED`.

## 5. RT BGM is a continuous reference surface; its interruption can carry more information than a new stinger

### SOURCED FACT

花火GAME is a fixed 20G RT and major strategy sources explicitly state `BGM停止でボーナス`. Existing Case Study 02 evidence records the same interruption role for 花火チャレンジ.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/64/
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/65/

### INTERPRETATION

The BGM has two simultaneous jobs:

1. state continuity;
2. a predictable reference whose disappearance becomes anomalous.

This is stronger than treating music as decoration because the player learns its persistence as a rule.

Reusable principles:

- `CONTINUITY CAN BE AN INFORMATION CHANNEL.`
- `INTERRUPTION CAN OUTPERFORM ADDITION WHEN THE BASELINE IS TRUSTED.`
- `A STATE BED SHOULD BE STABLE ENOUGH THAT ITS ABSENCE IS IMMEDIATELY LEGIBLE.`
- `SOUND SYSTEMS CAN SAVE HEADROOM BY MAKING CONTINUITY MEANINGFUL.`

No protected track or arrangement is reproduced.

## 6. Sparse presentation works by separating three audio layers

### INTERPRETATION

Run 5 formalizes three distinct layers:

1. **Motor / routine confirmation layer** — BET, lever/start, reel motion, STOP, settling, payout/replay confirmation.
2. **Rule / attention layer** — delay, warning sound, temporary cue, interruption.
3. **Reward / phase layer** — BONUS / RT music and state transitions.

If these layers are all equally salient, the player cannot tell which sound requires action.

Reusable principles:

- `ROUTINE AUDIO, ATTENTION AUDIO AND REWARD AUDIO SHOULD NOT COMPETE AT THE SAME SEMANTIC LEVEL.`
- `THE PLAYER SHOULD BE ABLE TO HEAR WHETHER A SOUND CONFIRMS, INSTRUCTS OR CELEBRATES.`
- `SPARSE PRESENTATION IS NOT FEWER SOUNDS; IT IS FEWER SIMULTANEOUS SEMANTIC CLAIMS.`

This is especially important in a reel-first machine because reel discrimination and stop timing already consume visual/motor bandwidth.

## 7. Post-STOP3 is a natural audio punctuation point

### SOURCED FACT

Published normal-play documentation places flash interpretation after the stop sequence / payout relationship. Visual Run 5 identifies STOP3 as a natural visual punctuation point because the physical result is complete.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_shinhanabi/

### INTERPRETATION

After STOP3, the machine can safely spend more interpretive audio energy because the player no longer needs to preserve attention for an unresolved stop.

Reusable principles:

- `AFTER THE PRIMARY PHYSICAL RESULT IS COMPLETE, AUDIO MAY INTERPRET IT MORE AGGRESSIVELY.`
- `RESULT AUDIO SHOULD FOLLOW RESULT CERTAINTY.`
- `THE STRONGEST SOUND NEED NOT BE THE EARLIEST SOUND.`

This does not imply copying any HANABI flash/sound synchronization table.

## 8. Long-session fatigue: false-listening duty is driven by ambiguous required-action cues

### SOURCED FACT

RT documentation explicitly distinguishes warning/flash cases from “otherwise free stopping.” REG similarly distinguishes games with/without warning sound and different required procedures.

Sources:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3869/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/134/3989/

### INTERPRETATION

The long-session risk is not simply loudness. It is the cost of repeatedly wondering whether an ordinary sound has opened a mandatory task.

Run 4 model:

`FALSE LISTENING DUTY = FREQUENT AMBIGUOUS CUES × COST OF MISSING THEM`

Run 5 extends it:

`AUDITORY DUTY LOAD ≈ REQUIRED CUE DETECTION + CUE DISAMBIGUATION + RE-ORIENTATION COST`

Reusable principles:

- `A HIGH-FREQUENCY CUE SHOULD HAVE LOW DISAMBIGUATION COST.`
- `MANDATORY-ACTION CUES SHOULD BE MORE SEMANTICALLY DISTINCT THAN OPTIONAL-CURIOSITY CUES.`
- `LONG-SESSION AUDIO FATIGUE CAN COME FROM DECODING, NOT ONLY VOLUME.`
- `AN EXPERT SHOULD DEVELOP SELECTIVE DEAFNESS TO SAFE ROUTINE, NOT PERMANENT VIGILANCE.`

This is a QA model, not a measured human-factors claim about exact 新ハナビ fatigue.

Current authoritative online material is sufficient for event roles but not for exact driver count, crossover, enclosure path, SPL, cabinet transfer or seated low-frequency behavior.

### INTERPRETATION

Long-form/compressed video cannot safely establish the physical mix because hall noise, camera AGC, microphone placement and upload compression alter perceived balance.

Reusable principle:

`ONLINE VIDEO CAN SUPPORT EVENT-TIMING OBSERVATION; IT CANNOT VERIFY PHYSICAL MIX VALUES.`
