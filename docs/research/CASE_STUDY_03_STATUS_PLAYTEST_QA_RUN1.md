## Main QA findings

- beginner onboarding burden is learning **when extra aiming duty opens**, not only where BAR is;
- a 100G ordinary loop is best modeled as `routine → rare-role question → deferred consequence → closure → return home`;
- 500G dry-play quality must be separated from relief when a later hit finally arrives;
- BIG must retain local completion value even when ART does not follow;
- the three BIG notification modes are different **certainty schedules**, not merely cosmetic choices;
- CZ failure may leave system memory, but player duty needs a clear release back to normal;
- base ART must be tested without Magica Quest/Walpurgis highlights to prove that the persistent state itself is sustainable;
- `ART → BIG → ART` needs low re-orientation cost and a clear return address;
- experienced play shifts load from motor aiming toward contextual/statistical inference;
- three-hour fatigue risk is especially `context-switch + semantic carryover + setting-inference + concurrency` rather than constant difficult eye skill;
- reward peaks and quality of the road to them must be scored separately.

## New QA metrics

- `CONSEQUENCE CLOSURE LATENCY`
- `RELIEF DEPENDENCE RATIO`
- `BIG-ONLY SATISFACTION`
- `CERTAINTY SCHEDULE FIT`
- `HOME RECOGNITION LATENCY`
- `BASE-ART INDEPENDENCE`
- `RE-ANCHOR LATENCY`
- `CONCURRENT CLAIM COUNT`
- separate `INTRINSIC PRE-HIT PLAY VALUE` from `RELIEF VALUE`

## Key reusable principles

- `ONBOARDING SHOULD TEACH THE TRIGGER FOR EXTRA DUTY, NOT MAKE EVERY GAME FEEL LIKE EXTRA DUTY.`
- `A HIT AFTER A DRY SPELL MUST NOT BE USED AS RETROACTIVE PROOF THAT THE DRY SPELL WAS GOOD.`
- `IN A PARALLEL-REWARD MACHINE, A REAL BONUS MUST RETAIN LOCAL COMPLETION VALUE EVEN WHEN IT DOES NOT OPEN THE PERSISTENT STATE.`
- `THE PERSISTENT STATE MUST BE TESTED WITHOUT ITS HIGHLIGHT SUBSTATES.`
- `A TEMPORARY REWARD INTERRUPTION SHOULD RETURN THE PLAYER TO THE PERSISTENT STATE WITH MINIMAL ORIENTATION COST.`
- `MASTERY CAN MOVE LOAD FROM THE HANDS TO THE MODEL IN THE PLAYER'S HEAD.`
- `FATIGUE CAN COME FROM TOO MANY SMALL OPEN QUESTIONS, NOT ONLY FROM HIGH PRESENTATION INTENSITY.`
- `REWARD PEAKS AND THE QUALITY OF THE ROAD TO THEM ARE SEPARATE VARIABLES.`
- `THE MACHINE MUST LET THE PLAYER RETURN TO A TRUSTED HOME ROUTINE AFTER EACH TEMPORARY CONTEXT, OR PARALLEL DEPTH TURNS INTO PARALLEL FATIGUE.`
