## Purpose

Run 1 established reel-first visual hierarchy and restrained presentation. Run 2 established depth allocation, hand/gaze plane separation, peripheral energy and two-resolution cabinet recognition. Run 3 established stable REST hierarchy, protected reel work-surface, temporary task priority and attention release.

Run 4 does **not** design a Chappy5 cabinet and does not reproduce protected HANABI artwork, exact ornament geometry, flash sequences, logos, panel graphics, symbols or proprietary lamp programming.

This pass asks a narrower physical-visual question:

**How does the cabinet make temporary information easy to notice while keeping the physical distance back to the three reels short, and how do dark voids / bright surfaces / side illumination frame rather than invade the precision reel task?**

The study therefore concentrates on:

- gaze travel between reels, information band, temporary instruction and controls;
- bilateral symmetry and dark acoustic/structural voids as visual framing;
- luminance topology around the reel aperture;
- operation-deck projection as a near-hand plane without becoming the primary gaze plane;
- oblique-view evidence of front-shell depth versus rear cabinet box;
- stable silhouette across normal / bonus / RT despite changing state information;
- what remains unresolved without controlled physical measurement.

## 1. Gaze return distance is physically compressed

### Direct observation

In front photographs, the main repeated-play surfaces occupy a vertically compact central stack:

`upper identity / context → physical reels → narrow information / counter / mini-display band → operation deck → STOP row`

The mini information surface is immediately below the reels rather than displaced to a distant upper-screen zone. The STOP row sits directly below the deck and within the same central body width.

### Interpretation

When a temporary cue appears below the reels, the player does not need to make a large visual relocation before returning to symbol discrimination. Likewise, the hand can operate the STOP row beneath the reel field while gaze remains near the reel aperture.

This extends `HAND PLANE != GAZE PLANE` into a recovery principle:

`ATTENTION CLAIMS SHOULD HAVE A SHORT RETURN PATH TO THE PRIMARY PRECISION SURFACE.`

This does not assert exact eye-tracking distances. It is a spatial interpretation from photographed adjacency only.

Reusable principle:

`GREAT SECONDARY INFORMATION IS NOT ONLY SMALL; IT IS EASY TO LEAVE.`

A secondary surface can be meaningful without becoming sticky if the geometry makes the primary task the natural place for gaze to return.

---

## 2. Bilateral dark voids stabilize the center rather than competing with it

### Direct observation

The upper left and right grille cavities are large, dark and approximately bilateral. They flank the upper identity / bonus-reference region but do not occupy the central reel rectangle.

Across powered photos, these cavities often remain visually darker than the illuminated identity, reel and lower-panel surfaces.

### Interpretation

The dark apertures do at least three visual jobs independent of their acoustic function:

1. create coarse left/right symmetry at aisle distance;
2. give the saturated upper shell visible internal depth;
3. create negative-value zones that prevent the entire upper face from becoming one continuous luminous billboard.

Reusable principle:

`DARK FUNCTIONAL VOIDS CAN FRAME HIGH-INFORMATION SURFACES.`

This is not a recommendation to copy grille shape or speaker placement. It is the observation that physical function can also create useful visual negative space.

### Boundary

Exact speaker count, acoustic routing, driver placement and whether every dark cavity is acoustically active remain unresolved.

---

## 3. Luminance is topological: center precision, side energy, lower identity

### Direct observation

Powered cabinet photographs repeatedly show three different bright-region roles:

- a bright, regular reel rectangle in the center;
- tall peripheral luminous / translucent-looking side zones flanking the reel area;
- a broad illuminated lower identity panel.

These bright areas do not collapse into one undifferentiated luminous plane.

### Interpretation

The cabinet can therefore spend brightness in separate spatial jobs:

- **reel brightness** for symbol discrimination;
- **side brightness** for peripheral energy / silhouette reinforcement;
- **lower-panel brightness** for machine identity at distance.

Run 2 called this `PERIPHERAL ENERGY, CENTRAL PRECISION`. Run 4 adds a stronger boundary condition:

`HIGH LUMINANCE MAY SURROUND A PRECISION SURFACE, BUT SHOULD NOT DESTROY ITS INTERNAL CONTRAST.`

The important point is not the exact colors. It is that bright identity / ambient zones are spatially displaced from the exact symbol-reading interior.

### Unresolved

Photographs cannot establish actual luminance ratios, glare, bloom, diffuser construction, dark-adapted perception or hall-light reflections.

## 4. The reel aperture behaves like a visual chamber

### Direct observation

Oblique photography clearly shows the reel cylinder plane behind a front cover / bezel plane. The surrounding face has a visible border thickness, and the reel strip is not coplanar with the decorative front shell.

### Interpretation

The reel window reads as a **chamber** rather than an image pasted onto a flat front:

`front shell / cover plane → aperture / bezel depth → reel cylinder plane`

This physical recess does more than add realism. It can visually isolate the reel field from reflective side trim and panel graphics.

Reusable principle:

`DEPTH CAN BE USED AS INFORMATION ISOLATION.`

This extends Run 2's `DEPTH SHOULD CLARIFY INFORMATION`: a recess can create a physical boundary around the object the player must read repeatedly.

### Boundary

Exact cover-to-reel distance, reel diameter, glass/polycarbonate thickness, anti-reflection treatment and viewing-angle performance remain unresolved.

---

## 5. Front shell and rear box are visually different systems

### Direct observation

Three-quarter actual-unit photography reveals a deep, dark rectangular rear cabinet body behind the brighter sculpted red / reflective front assembly. The decorative front does not visually describe the entire machine depth; it is attached to a deeper serviceable box.

### Interpretation only

This supports a reality reading of:

`SERVICE / STRUCTURAL VOLUME BEHIND → PRESENTATION / TOUCH VOLUME IN FRONT`

The exact construction, materials and attachment method are not established by photography.

Reusable principle:

`A MACHINE CAN LOOK SCULPTED FROM THE FRONT WHILE REMAINING A DEEP SERVICEABLE BOX IN SPACE.`

This strengthens `FRONT THEATRE / BACK BOX SEPARATION` without prescribing any protected contour.

For real-machine presence, oblique thickness matters because a front elevation alone can hide the fact that displays, reels, controls, speakers and service volume occupy different depths.

---

## 6. Operation deck projection creates a hand-near / eye-far hierarchy

### Direct observation

The operation deck projects toward the player relative to the reel/front panel plane. The lever is isolated toward the player's left, while the STOP targets form a fixed horizontal row closer to the player's body.

### Interpretation

Projection lets the controls be physically easy to reach without visually moving them into the reel plane.

This creates an embodied hierarchy:

`near body = repeated action`
`farther / higher = repeated reading`

Reusable principle:

`ERGONOMIC PROMINENCE DOES NOT REQUIRE VISUAL DOMINANCE.`

A control can be physically prominent because it protrudes toward the hand while remaining visually subordinate to the information surface above it.

### Boundary

No physical measurement was made of reach distance, deck angle, button spacing, actuation force, lever travel, palm clearance or seated posture.

---

## 7. Symmetry provides orientation; asymmetry carries action

### Direct observation

At the coarse cabinet level, the upper red shoulders, dark grille cavities, reflective side framing and three-reel rectangle are strongly balanced left-to-right. At the operation level, the lever and individual controls are not simply mirrored duplicates.

### Interpretation

This suggests two simultaneous layers:

- **global symmetry** makes the cabinet easy to parse and recognizable;
- **local asymmetry** allows controls / status information to have distinct functions.

Reusable principle:

`USE COARSE SYMMETRY FOR ORIENTATION; ALLOW LOCAL ASYMMETRY FOR FUNCTION.`

The principle is abstract. No specific cabinet outline, grille pattern or control layout is reusable as protected design.

## 8. State change preserves the player's spatial map

### Sourced behavior

Normal, bonus, 花火チャレンジ and 花火GAME change rules, audio, navigation, flash / panel information and technical responsibility. The same physical reel window, operation deck, lever and three STOP positions remain in use.

### Direct observation / interpretation

The cabinet silhouette does not need to physically reconfigure for the player to experience a different state. Instead, existing surfaces change **meaning and activation**.

Run 3 established `STATE ESCALATION CAN REALLOCATE EXISTING SURFACES BEFORE ADDING NEW SURFACES.`

Run 4 adds the spatial-memory reason this matters:

`STATE CHANGE CAN BE STRONG WHILE PRESERVING THE PLAYER'S SPATIAL MAP.`

This matters especially in a technical-intervention machine because a reward state may increase the need for accurate stopping. Destroying the learned spatial map exactly when precision matters would add avoidable duty.

---

## 9. Quiet visual zones are active design resources

### Direct observation

Not every visible surface is illuminated to the same intensity in powered photographs. Dark speaker/grille regions, dark borders around the reel block and less-active portions of the information band coexist with bright reels and panels.

### Interpretation

Visual quiet is not absence of design. It creates reserve capacity for deviations.

A stronger formulation of prior principles is:

`VISUAL HEADROOM IS CREATED BY SURFACES THAT ARE ALLOWED TO STAY QUIET.`

If every region were continuously bright, animated and semantically loaded, blackout, flash, side-light change or temporary instruction would have less perceptual contrast.

This parallels Sound Run 2/3's acoustic-headroom findings without turning it into an original design rule.

---

## 10. Visual recovery is part of state communication

### Sourced behavior

RT procedure contains temporary high-duty moments (reverse-order navigation / aim, JAC-IN handling, flash-related small-role handling) and lower-duty moments where free stopping is allowed.

### Interpretation

The visual system must not only **claim** attention; it must make the end of the claim legible. Because the same reel and control surfaces remain visible throughout, return can be expressed as the disappearance / de-prioritization of temporary information rather than a full scene change.

Reusable principle:

`A TEMPORARY VISUAL DUTY NEEDS A VISIBLE RELEASE CONDITION.`

Combined with Run 3:

`TEMPORARY TASK PRIORITY SHOULD EXPIRE CLEANLY`

and Run 4:

`THE PLAYER SHOULD NOT HAVE TO SEARCH FOR THE NORMAL VIEW AFTER THE TASK ENDS.`

---

## 11. Why the machine remains identifiable in a noisy hall without giant moving mechanisms

Run 4 reinforces a three-scale explanation.

### Hall / coarse scale

- saturated upper shell mass;
- bilateral dark cavities;
- reflective / luminous side framing;
- broad lower identity panel;
- strong dark-light segmentation.

### Seated / medium scale

- central recessed reel chamber;
- compact information band immediately below it;
- projecting operation deck;
- repeated STOP row;
- stable left/right orientation.

### Moment-to-moment / fine scale

- reel illumination / blackout condition;
- flash / panel-state change;
- temporary RT navigation;
- mini-display interpretation;
- clean release to the stable reel-centered baseline.

Integrated Run 4 principle:

`IDENTITY CAN LIVE IN THE STATIC SILHOUETTE; EXCITEMENT CAN LIVE IN TEMPORARY LIGHT / INFORMATION REALLOCATION; PRECISION CAN REMAIN IN THE REELS.`

No large moving mechanism is required for these three jobs to be separated.

## 12. Fact / observation / interpretation boundary

### Sourced facts

- New Hanabi is a July 2021 Eleco 6th-generation bonus + RT machine on the `Archer` cabinet.
- Official material identifies the mini-LCD `たまやチャレンジ`, changed reel arrangement and changed blackout rules.
- RT procedure includes temporary reverse-order navigation / aiming, normal-order JAC-IN navigation, flash-related small-role handling, and low-duty free-stop games.
- 花火GAME is a fixed 20G RT and uses panel flash / warning-sound relations; mini-display events may still occur.

### Direct visual observations from actual-unit photography

- deep black rear cabinet volume is visible in oblique view behind the sculpted front;
- reel cylinder surface is recessed behind a front bezel / cover plane;
- operation deck projects toward the player;
- upper bilateral dark grille cavities are prominent;
- tall side luminous-looking regions flank the central reel area;
- broad lower illuminated identity area sits below the STOP row;
- repeated STOP targets form a compact horizontal hand plane.

### Interpretation only

- exact material family / chemistry / coating of red, reflective, transparent and translucent surfaces;
- whether side-lighting was primarily intended for identity, state communication or both;
- whether dark grille cavities were deliberately composed as negative space;
- actual eye-movement distance / gaze-return time;
- actual motor-memory performance of experienced players;
- actual glare / peripheral distraction in a hall;
- exact mechanical assembly sequence and service construction behind the photographed front shell.
