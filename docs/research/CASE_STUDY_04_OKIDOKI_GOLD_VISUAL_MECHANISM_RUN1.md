## 0. Run question

Game & Reel Run 1 established a stable low-cost motor HOME and a temporal state architecture: ordinary play can stretch toward a long horizon, while BONUS closes the local reward question and compresses the next question into 32G. This Visual & Mechanism pass asks:

**HOW DOES A MACHINE WITH A SIMPLE DIRECT-NOTIFICATION CONTRACT KEEP THE PHYSICAL REELS READABLE AND THE BODY MAP STABLE, WHILE MAKING ONE PAIR OF PHYSICAL LAMPS STRONG ENOUGH TO REORGANIZE ATTENTION AT THE MOMENT OF NOTIFICATION?**

## 1. Product identity is physically concentrated rather than screen-dominant

### SOURCED FACT

Universal Entertainment announced 沖ドキ！GOLD in October 2022 as a new Okin Doki series machine, emphasizing post-BONUS 32G continuation and the new GOLD continuation system rather than a large LCD feature stack.

Source:
- https://news.p-world.co.jp/articles/21932/nippon
- official machine-site entry: https://www.universal-777.com/ps/okidoki_gold/

### DIRECT REAL-MACHINE PHOTO OBSERVATION

Front photographs from P-WORLD and なな徹 consistently show a vertically conventional 30φ pachislot silhouette rather than a screen-first or giant-moving-mechanism silhouette. The strongest visible layers are:

`UPPER GOLD CANOPY / TOP DISPLAY AREA`
`PAIR OF FLOWER NOTIFICATION LAMPS ABOVE THE REELS`
`CENTRAL PHYSICAL 3-REEL WINDOW`
`LEFT/RIGHT VERTICAL DECORATIVE-LAMP ZONES`
`THREE STOP BUTTONS DIRECTLY BELOW THE REELS`
`LOWER CABINET/PANEL`.

The reel window occupies the central seated task area. The notification lamps are close enough to that reel area that the player does not need to redirect attention to a distant upper LCD to learn the main reward truth.

Photo references:
- P-WORLD machine database: https://www.p-world.co.jp/machine/database/9750
- なな徹 machine / field-test images: https://nana-press.com/post/1575235
- なな徹 lamp analysis: https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12854/

### INTERPRETATION

This cabinet does not achieve hall identity by continually moving a large object. Its distance identity is concentrated into a small number of persistent cues: a bright gold upper mass, strong left/right luminous edges and the paired flower-lamp face. At seated distance, however, the visual hierarchy collapses back toward `lamp + physical reels + STOP row`.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**HALL IDENTITY AND SEATED TASK IDENTITY CAN BE CONCENTRATED IN DIFFERENT SCALES OF THE SAME CABINET.**

A cabinet may be recognizable from a distance through silhouette / material field / edge lighting, while keeping the seated high-frequency task area comparatively simple.

## 2. The reel window remains the stable physical HOME

### DIRECT REAL-MACHINE PHOTO/VIDEO OBSERVATION

The three physical reels remain unobscured in normal play photographs. They sit inside a clearly bounded rectangular cavity with visible upper/lower masking, rather than being visually merged into a large animation surface. The STOP row remains directly beneath the corresponding left / center / right reel positions.

Long-form hall footage was used only for repeated-layout observation, not for exact photometry:
- しいたけTV「沖ドキGOLDホール実践」2025/11/12: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3H0INvTd60
- しいたけTV 横画面実戦 2025/4/7: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JE-oGbhNx9I

### INTERPRETATION

The physical HOME is unusually stable because the core cabinet does not reconfigure between ordinary play, BONUS expectation and the 32G post-BONUS question. The horizon changes dramatically, but the body map does not:

`LEVER → REEL WINDOW → STOP L/C/R` remains stable.

This supports the Game & Reel finding that state value can change through resolution horizon without requiring a new motor interface.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**WHEN THE INFORMATION HORIZON CHANGES, THE PLAYER DOES NOT ALSO NEED A NEW PHYSICAL HOME.**

State escalation can be made legible through timing and selective signals while preserving the same reel/STOP coordinates.

## 3. Reel-window depth/readability: what can and cannot be claimed

### SOURCED FACT — CABINET FAMILY BASELINE, NOT MACHINE-SPECIFIC CONFIRMATION

ZEEG publishes current ARCHER hardware dimensions of H810 × W475 × D432.5mm and physical reels of φ230 × 79.5mm with 168-step HB motors. It also documents multiple internal speaker units. This is useful as a general physical-cabinet reference.

Source:
- https://www.zeeg.jp/lineup/st230-ar

### UNRESOLVED / CONTRADICTION

A current authoritative source directly identifying the 2022 沖ドキ！GOLD production unit as the exact `ST230-AR / ARCHER` specification was not found in this pass. A later official 沖ドキ！BLACK page explicitly identifies BLACK as using the ARCHER cabinet, but that does **not** prove GOLD uses identical hardware. Therefore the ZEEG dimensions / speaker specification are not assigned to GOLD as confirmed machine facts.

Later-series reference only:
- https://www.universal-777.com/product/slot/okidoki_black/

### DIRECT PHOTO OBSERVATION

GOLD photographs clearly show a recessed physical reel surface behind the front trim/glass plane, with masking above/below the visible symbols and surrounding trim producing an identifiable cavity boundary.

### INTERPRETATION

Exact millimeter depth is unresolved, but the visual mechanism is still clear: the reels are read as rotating objects inside volume, not symbols printed onto the front face. The dark/quiet cavity surrounding the moving reel bands also separates reel reading from brighter gold/colored decoration.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**A REEL WINDOW NEEDS LOCAL CONTRAST AND OCCLUSION AS MUCH AS IT NEEDS DECORATION.**

Reel readability benefits from a bounded cavity whose inner plane is quieter than the outer identity surfaces.

## 4. The paired direct-notification lamps are not merely decoration

### SOURCED FACT

Major strategy documentation records 30 distinct notification-lamp lighting / flashing patterns. Those patterns can carry mode information, including default patterns, normal-B-or-better suggestions/confirmations, heaven-or-better and higher-state implications. Documented examples include left/right asymmetry, flower-only / leaf-only behavior, flow direction, speed changes, panel blackout combined with flashing, and colored-flow behavior.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12854/
- mirrored detail in https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12855/

### DIRECT PHOTO OBSERVATION

The paired lamps are placed immediately above the physical reel window and symmetrically around the centerline. Their red/orange flower faces and green leaf elements are visually distinct from the darker normal surround. In lit photos they become the strongest local sources above the reel surface while still leaving all three reels unobstructed.

### INTERPRETATION

The lamp pair has at least four simultaneous jobs:

1. `REWARD TRUTH` — direct notification;
2. `SPATIAL LANDMARK` — a permanent face above the reels;
3. `PATTERN SURFACE` — two-sided / sub-region / flow timing lets one physical object encode many states;
4. `LIGHT SOURCE` — illumination contrast changes the perceived machine state without moving the mechanism.

The notable mechanism lesson is that **complexity can live inside temporal modulation of a fixed physical object**. Thirty documented patterns do not require thirty separate screens or moving parts.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**A FIXED PHYSICAL SIGNAL CAN HAVE DEEP STATE VOCABULARY IF POSITION IS STABLE AND TEMPORAL MODULATION IS LEGIBLE.**

Do not infer that more game states require more physical mechanisms.

## 5. REST VISUAL is an active condition, not “nothing is happening”

### DIRECT REAL-MACHINE VIDEO/PHOTO OBSERVATION

Across ordinary unnotified play, the cabinet remains visually branded: gold/purple decorative fields, side illumination zones, reel illumination and stable button/operation areas remain visible. However, the paired notification lamps are not held in their full reward-signalling condition on every game.

### INTERPRETATION

The important REST contrast is not `DARK MACHINE → BRIGHT MACHINE`. It is closer to:

`IDENTITY LIGHT PRESENT + REELS READABLE + SIGNAL SURFACE QUIET`
→ `SIGNAL SURFACE CLAIMS ATTENTION`.

If the entire cabinet were visually maximized during every normal game, a fixed lamp flash would lose perceptual priority. The cabinet therefore relies on **reserved contrast**, not absolute darkness.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**REST VISUAL SHOULD RESERVE CONTRAST FOR THE TRUTH SIGNAL WITHOUT ERASING MACHINE IDENTITY.**

The quiet state can remain colorful and recognizable as long as the information-bearing surface still has unused contrast range.

## 6. Lamp language shows that “brighter” is not the only escalation axis

### SOURCED FACT

Documented lamp patterns use multiple dimensions besides gross brightness: left/right order, speed, gradual acceleration, flower-versus-leaf segmentation, directional flow, alternating behavior, color flow and simultaneous panel blackout.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12854/

### INTERPRETATION

A fixed lamp becomes readable over repeated play because the machine varies **structure**, not only intensity. A player can learn to notice `which side`, `which sub-part`, `which temporal direction`, `which rate`, and whether another panel goes dark.

This matters to long-session design. If every stronger state were expressed only by “more brightness,” the visual channel would quickly saturate.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**SIGNAL ESCALATION NEEDS ORTHOGONAL DIMENSIONS BEFORE IT NEEDS MORE LUMINANCE.**

Possible abstract dimensions are spatial asymmetry, segmentation, cadence, direction, onset timing and selective subtraction.

## 7. Selective subtraction is itself a visual mechanism

### SOURCED FACT

One documented notification pattern combines lamp flashing with panel blackout and denotes a higher state class than ordinary patterns.

Source:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12854/

### INTERPRETATION

This is important because the cabinet can strengthen a signal by **turning something else off**. The eye is not always driven by adding LEDs. Removing a normally present surrounding light can isolate the lamp and increase informational salience while preserving the same hardware.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**VISUAL EMPHASIS CAN BE CREATED BY SUBTRACTING COMPETING LIGHT, NOT ONLY BY ADDING OUTPUT.**

The baseline illumination field is therefore part of the mechanism vocabulary.

## 8. Gold identity versus reel readability

### DIRECT PHOTO OBSERVATION

Gold-colored surfaces are concentrated in upper trim / canopy / decorative panel zones and are paired with darker purple/black fields around key information surfaces. The actual moving reel bands remain bright and high-contrast against a bounded inner area.

### INTERPRETATION — MATERIAL ONLY

Photographs suggest a mixture of glossy molded trim, printed/decorative panel surfaces and translucent illuminated pieces, but photographs do not establish the exact resin, plating, paint, metal or surface-treatment stack. `Gold-colored` is observable; `metal`, `vacuum plating`, `chrome` or a specific resin process is not confirmed here.

The visual lesson is not “make everything gold.” It is **use the identity material field outside the precision-reading field**. The reel region is allowed to remain optically simpler than the surrounding brand shell.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**HIGH-IDENTITY SURFACES SHOULD FRAME, NOT OCCUPY, THE PRECISION-READING FIELD.**

A loud cabinet material language can coexist with readable reels if decorative complexity falls away at the actual task plane.

## 9. Operation deck / three STOP ergonomics

### DIRECT PHOTO OBSERVATION

The three circular STOP buttons form a simple horizontal row directly below the three reels. The lever is on the player's left side. The operation deck does not visually separate each game state into different control zones; the same motor map is reused throughout.

### INTERPRETATION

The cabinet's visual ergonomics reinforce low motor cost:
- reel-to-corresponding-STOP vertical mapping is immediate;
- the direct-notification lamp is above the same visual column, not on a remote secondary display;
- the strong visual event therefore does not demand a large hand-path change.

The player can receive a major state truth while remaining physically anchored to the next lever/reel/STOP loop.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**A MAJOR PRESENTATION EVENT SHOULD NOT AUTOMATICALLY DESTROY INPUT-LOCATION CERTAINTY.**

The stronger the reward signal, the more valuable stable hand coordinates become for rapid re-entry into repeated play.

### UNRESOLVED

Exact button diameter, center-to-center spacing, deck angle, lever travel, push force, surface texture and seated reach measurements were not located in authoritative machine-specific documentation.

## 10. BONUS / 32G: the visible body map stays stable while attention horizon contracts

### SOURCED FACT

Published strategy material documents BIG as 70G/about 210 coins, REG as 30G/about 90 coins, and the high-continuation mode family as resolving within 32G. Launch material specifically marketed heightened post-BONUS 32G continuation expectation.

Sources:
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12853/
- https://nana-press.com/kaiseki/machine/478/12848/
- https://news.p-world.co.jp/articles/21932/nippon

### FIELD REPORT / LONG-FORM OBSERVATION

A なな徹 3000G setting-6 field test and a separate game-number-target field report both show repeated rapid BONUS returns inside a chain, with the same front cabinet/reel/STOP geometry maintained rather than switching to a different physical control interface. One field report describes an 18-BONUS chain; its photographs show the same lamp/reel front continuing to carry the play.

Sources:
- https://nana-press.com/post/1575235
- https://nana-press.com/post/1578110

### INTERPRETATION

The visual transition into 32G tension is therefore unusual compared with a screen-state ladder. The player knows the **meaning of the next 32 games has changed more than the physical scene has changed**.

This produces a valuable separation:

`MOTOR HOME = STABLE`
`INFORMATION HORIZON = COMPRESSED`
`VIGILANCE = ELEVATED`
`PRIMARY TRUTH SURFACE = SAME LAMP`.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**STATE INTENSITY DOES NOT REQUIRE SCENE REPLACEMENT.**

Repeated tension can come from changing the meaning of a stable physical field, provided the player understands the new time horizon.

## 11. How the cabinet remains identifiable in a noisy hall without a giant moving mechanism

### DIRECT PHOTO OBSERVATION + INTERPRETATION

The front uses a layered recognition strategy:

- `DISTANCE`: broad gold upper field + bright side accents create a high-area silhouette/color signature;
- `MID DISTANCE`: paired flower lamps give a bilateral face-like landmark;
- `SEATED`: three reels + STOP row + lamp pair form the compact task identity;
- `EVENT`: lamp temporal pattern becomes the strongest local change.

No giant continuously moving mechanism is needed because identity is distributed across **static silhouette, luminous frame and one high-semantic signal device**.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**A CABINET CAN BE MEMORABLE THROUGH A THREE-SCALE IDENTITY STACK: SILHOUETTE → LANDMARK → EVENT SIGNAL.**

Movement is one identity tool, not a requirement.

## 12. Direct contrast with Case Study 02 新ハナビ

新ハナビ keeps visual presentation restrained so the physical reel surface can carry dense reach-eye / slip / technical meaning.

沖ドキ！GOLD also protects reel readability, but for a different reason. Its main reward certainty lives in a separate direct-notification lamp, while the reels carry motor cadence, role handling and BONUS execution.

So two visually restrained reel-centric machines can have different information economics:

`NEW HANABI: REEL SURFACE ITSELF = HIGH-DENSITY EVIDENCE`

`OKIDOKI GOLD: REEL SURFACE = STABLE MOTOR / ROLE FIELD; FIXED LAMP = PRIMARY REWARD TRUTH`.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**VISUAL RESTRAINT IS NOT A GENRE. IT MUST BE EXPLAINED BY WHAT INFORMATION THE PLAYER NEEDS TO READ.**

Do not automatically copy “quiet visuals” from one lineage into another without identifying the protected information surface.
