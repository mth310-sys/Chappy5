## Phase B integrated findings retained as translation questions only

The app cycle established useful distinctions without resolving the real machine:

- `INPUT FIDELITY ≠ RULE FIDELITY`
- `REEL IMAGE FIDELITY ≠ REEL TASK FIDELITY`
- `STRUCTURAL IMAGE FIDELITY ≠ PHYSICAL PRESENCE FIDELITY`
- `AUDIO ASSET FIDELITY ≠ ACOUSTIC EXPERIENCE FIDELITY`
- simulated games, human-operated games, human-watched games and human-heard games are different measures;
- automation changes action ownership and experienced waiting cost;
- service-wrapper UI/economy is not part of the original machine.

These are not reasons to continue app study now. They identify what the real-machine baseline still needs to make explicit.

## Real-machine Run 2 priorities

### Machine-level question

Deepen why the A+ART architecture remains coherent over repeated play when a short real BONUS can interrupt but not erase persistent ART value. Reconstruct the complete seated loop rather than isolated premium states:

`NORMAL HOME → rare-role / state question → BONUS or CZ/ART connection → temporary foreground → persistent-state return or NORMAL HOME`

### Game & Reel

Return to real-machine evidence for actual reel arrays/roles where documented, normal aiming routine, rare-role follow-up, bonus detection, navigation responsibility, BONUS-in-ART handling, ART end/preparation/return, setting inference channels, and 100G/500G/3-hour responsibility changes. Never invent stop-control tables.

### Visual & Mechanism

Return to cabinet photographs and long-form real-machine footage. Resolve or narrow cabinet proportions, physical reel-window readability/depth, seated sightline, LCD↔reel gaze travel, operation-deck ergonomics, lamp/speaker placement where documented, REST VISUAL, state transitions and the visual re-anchor after temporary foreground states. Material/depth inference remains interpretation unless sourced.

### Sound & Experience

Return to long-form real-machine play and authoritative documentation. Map baseline BET/lever/reel/STOP/settling/payout/replay cadence where safely observable; distinguish repeated baseline cues from exceptional cues; study BONUS/ART foreground and audible return-to-home behavior; separate memorable impact from repeated fatigue. Do not reproduce protected sounds/music.

### Playtest & QA

Reconstruct first 5 minutes, normal 100G, 500G dry spell, BIG without ART, ART without exceptional extension, BIG during ART, return to normal, and ~3-hour fatigue from real-machine evidence. Keep relief after a hit separate from intrinsic quality of the preceding dry spell. Measure consequence closure, home recognition, re-anchor cost and concurrent open questions.
