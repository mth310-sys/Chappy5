## What changed
Playtest & QA now has a research-only candidate method for estimating attention efficiency on the target iPhone without eye tracking:

- do not require continuous introspection;
- use sparse, unannounced post-boundary probes;
- score detection/prediction against known game truth;
- pair confidence with correctness;
- log false alarms as well as misses;
- compare novice / learned / returning exposure;
- treat probe/self-monitoring reactivity as QA error;
- use short blocks to detect vigilance risk but long play to judge product cost.

Central principle:

`MEASURE THE CONSEQUENCES OF ATTENTION, NOT ONLY THE CLAIM OF ATTENTION.`

`truth-linked detection/prediction maintained or improved + unnecessary monitoring/error reduced + burden stable or reduced`
