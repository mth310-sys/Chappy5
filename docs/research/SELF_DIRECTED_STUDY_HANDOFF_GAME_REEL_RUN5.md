## Integrated model

`REPEATED OBSERVABLE INVARIANTS -> CANDIDATE-SET COMPRESSION -> BETTER BOUNDED PREDICTION -> LOWER DEFENSIVE MONITORING -> DIAGNOSTIC SURPRISE`

The key distinction is:

`MASTERY != FASTER REPETITION`

`MASTERY = THE SAME EVIDENCE LEAVES FEWER PLAUSIBLE CAUSAL / ROLE / ROUTE HYPOTHESES WITHOUT FALSE CERTAINTY ABOUT HIDDEN CONTROL.`

## Main principles

- `MASTERY SHOULD SHRINK THE LIVE HYPOTHESIS SET, NOT MERELY INCREASE PLAY SPEED.`
- `LEARN THE FAMILY OF LEGAL CONSEQUENCES; DO NOT REQUIRE IDENTICAL RESULTS.`
- `OBSERVABLE INVARIANTS SHOULD BE RELATIONAL, STATE-AWARE AND FALSIFIABLE.`
- `A PARTIAL STOP IS VALUABLE WHEN IT ELIMINATES POSSIBILITIES OR CHANGES RESPONSIBILITY.`
- `NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE — ROLE DENIAL / CONTRADICTION — IS PART OF REEL MASTERY.`
- `TRUE SKILL CAN INCLUDE A CALIBRATED UNKNOWN ABOUT HIDDEN CONTROL.`
- `DO NOT FILL PROPRIETARY CONTROL GAPS WITH CONFIDENT COUNTERFACTUALS.`
- `REPETITION SHOULD SHARPEN SURPRISE AUTHORITY; IT SHOULD NOT NORMALIZE ARBITRARINESS.`
- `LOWER MONITORING WITH PRESERVED DETECTION ACCURACY IS STRONGER MASTERY EVIDENCE THAN FASTER PLAY ALONE.`
