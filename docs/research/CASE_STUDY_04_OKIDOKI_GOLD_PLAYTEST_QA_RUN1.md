## 2. First 5 minutes — safe play before expert law knowledge

### SOURCED FACT

Ordinary procedure can be reduced to a clear safe routine: left BAR reference, classify the first stop, follow watermelon when that branch appears, otherwise complete the remaining stops at low precision cost.

### INTERPRETATION

A beginner does not need to know the full lamp-pattern dictionary, mode theory, cherry A/B statistics or BONUS sound-law exceptions to operate safely. The first five minutes can therefore teach a small motor contract:

`BET → LEVER → LEFT REFERENCE → CONDITIONAL FOLLOW → STOP → SETTLE → REPEAT`.

The primary beginner question is not “what hidden mode am I in?” but “can I complete a game without losing track of what to do?” On that criterion the machine is comparatively forgiving.

The risk is informational intimidation after the first hit: once the player learns that lamp patterns, voice variants, STOP-sound persistence and 32G can all carry future-state information, an apparently simple machine can suddenly look like a memorization test.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**A SIMPLE MACHINE MAY HAVE DEEP EXPERT LAW, BUT SAFE FIRST-SESSION OPERATION MUST NOT DEPEND ON THAT LAW.**

QA should separately score:
1. `SAFE OPERATION KNOWLEDGE`;
2. `OPTIONAL EXPECTATION KNOWLEDGE`;
3. `LONG-HORIZON INFERENCE KNOWLEDGE`.

If category 2 or 3 becomes mandatory for category 1, optional mastery has turned into friction.

## 3. Ordinary 100G — can the HOME become automatic without becoming empty?

### SOURCED FACT

At setting 1 the published BONUS first-hit rate is about 1/299.6. A 100G block therefore cannot be evaluated under the assumption that a BONUS must appear. The visible routine must survive many ordinary repetitions.

### INTERPRETATION

The physical loop is cheap enough to automate: only the left reference is routinely precise, with conditional escalation on watermelon. Unlike New Hanabi, the player is not continuously extracting reach-eye/slip evidence from every miss. Therefore intrinsic reel-reading satisfaction is lower by design.

What remains alive during an ordinary 100G is:
- the cadence of physical stopping;
- occasional role-classification duty;
- anticipation that the fixed direct-notification surface may change;
- optional monitoring of small clues relevant to mode/setting inference.

That can be restful, but only if the player is allowed to **not inspect every micro-deviation**. The Sound pass already identified silence/timing deviations as meaningful. QA therefore treats excessive self-monitoring as a real failure mode: if players feel they must audit every start, STOP and tiny sound difference, sparse presentation becomes vigilance work.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**AUTOMATIC OPERATION IS NOT THE SAME AS EMPTY OPERATION.**

A low-cost HOME passes 100G only when the player can alternate between:
`ROUTINE ATTENTION` and `EVENT ATTENTION`.

It fails when every routine game feels like a hidden test that might punish inattention.

## 4. 500G dry spell — the machine's hardest QA case

### SOURCED FACT

Normal A/B can extend to a maximum 999G. Public expectation-value guides explicitly model late-game value rising as the remaining ceiling distance contracts; these are strategy models rather than manufacturer guarantees, but they show why a 500G position is strategically different from a fresh 32G position.

Source:
- https://slot-seven.com/okidoki_gold-tenzyou/

### INTERPRETATION

A 500G dry spell exposes the central tension of this lineage. The machine has no continuous CZ ladder, no normal-mode mission chain and no New-Hanabi-style reach-eye discovery task to segment the wait. Repetition is therefore supported by three different motives that QA must not confuse:

1. `INTRINSIC PHYSICAL ROUTINE` — the tactile cadence remains comfortable;
2. `TIME/HORIZON VALUE` — the player is materially closer to a ceiling or may infer a favorable mode;
3. `RELIEF SEEKING` — the player simply wants the lamp finally to light because the wait has become aversive.

The third is not proof that the dry spell was engaging.

The field comments describing `ひりつく感じ` support tension as part of the product, but they do not establish that every long dry block is pleasant. A machine can be compelling and uncomfortable at the same time.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**DIRECT-NOTIFICATION QA MUST SEPARATE ANTICIPATION FROM RELIEF DEBT.**

After a first hit, ask two separate questions:
- `Was the notification/reward satisfying?`
- `Would the preceding 500G still be acceptable if the eventual reward were ordinary?`

If only the first passes, the machine is borrowing pleasure from the end of the wait rather than sustaining the wait itself.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**A DEEP DRY SPELL NEEDS A FATIGUE LEDGER, NOT A SINGLE “BORING / NOT BORING” SCORE.**

Log independently:
- motor fatigue;
- visual vigilance;
- acoustic vigilance;
- mode-inference burden;
- financial/ceiling commitment pressure;
- frustration from repeated non-events;
- comfort of the stable HOME.

## 5. Notification → first BONUS — reward versus relief

### SOURCED FACT

The primary visible contract is direct BONUS notification. BIG and REG then provide distinct local reward lengths/payouts. The notification does not itself guarantee the same downstream mode value every time.

### INTERPRETATION

A first light after a dry spell closes a very clear binary question: `not yet → now`. This is strong because it reduces interpretive debt at the same moment it creates reward anticipation.

But QA must separate four feelings:
1. `TRUTH SATISFACTION` — certainty arrived cleanly;
2. `LOCAL REWARD SATISFACTION` — BIG/REG itself is worth playing;
3. `RELIEF` — the wait finally ended;
4. `FUTURE-VALUE ANTICIPATION` — what happens after BONUS and within 32G.

If REG feels disappointing only because it is smaller than BIG, that is ordinary reward differentiation. If **every** BONUS feels like a failed prelude unless followed by heaven-class continuation, the local reward has been devalued by the 32G layer.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**A SHORT REWARD CAN BE SMALL WITHOUT BEING A FAILED REWARD.**

Evaluate BIG and REG before considering whether they lead into a chain.

---

## 6. BONUS play — same 3 STOPs, different attention contract

### SOURCED FACT

During BONUS, documented play can change stop-order responsibility. When navigation is absent, middle-first play can classify roles earlier; navigation can instead own the order. Published sound rules also make STOP1/STOP2/STOP3 persistence informative about 1G-chain expectation.

### INTERPRETATION

This is the most important source of **local engagement inside BONUS without technical-payout punishment**. The same three buttons become a temporal ruler:

`STOP1 → DID THE DEVIATION PERSIST? → STOP2 → STILL? → STOP3 → RESOLVE`.

A beginner may simply follow instructions and receive the reward. A skilled player can read additional temporal law without needing a new control surface.

This differs from New Hanabi mastery. Mastery here is less about improving payout through precise intervention and more about **recognizing future-state information earlier**.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**MASTERY CAN CHANGE WHAT THE SAME INPUT REVEALS WITHOUT CHANGING WHAT THE INPUT REQUIRES.**

This is valuable when optional expertise deepens prediction while basic operation remains safe.

---

## 7. BONUS → 32G — horizon compression as a real state change

### SOURCED FACT

Official and strategy materials make post-BONUS 32G central: heaven-class states resolve the next BONUS within that horizon, and the official product page explicitly says the new system makes the post-BONUS 32G more tense.

### INTERPRETATION

The player does not need a new screen layout or a new motor interface to feel the state change. What changes is the expected time-to-resolution.

The experiential transition is:

`LONG/UNCERTAIN HORIZON → BONUS LOCAL REWARD → 32G SHORT HORIZON`.

This naturally raises vigilance. QA should therefore **not** treat stronger continuous audio/visual density as necessary proof that 32G is special. If the player already knows the answer window is short, ordinary physical actions can acquire urgency by context alone.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**MEASURE STATE CHANGE BY PLAYER EXPECTATION, NOT ONLY BY PRESENTATION DELTA.**

A state can feel radically different while the cabinet HOME remains almost unchanged.

---

## 8. Repeated chain — celebration needs release

### SOURCED FACT

Heaven and higher modes support repeated BONUS within 32G; published sources give 80% loop for Doki-Doki and 90% for Super Doki-Doki, with guarantee handling after drop. Official promotion centers the machine on strong heaven-loop value.

### INTERPRETATION

Repeated chain play reverses the dry-spell problem. The risk is no longer too little event density, but **too little recovery between salient events**.

A good chain rhythm is not necessarily:
`MAXIMUM CUE → MAXIMUM CUE → MAXIMUM CUE`.

It can be:
`NOTIFICATION PEAK → BONUS ROUTINE → SHORT RELEASE → 32G TENSION → NEXT PEAK`.

Because the same music/cues can recur several times, repetition fatigue must be distinguished from reward fatigue. A player can still value the chain economically while becoming acoustically tired.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**REPEATED SUCCESS STILL NEEDS RECOVERY WINDOWS.**

Measure `ATTENTION RELEASE TIME` between consecutive high-salience moments, not just peak impact.

---

## 9. Chain end → normal — re-anchoring and the stop decision

### SOURCED FACT

32G is a meaningful boundary for heaven-class continuation. Strategy sources commonly treat 32G after BONUS as a basic stop/continue decision point, while specific mode evidence can justify continuation.

### INTERPRETATION

This machine turns `return to normal` into both a perceptual and economic decision. The player must recognize:
- the short horizon is over;
- the ordinary HOME has resumed;
- any remaining mode clue is optional/strategic context, not the same guarantee as heaven continuation.

The end can therefore be emotionally sharp even without a dramatic failure animation.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**A CLEANLY CLOSED SHORT HORIZON CAN BE MORE LEGIBLE THAN A LONG FAILURE ANIMATION.**

QA should record `RE-ANCHOR LATENCY`: how many games after the 32G boundary it takes before the player again treats ordinary BET→LEVER→STOP as the default rather than as “one more delayed chain check.”

## 10. Beginner versus skilled player

### BEGINNER MODEL

Likely attention hierarchy:
1. safe left-reference routine;
2. primary notification lamp;
3. simple BIG/REG distinction;
4. “stay through 32G after BONUS.”

The beginner can ignore most mode/lamp/sound-law detail and still complete games.

### SKILLED MODEL

Adds optional layers:
- conditional role classification and setting-relevant role categories;
- lamp-pattern / voice / STOP-sound future-state clues;
- mode-history and stop/continue inference;
- recognition of which deviations are truly confirming versus merely suggestive.

### INTERPRETATION

Mastery does not transform the three STOPs into New-Hanabi-style precision work. It transforms **what counts as evidence** around the same low-cost loop.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**EXPERTISE SHOULD COMPRESS UNCERTAINTY, NOT EXPAND BASIC OPERATING BURDEN.**

The skilled player should be able to know more without forcing the beginner to process everything.

## 11. Setting 1 versus higher setting — same HOME, different drought pressure

### SOURCED FACT

Published BONUS first-hit probability improves from about 1/299.6 at setting 1 to about 1/237.0 at setting 6. Some role frequencies also differ by setting.

### INTERPRETATION

The same cabinet and STOP routine can feel materially different because lower hit frequency increases the proportion of session time spent in ordinary waiting. Thus setting changes not only expected payout but **exposure duration to the baseline experience**.

This makes low-setting QA particularly important. If normal HOME is only pleasant when rewards arrive frequently, high-setting reports can hide a weak baseline.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**TEST THE BASELINE WHERE THE PLAYER IS EXPOSED TO IT LONGEST.**

For direct-notification machines, low-reward conditions are the strongest test of whether sparse play is genuinely restful or merely under-stimulated.

## 12. Three-hour fatigue model

QA should not reduce three-hour fatigue to “loud / not loud.” For this machine, six fatigue channels are separable:

| Fatigue channel | Likely source | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | repeated BET/lever/3STOP | probably low-to-moderate because precision duty is conditional |
| Visual vigilance | waiting for fixed lamp change | sparse visuals can still create constant monitoring |
| Acoustic vigilance | silence/delay/STOP-sound law | optional clues become tiring if treated as mandatory |
| Reward repetition | repeated BONUS music/cues in chains | positive events can still fatigue through repetition |
| Inference | mode/history/setting clues | expertise adds cognitive bookkeeping |
| Commitment | deep game count / ceiling proximity | later games can feel harder to abandon than earlier ones |

### INTERPRETATION

The machine's likely three-hour strength is a **stable low-cost physical HOME**. Its likely failure boundary is **hyper-vigilance plus commitment pressure**: the player may be physically comfortable but mentally unwilling to relax because the next lamp, the next 32G or the inferred mode could matter.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**LOW MOTOR COST CAN MASK HIGH VIGILANCE COST.**

Long-session QA must measure both.

## 13. Intrinsic satisfaction versus outcome satisfaction

For this lineage the following must be scored independently:

### Intrinsic reel-play satisfaction
- comfort of repeated physical stopping;
- clarity of conditional aiming duty;
- satisfaction of recognizing a role correctly;
- optional satisfaction from reading a STOP-linked sound law.

### Outcome satisfaction
- lamp notification certainty;
- BIG/REG payout;
- 1G-chain cue recognition;
- 32G continuation;
- repeated heaven-class chain.

### INTERPRETATION

Compared with New Hanabi, much more of Okin Doki GOLD's remembered reward is outcome-centered. That is not automatically a defect; it is a different lineage. The QA requirement is simply not to mislabel `I finally got the light` as proof that the preceding physical reel loop itself was rich.

### REUSABLE QA PRINCIPLE

**DO NOT CREDIT OUTCOME RELIEF TO THE INTRINSIC GAME LOOP.**

Keep separate scores for `LOOP QUALITY`, `HIT QUALITY`, and `POST-HIT HORIZON QUALITY`.

## 14. Contradictions and unresolved items

### UNRESOLVED — long-form video timing
Timestamp-level observation of beginner/expert eye behavior, missed cues, exact rest intervals and repeated-chain fatigue remains unresolved because stable video playback was not available in this run. Do not invent observations.

### UNRESOLVED — actual attention ratio
No reliable source gives percentages for time spent watching reels versus lamp versus counters/history. Treat all such ratios as unmeasured.

### UNRESOLVED — cue miss cost
The economic/strategic cost of missing every individual optional lamp/voice/sound clue has not been quantified. Some clues may alter optimal continue/quit decisions, but this study does not assign an invented expected-value penalty.

### CONTRADICTION TO WATCH
Strategy writing often presents many clue laws because it optimizes extraction. That can make the machine appear cognitively denser than ordinary players actually experience it. Future video observation must distinguish `available law` from `law actively monitored every game`.

### CONTRADICTION TO WATCH
A 500G position can carry increased strategy value because of ceiling proximity, while the lived 500G path can still be unpleasant. `VALUE TO CONTINUE` and `QUALITY OF THE PATH SO FAR` are not the same metric.

## 15. Run 1 QA synthesis

The Case Study 04 real-machine loop is reconstructed as:

`LOW-COST HOME → WAIT / OPTIONAL INFERENCE → DIRECT TRUTH SIGNAL → LOCAL BIG/REG REWARD → STOP-LINKED OPTIONAL PREDICTION → 32G HORIZON COMPRESSION → CHAIN OR CLEAN CLOSURE → RE-ANCHOR TO LOW-COST HOME`.

The machine's simplicity is therefore **not empty by default**. It concentrates mandatory duty into a cheap physical routine while placing much expert depth into optional prediction and continue/quit inference. The same property creates its main risk: because visible activity is sparse and the direct truth signal is valuable, players may become hyper-vigilant and treat every ordinary game as waiting for permission to feel something.

Run 1 QA judgment:

**THE REAL-MACHINE HOME IS PLAUSIBLY LOW-COST AND BEGINNER-SAFE, BUT LONG-SESSION QUALITY DEPENDS ON WHETHER THE PLAYER CAN RELAX BETWEEN TRUTH EVENTS. THE PRIMARY FAILURE BOUNDARY IS NOT THREE-STOP DIFFICULTY; IT IS VIGILANCE, RELIEF DEBT AND COMMITMENT PRESSURE DURING LONG DRY PLAY.**

New reusable principles from this run:

1. **A SIMPLE MACHINE MAY HAVE DEEP EXPERT LAW, BUT SAFE FIRST-SESSION OPERATION MUST NOT DEPEND ON THAT LAW.**
2. **AUTOMATIC OPERATION IS NOT THE SAME AS EMPTY OPERATION.**
3. **DIRECT-NOTIFICATION QA MUST SEPARATE ANTICIPATION FROM RELIEF DEBT.**
4. **A SHORT REWARD CAN BE SMALL WITHOUT BEING A FAILED REWARD.**
5. **MASTERY CAN CHANGE WHAT THE SAME INPUT REVEALS WITHOUT CHANGING WHAT THE INPUT REQUIRES.**
6. **MEASURE STATE CHANGE BY PLAYER EXPECTATION, NOT ONLY BY PRESENTATION DELTA.**
7. **REPEATED SUCCESS STILL NEEDS RECOVERY WINDOWS.**
8. **EXPERTISE SHOULD COMPRESS UNCERTAINTY, NOT EXPAND BASIC OPERATING BURDEN.**
