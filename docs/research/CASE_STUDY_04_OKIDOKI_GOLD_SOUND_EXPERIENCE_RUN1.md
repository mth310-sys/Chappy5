## 0. Run question

Case Study 04 is deliberately not a constant-CZ/AT-escalation machine and not a reach-eye/technical-intervention machine. Its visible contract is unusually concentrated: operate the same physical reels, wait for a direct truth signal, take a pseudo-BONUS, then immediately enter a compressed 32G continuation horizon.

The Sound & Experience question is therefore:

**HOW DOES A MACHINE WITH A LOW-COST REPEATED MOTOR LOOP USE ACOUSTIC ABSENCE, TIMING DEVIATION, DIRECT NOTIFICATION, STOP-SYNCHRONIZED VARIATION AND BONUS MUSIC TO MAKE SMALL EVENTS IMPORTANT WITHOUT KEEPING THE PLAYER IN A CONSTANT ACOUSTIC FOREGROUND?**

Evidence labels used below:
- `SOURCED FACT`
- `DIRECT REAL-MACHINE VIDEO/PAGE OBSERVATION`
- `FIELD REPORT`
- `INTERPRETATION`
- `REUSABLE PRINCIPLE`
- `UNRESOLVED / CONTRADICTION`

Protected melodies, voice lines, signature sound samples and exact proprietary sound assets are not reproduced.

## 1. Baseline acoustic HOME: what must stay ordinary

### SOURCED FACT

The documented normal game is structurally simple: ordinary play repeats BET / lever / reel rotation / three STOPs / settlement, with the hibiscus lamp functioning as the primary direct BONUS notification surface. Major beginner and strategy material describes the machine as primarily aiming for pseudo-BONUS from per-game roles and/or game-number selection, with BONUS indicated by hibiscus illumination.

Sources:
- https://hazuse.com/machine/pachislot/2S1205/genre/203/
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold/

### INTERPRETATION

For the direct notification to retain force over hundreds of games, the ordinary acoustic loop cannot behave as if every game is already an event. The useful reference model is:

`BET → LEVER → REEL MOTION → STOP1 → STOP2 → STOP3 → SETTLEMENT / PAYOUT / REPLAY → RELEASE ATTENTION`.

The exact waveform, loudness and mechanical/acoustic contribution of each ordinary sound remain unresolved, but the experience architecture requires a **trusted acoustic HOME**: a repeated sequence whose timing becomes predictable enough that a small departure can be noticed immediately.

This differs from an AT ladder where BGM layers, battle cues, narration and escalating effect sounds may continuously signal state progression. In 沖ドキ！GOLD, ordinary repetition is not merely empty time; it is the reference against which deviation is legible.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**A DIRECT-NOTIFICATION MACHINE NEEDS A TRUSTED ACOUSTIC HOME BEFORE IT NEEDS A LOUD NOTIFICATION.**

A signal becomes meaningful not only because of its absolute intensity, but because repeated ordinary timing gives the player a stable expectation to violate.

## 2. Silence is not lack of content; it can be positive information

### SOURCED FACT

Published normal-play law material documents several BONUS-confirming deviations:

- a delay / short lock in reel start timing;
- LED flash at role settlement;
- pre-illumination (`先チカ`);
- **no-sound / silence**, which is documented as BIG-confirming.

The same source documents that the short reel-start lock can occur on the game immediately before BONUS notification, with occurrence probability depending on the winning role.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold/59/
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/59/

### INTERPRETATION

This is one of the clearest differences from an escalation-first sound design. A strong event does not always add a new foreground sound. It may instead **remove an expected component**.

To notice `no sound`, the player must already know what normally belongs at that timing point. Silence therefore depends on learned repetition. It is not empty space; it is a contradiction between expected and observed acoustic grammar.

The same applies to a reel-start delay. The meaningful unit is not “a dramatic delay effect” in isolation but:

`EXPECTED LEVER-TO-REEL LATENCY → VIOLATED LATENCY → PLAYER DETECTS EXCEPTION`.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLES

**SILENCE CAN BE A HIGH-SALIENCE EVENT WHEN THE BASELINE TIMING IS TRUSTED.**

**TIMING DEVIATION CAN CARRY MORE INFORMATION THAN ADDED SOUND.**

**ACOUSTIC EMPHASIS CAN BE CREATED BY SUBTRACTING AN EXPECTED EVENT, NOT ONLY BY ADDING OUTPUT.**

This is the sound analogue of Visual Run 1's finding that panel blackout can strengthen the primary visual truth signal by subtracting competing light.

## 3. Direct notification: the event should close uncertainty, not create another decoding task

### SOURCED FACT

The hibiscus illumination is the machine's primary direct BONUS truth signal. Published material also documents multiple lamp patterns with mode implications, but the baseline illumination itself communicates the local BONUS result directly.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold/
- https://hazuse.com/machine/pachislot/2S1205/genre/203/

### INTERPRETATION

The acoustic partner to this visual event should be evaluated as a **closure event**, not merely as spectacle. Before notification, the player can spend a long dry interval in a low-information horizon. When the truth signal arrives, it should collapse the local question quickly:

`WAITING → EXCEPTION / DIRECT SIGNAL → BONUS KNOWN`.

The important experiential contrast is therefore not “quiet versus loud” alone. It is:

`UNCERTAIN / REPETITIVE → CERTAIN / LOCAL REWARD`.

A direct-notification architecture becomes weaker if the sound after the lamp still demands a long interpretive chain before the player knows whether the machine has actually paid off the wait.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**A DIRECT TRUTH SIGNAL SHOULD REDUCE INTERPRETIVE DEBT AT THE SAME MOMENT IT CLAIMS ATTENTION.**

Strong notification is useful when it closes a question, not when it merely opens another presentation layer.

## 4. BONUS entry adds a second acoustic grammar: indication, not just celebration

### SOURCED FACT

Published BONUS material documents BIG as 70G/about 210 coins and REG as 30G/about 90 coins. BONUS template/voice variations at symbol tension/entry can carry next-mode or benefit indications. Strategy sources document several distinct tension/voice patterns that indicate heaven-or-higher, doki-doki-or-higher, super-doki-doki-or-higher, or forthcoming music change.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/61/
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/62/
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/80/

### INTERPRETATION

BONUS audio therefore performs at least two jobs simultaneously:

1. **local reward atmosphere** — the machine has moved from waiting into a finite payout episode;
2. **future-state evidence** — entry/tension audio can change what the player expects after the current reward ends.

This is important because the machine's hidden state depth is temporal. The player is not simply asking “how exciting is this BIG?” but “what does this event imply about what may happen next, especially inside the next 32G?”

A useful decomposition is:

`NOTIFICATION = CLOSE CURRENT QUESTION`

`BONUS ENTRY AUDIO = MARK LOCAL REWARD + OPTIONALLY MODIFY FUTURE EXPECTATION`

`BONUS SUSTAIN = SUPPORT REPETITION WITHOUT MASKING STOP/NAV INFORMATION`

`BONUS END = HAND ATTENTION BACK TO THE 32G HORIZON`.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**REWARD AUDIO CAN CELEBRATE THE PRESENT WHILE QUIETLY RE-PRICING THE NEXT TIME HORIZON.**

The two functions should remain distinguishable so that celebration does not obscure actionable information.

## 5. BONUS stop sounds form a temporal information chain

### SOURCED FACT

During BONUS, published strategy material documents a preview sound (`予告音`) and changed STOP sounds. The duration of the changed STOP-sound sequence carries 1G-chain information:

- a weaker documented form continues through STOP1;
- a stronger form continues through STOP2;
- continuation through STOP3 is documented as a 1G-chain-confirming pattern;
- repeated preview/changed-stop patterns across consecutive games raise 1G-chain expectation;
- specific contradictions between role type and how long the changed STOP sound continues can also confirm the 1G chain.

Published values include, for example, stronger repeated patterns carrying much higher next-game 1G-chain expectation than isolated weak sequences. Exact samples are not reproduced here.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/61/
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/62/
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/80/
- https://p.hisshobon.jp/machine/4044/1/92975

### INTERPRETATION

This is a particularly reusable mechanism because the machine does not need a separate audiovisual cutscene to communicate increasing expectation. It uses the three physical STOPs themselves as a **time ruler**:

`PREVIEW → STOP1 SOUND STATE → STOP2 SOUND STATE → STOP3 SOUND STATE`.

Expectation grows according to **how long the altered acoustic condition survives through the player's own actions**.

The three STOPs therefore become an auditory countdown/continuation grammar even though the player is not performing Hanabi-like technical intervention.

The player is not just hearing an effect; they are actively revealing whether the effect persists.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLES

**PLAYER INPUT CAN SERVE AS THE CLOCK THAT REVEALS AN AUDIO EVENT.**

**A THREE-STOP INTERFACE CAN EXPRESS EXPECTATION THROUGH PERSISTENCE ACROSS STOP1 / STOP2 / STOP3, WITHOUT ADDING A SEPARATE SCREEN LAYER.**

**PERSISTENCE LENGTH IS AN INFORMATION DIMENSION DISTINCT FROM LOUDNESS.**

This parallels Visual Run 1's finding that the fixed lamp gains vocabulary from cadence, order, segmentation and direction rather than brightness alone.

## 6. Contradiction is a learned reward for experienced ears

### SOURCED FACT

Published BONUS law material documents role-dependent basic relationships between preview sound and changed STOP-sound duration, plus contradiction patterns that imply a 1G chain. It also documents a pattern where changed STOP sounds occur despite no preview sound on a navigated game, again carrying strong benefit information.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/80/
- https://www.atari7.com/slot/okidoki-gold.php

### INTERPRETATION

For a beginner, the safe contract is simple: follow navigation, complete the BONUS, and notice obvious notification/music changes. For an experienced player, repeated exposure teaches a finer grammar:

`EXPECTED PREVIEW/STOP SOUND RELATIONSHIP → MISMATCH → BENEFIT INFERENCE`.

This is analogous to a reach-eye contradiction, but it lives in **time and sound rather than reel geometry**.

It creates mastery without making mastery mandatory for safe operation. The beginner does not lose the core game by failing to decode every acoustic contradiction; the expert receives extra predictive pleasure.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**OPTIONAL MASTERY CAN LIVE IN VIOLATIONS OF A SAFE BASELINE GRAMMAR.**

A machine can reward trained listening without turning every game into a listening exam.

## 7. Music change is state evidence, not decorative playlist rotation

### SOURCED FACT

Published strategy material documents several BONUS music changes tied to 1G-chain and/or higher-mode benefits. One documented music state indicates a 1G chain; another carries additional future BONUS benefit; another indicates 1G chain plus doki-doki-mode-level benefit.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/66/
- https://p.hisshobon.jp/machine/4044/1/92975

### INTERPRETATION

The important lesson is not the melody identity. It is that **BGM entry itself changes the player's model of the future**.

This makes music a persistent state marker:

`ORDINARY BONUS MUSIC → SPECIAL MUSIC ENTRY → FUTURE BENEFIT KNOWN / STRONGLY REPRICED`.

Because BGM persists longer than a one-shot SE, it can carry a larger piece of context without requiring the player to remember a fleeting flash. But persistent music also occupies more attention and has higher fatigue potential, so it is most defensible when it represents persistent value rather than merely repeating intensity.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLES

**PERSISTENT AUDIO SHOULD REPRESENT PERSISTENT VALUE OR CONTEXT.**

**A MUSIC CHANGE IS MOST USEFUL WHEN IT CHANGES THE PLAYER'S FUTURE MODEL, NOT ONLY THE CURRENT MOOD.**

No melody, lyric, arrangement or recognizable protected music is copied into Chappy5 research.

## 8. BONUS navigation voice: action information must outrank decorative information

### SOURCED FACT

Published BONUS material documents navigation voice variation; changed navigation phrasing can itself indicate a 1G-chain benefit. Regardless of implication, navigation audio is temporally tied to player action/order responsibility.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold_30/80/

### INTERPRETATION

When an audio channel carries stop-order responsibility, it belongs to a higher priority class than decorative ambience. The sound hierarchy should be evaluated as:

1. `ACTION-REQUIRED` — navigation/order/timing necessary to play correctly;
2. `RESULT / TRUTH` — direct notification and confirmed benefit;
3. `PREDICTIVE` — preview/STOP persistence/contradictions/mode implication;
4. `ATMOSPHERE` — BONUS BGM and identity bed;
5. `ORDINARY FEEDBACK` — routine operation and payout cadence.

If atmosphere masks action-required information, the experience fails even if the soundtrack is exciting.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**ACTION-REQUIRED AUDIO MUST WIN THE MIX OVER REWARD ATMOSPHERE.**

This remains a design principle only; no machine-specific mix ratio or dB target is inferred.

## 9. The 32G post-BONUS horizon: vigilance should rise without requiring 32G of foreground sound

### SOURCED FACT

Published machine material documents heaven-class modes with a 32G horizon. After BONUS, 32G is therefore a meaningful continuation window, and common strategy guidance treats 32G as a major continue/quit boundary.

Sources:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold/
- https://uchidokoro.com/machines/okidoki_gold/

### INTERPRETATION

Game & Reel Run 1 identified the key architecture as **resolution-horizon compression**. Visual Run 1 found that the physical HOME stays stable while vigilance rises. Sound should be evaluated against the same requirement.

The strongest model is not:

`BONUS ENDS → 32G OF CONTINUOUS HIGH-INTENSITY AUDIO`.

It is:

`BONUS END → ATMOSPHERE RELEASE / HOME RECOVERY → EACH LEVER AGAIN MATTERS MORE → EXCEPTION CUES REMAIN HIGH-SALIENCE → NOTIFICATION OR 32G CLOSURE`.

The player can be more vigilant because the expected time to answer is short, even if the baseline sound itself remains restrained.

This distinction matters for fatigue. If every one of the 32 games is acoustically foregrounded merely because the horizon is important, repeated chains multiply listening duty rapidly.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLES

**A SHORTER REWARD HORIZON CAN RAISE VIGILANCE WITHOUT RAISING CONTINUOUS ACOUSTIC INTENSITY.**

**STATE PRESENCE DOES NOT REQUIRE CONTINUOUS LISTENING DUTY.**

**POST-REWARD AUDIO SHOULD RE-ANCHOR THE PLAYER BEFORE ASKING FOR THE NEXT EXCEPTION.**

## 10. Dry 100G / 500G: fatigue comes from vigilance, not only volume

### SOURCED FACT

Published setting-1 first-BONUS rate is around 1/299.6, while normal A/B can extend to a 999G ceiling. A 500G section without the primary notification is therefore a relevant field condition, not an absurd QA edge case.

Source:
- https://1geki.jp/slot/s_okidoki_gold/

### INTERPRETATION

The danger in a sparse direct-notification machine is **hyper-vigilance**: the player may spend hundreds of games waiting for a single deviation. Even if each ordinary sound is individually mild, the cognitive question “was that different?” can produce fatigue if too many ambiguous cues demand scrutiny.

The healthiest sparse loop therefore needs:

- ordinary operation sounds that become automatic rather than ambiguous;
- exceptional cues that are reliably different enough to notice;
- optional expert layers that do not punish beginners for ignoring them;
- regular attention release after non-events;
- no requirement to audit every ordinary STOP sound for hidden meaning during normal dry play.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**SPARSE PRESENTATION FAILS WHEN THE PLAYER MUST CONTINUOUSLY AUDIT THE BASELINE FOR POSSIBLE MICRO-DEVIATIONS.**

Sparse does not automatically mean restful. Rest requires **trust that ordinary really is ordinary**.

## 13. Experience model across the complete loop

### First 5 minutes — INTERPRETATION

A beginner can safely learn a small acoustic contract:

`ordinary game sounds = continue routine`

`strong deviation / hibiscus truth event = BONUS`

`navigation = obey`

`music or unusual BONUS cues = something extra may be happening`.

The player does not need to memorize all preview/STOP contradiction laws to operate the machine safely.

### Ordinary 100G — INTERPRETATION

The repeated operation should become background enough that the player can watch the primary visual truth surface while still feeling each lever and STOP. Small timing exceptions matter because the reference timing is stable.

### 500G dry play — INTERPRETATION

The central QA risk is not absence of a soundtrack event but whether waiting for deviation becomes mentally expensive. If the player must listen as intensely on game 487 as on a BONUS game, the hierarchy has failed.

### Notification → BONUS — SOURCED FACT + INTERPRETATION

Direct visual truth collapses the wait. BONUS introduces a finite reward rhythm and a richer predictive acoustic grammar: tension/voice variation, preview sounds, STOP-sound persistence, navigation variation and possible music change.

### BONUS → 32G — INTERPRETATION

The reward ends but expectation does not return immediately to ordinary scale. The information horizon compresses to 32G. Sound should permit physical HOME recovery while keeping exception cues maximally legible.

### Chain → repeat — INTERPRETATION

Each new notification closes one short question and opens another BONUS/32G cycle. Repetition remains satisfying only if the audio system still provides release between foreground claims.

### Chain end → normal — INTERPRETATION

Once the 32G question closes without another BONUS, the machine must feel able to return to ordinary acoustic HOME rather than leaving a phantom “still in event” foreground.

### Three hours — INTERPRETATION

Likely fatigue channels:

- notification vigilance during dry play;
- repeated high-salience reward events during long chains;
- BONUS BGM persistence;
- navigation listening duty;
- expert monitoring of preview/STOP contradiction and mode implication;
- hall noise forcing stronger listening effort.

Likely fatigue reducers:

- predictable ordinary cadence;
- clear difference between ordinary and exceptional cue;
- silence/absence as an occasional high-information device rather than constant emptiness audit;
- action audio priority;
- state audio that releases attention when no action is needed.

Playtest & QA should treat these as hypotheses to reconstruct from beginner/expert repeated play, not as final pass/fail conclusions.

## 14. Direct comparison with prior cases

### Versus スマスロ北斗の拳

北斗 can sustain value through visible state progression and continuation-oriented presentation. 沖ドキ！GOLD can create comparable urgency by **compressing time-to-next-answer** while leaving the motor/audio HOME structurally similar.

### Versus 新ハナビ

新ハナビ rewards trained perception of reel geometry, stop position, slip, reach-eye and technical intervention. 沖ドキ！GOLD offers optional trained perception in a different channel: **timing, absence, STOP-sound persistence and contradiction**.

### Versus まどマギ2

まどマギ2 Run 3 exposed fatigue from overlapping unresolved questions and persistent attention claims. 沖ドキ！GOLD provides a useful counter-case: the visible current question can be extremely simple, but a sparse architecture can still become tiring if the player remains hyper-vigilant for a single truth signal.

### REUSABLE PRINCIPLE

**LOW INFORMATION DENSITY AND LOW ATTENTION COST ARE NOT THE SAME THING.**

## 16. Contradictions / unresolved items for later passes

### UNRESOLVED — ordinary machine sound inventory

Authoritative sources found in this pass document exceptional sound laws far more clearly than ordinary BET / lever / reel / STOP / payout / replay samples. The exact default acoustic event inventory should not be guessed.

### UNRESOLVED — notification audio timing

The visual hibiscus truth event is well documented; this pass does not claim an exact millisecond relationship among lamp onset, any associated notification sound, reel start and STOP availability without a machine-specific clean timing source.

### UNRESOLVED — 32G background treatment

The gameplay importance of 32G is sourced. A machine-specific rule stating that 32G uses a unique continuous BGM bed was not established in this pass, so no such claim is made.

### UNRESOLVED — acoustic fatigue rate

No calibrated long-session dataset establishes how often each exceptional sound occurs per 100G/500G/3h. Playtest reconstruction should avoid inventing cue densities.

### UNRESOLVED — hall masking

Long-form hall recordings are unsuitable for deriving exact intelligibility, SPL or low-frequency conclusions. A later app comparison may reveal which cues were considered essential enough to preserve on phone speakers, but app evidence must not be back-projected as proof of real-machine hardware.

## 17. Handoff to Playtest & QA Director

Playtest & QA should now reconstruct the same real-machine loop with explicit attention to **listening duty**, not just event presence.

Required QA questions:

1. Can a beginner safely play the first five minutes without knowing preview/STOP contradiction laws?
2. During ordinary 100G, does the baseline become automatic enough that silence/delay/direct notification remains obvious?
3. During a 500G dry section, does the player rest between games or continuously audit for micro-deviation?
4. On notification, is satisfaction generated by the event itself, or mainly relief that waiting ended?
5. During BIG/REG, which cues are action-required, predictive, celebratory or optional-expert information?
6. Does preview/STOP persistence make the same three STOPs more engaging during BONUS without increasing mandatory motor difficulty?
7. Does a music change alter future expectation clearly enough to justify its persistent foreground presence?
8. After BONUS, can 32G feel more urgent **without** 32G of continuous acoustic pressure?
9. During repeated chains, is there enough release between foreground claims to avoid celebration fatigue?
10. When the 32G chain ends, how quickly does the player feel acoustically back at HOME?
11. Across ~3h, separate fatigue from loudness, vigilance, navigation duty, music repetition, mode inference and hall masking.

Machine Director should later integrate this Sound pass with Game & Reel's `RESOLUTION HORIZON` and Visual's `FIXED PHYSICAL SIGNAL / RESERVED CONTRAST` findings.
