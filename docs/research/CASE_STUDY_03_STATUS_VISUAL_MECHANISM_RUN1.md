## What this run established

- Latest main confirms Case Study 03 is the active shared object; smartphone-app study remains deferred to Phase B.
- The real cabinet is `screen-first / body-map-stable`: a large landscape LCD dominates information area, while a separate compact physical reel cavity and fixed lever/MAX BET/3 STOP coordinates remain intact.
- Static cabinet identity is carried by the fixed silhouette, top-center translucent/jewel-like element, luminous side/perimeter regions, light/dark massing and lower illuminated panel; a giant continuously moving mechanism is not required for hall recognition.
- Physical reels remain a dedicated work surface despite occupying less area than the LCD. The bezel/occlusion/recess cues make them read as physical rotating strips rather than flat screen icons.
- Game & Reel Run 1's BAR/cherry/watermelon routine remains visually plausible because the reel area is fenced off and directly mapped to the STOP row.
- State complexity lives mainly in the LCD/context layer while the player's physical coordinates remain stable across normal / CZ / BONUS / ART / extension.
- The LCD has a justified role after STOP3: the reels can resolve the visible role while hidden-state/CZ/BONUS/ART consequence remains open and is carried by subsequent screen/state presentation.
- BIG can temporarily move the LCD into a direct choice surface (published touch selection of notification type/BGM) without replacing the physical reel controls.
- BONUS-inside-ART is treated as a temporary foreground reward layer over a persistent value contract, not evidence that the physical/motor state must be rebuilt.
- Material and exact depth claims are not promoted to fact; exact cabinet dimensions, speaker map, material composition, lamp-device map and tactile mechanisms remain unresolved.
- No protected artwork, character design, proprietary cabinet ornament or signature effect was copied.

## Key principles handed forward

- `SCREEN-FIRST DOES NOT HAVE TO MEAN REEL-IRRELEVANT.`
- `STATE COMPLEXITY CAN LIVE ABOVE A STABLE PHYSICAL BODY MAP.`
- `STOP3 CAN CLOSE ROLE IDENTITY WHILE PRESENTATION KEEPS OUTCOME OPEN.`
- `NAVIGATION SHOULD ANNOTATE THE BODY MAP, NOT REPLACE IT.`
- `REST VISUAL IS AN ACTIVE BASELINE, NOT AN ABSENCE OF PRESENTATION.`
- `STATIC FORM CAN CARRY IDENTITY SO DYNAMIC CHANGE CAN BE RESERVED FOR GAME MEANING.`
- `DISTANCE PRESENCE AND SEATED INFORMATION SHOULD BE DESIGNED AS DIFFERENT SCALES.`
- `VISUAL HIERARCHY SHOULD FOLLOW INFORMATION OWNERSHIP, NOT PIXEL AREA.`
