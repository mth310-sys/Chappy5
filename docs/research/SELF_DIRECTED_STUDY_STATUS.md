## Human directive
After each fixed Director has completed their current Technology Gap Audit answer, that Director enters a self-directed learning period.

This is not a new machine Case Study. Human does not prescribe the research topic.

Each Director must independently ask:

> `What do I still not know, in my own domain, that Chappy5 must understand to create a genuinely fun pachislot machine with convincing real-machine presence?`

The research target must be chosen for Chappy5 learning value, not ease of searching.

## Fixed Directors
- Machine Director
- Game & Reel Director
- Visual & Mechanism Director
- Sound & Experience Director
- Playtest & QA Director

Each Director may choose a different question. Independent specialist uncertainty is desirable.

## Topic-selection rule
A self-directed topic should originate from one or more of:
- questions created by prior Hokuto / Shin Hanabi / Madoka 2 / Oki Doki GOLD studies;
- an area whose understanding is still shallow;
- knowledge missing for believable real-machine construction;
- a pattern only visible by comparing multiple machine lineages;
- uncertainty about how real manufacturers design a system or experience;
- browser/iPhone translation problems;
- game-development technology, rendering, audio, UI/UX, ergonomics, measurement or quality-evaluation knowledge that pachislot Case Studies exposed but did not answer.

Do not repeat already-saved machine facts merely to create volume.

## Allowed research sources
As appropriate to the chosen specialist question:
- real machines and multiple-machine comparison;
- manufacturer official material;
- developer interviews;
- strategy/analysis material;
- long-form real-machine video;
- officially licensed smartphone ports;
- game-development technology;
- graphics/rendering research;
- audio research;
- UI/UX and human factors;
- quality and measurement methods.

Third-party IP, signature presentation, cabinet trade dress, copyrighted audio/visual assets and proprietary implementation details are not to be copied. Study the reusable design/technical/experience principle behind them.

## Evidence discipline
Where possible, explicitly separate:
- `SOURCED FACT`
- `OBSERVATION`
- `INTERPRETATION`
- `REUSABLE PRINCIPLE`
- `UNRESOLVED`

Do not promote unverified inference to fact.

## Required final record — every Director
Each self-directed study document must end with all ten items:
1. Why this topic was chosen.
2. What was not understood before the study.
3. What was investigated.
4. What was newly learned.
5. What changed from the prior understanding.
6. Reusable design principles for Chappy5.
7. Conditions where those principles do not apply, and risks of misuse.
8. What remains unknown.
9. What is worth studying next.
10. How this may help future Chappy5 production.

## Prohibited actions
This phase is study only.

Do not:
- start a new machine production project;
- modify or rank original concepts;
- finalize game specifications;
- create cabinet production assets;
- implement code;
- create prototypes;
- cross Human Gate.

If research creates an idea, record it only as `FUTURE_CONSIDERATION_CANDIDATE` without selecting or implementing it.
