## Current strategy evidence

- low Threat / shallow states tend to favor deep;
- higher Threat makes calm valuable;
- an active chain materially increases resonance value;
- previous one-step aggregate split was about 58.7% deep / 29.0% resonance / 12.3% calm.

These facts show local role identity, not durable strategic balance.

### Structural resonance pressure — GS-010 / GS-011 / EX-012

- one-step adaptive: roughly 10.9–11.2 permanent bank/run in repeated probes;
- fixed resonance: roughly 11.8–12.1 and lower collapse in repeated probes;
- production math explains the gap: established resonance compounds future value while deep has lower efficiency and higher risk;
- a shallow future-aware Monte Carlo policy improves on one-step adaptation and uses multiple routes, but still does not clearly beat fixed resonance;
- therefore strategic clearance remains blocked.

### First narrow intervention candidate — simulation only

Executive explored a slower resonance continuation slope, changing bonus growth conceptually from `1 + chainLen*2` to `1 + chainLen` without modifying production. In small deterministic probes, fixed resonance fell to roughly 10.61–10.69 bank/run, while a mixed future-aware policy produced roughly 10.85–11.31 with route shares around 40% resonance / 36–38% deep / 22–23% calm. This is exploratory `SIMULATED` evidence only. It is **not approved production tuning** until reproduced by the Systems harness.
