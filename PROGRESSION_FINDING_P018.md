# P-018 — Persistent progression must not subsidize active-chain preservation before switching cost is cleared

- **Director:** Progression & Content Analysis Director
- **Status:** BLOCKED_DESIGN_GATE / ACTIVE_CHAIN_SWITCH_COST_CONFIRMED
- **Severity (1-5):** 4
- **Confidence:** HIGH for short-horizon switching-cost evidence / MEDIUM-HIGH for progression-design consequence
- **Verification Type:** OBSERVED + SIMULATED + DESIGN ANALYSIS
- **Last updated:** 2026-08-26 14:35 JST

## Evidence

Executive EX-018 superseded the pre-TQ-018 EX-017 promotion evidence. After per-Run RNG isolation, neither mature-chain taper nor `+5` cap cleared fixed resonance, and a one-step secured-bank objective policy also remained materially below fixed resonance. High-chain reward tuning is therefore not a cleared foundation for progression.

Systems GS-016 then sampled **105,401** active-chain states and found that calm/deep is locally better by one-step secured-bank EV in **52.76%** of states, yet adding only a minimal next-resonance option term flips **21.30%** of those locally attractive switch states back toward resonance.

Executive EX-019 strengthened that result with a direct common-RNG branch comparison across **104,736** active-chain states. Calm/deep is locally ahead in **52.52%** of states, but after forcing switch versus resonance and following the same contextual continuation policy:

- **2 decisions:** switch wins **28.84%**, resonance wins **37.06%**, ties **34.10%**, mean `switch - resonance` secured bank **-0.9701**.
- **3 decisions:** switch wins **26.17%**, resonance wins **35.56%**, ties **38.28%**, mean `switch - resonance` secured bank **-1.2477**.

These are `SIMULATED` results, not `HUMAN_VERIFIED` evidence of fun, replay desire, or human strategy.

## Progression interpretation

The active-chain switching-cost dependency is no longer merely a suspected future-value issue. Short-horizon branch evidence confirms that accumulated chain renewal materially pushes locally attractive calm/deep switches back toward resonance.

Therefore the durable progression constraint is:

**Until the core creates genuinely viable chain-sacrifice states, persistent progression must not make an existing chain easier to preserve, more expensive to abandon, or more profitable merely for staying on resonance.**

That includes reduced chain-loss chance, free signal matching, chain insurance, larger continuation payout, resonance-only extraction multipliers, automatic chain renewal, or upgrades whose main value is avoiding the sacrifice involved in switching to calm/deep. These effects would amplify the exact pressure currently blocking strategic clearance.

The more promising long-term axis is not preservation power but **what becomes worth pursuing after sacrifice**. If the core later establishes valid switch states, a persistent commitment may change the alternative objective, information, extraction horizon, or post-switch payoff structure while keeping the sacrifice real.

## Recommended Action

1. Keep persistent progression implementation blocked while Executive directly compares production hidden-50 chain exit against visible-signal causality under the same 2–3 decision common-RNG branch probe.
2. Do not revive P-017's cap-specific promotion premise; the `+5` cap is not a cleared balance invariant.
3. Preserve the durable constraints from P-013 through P-016: no permanent named-route affinity, no flat resonance power, and no progression tied to simulator-sensitive coefficients.
4. After strategic clearance, require the first tiny progression prototype to demonstrate at least two viable intentions that differ in **pursuit plus extraction/sacrifice timing** over several decisions, not merely one-step EV.
5. Prefer effects that alter the value of an alternative objective after abandoning accumulated state rather than refunding or insuring the abandonment cost.
6. Reject any prototype that mainly insures an active chain against switching cost unless the cleared core proves that such insurance preserves contextual choice.
7. Do not add enemies, items, stages, discovery names, or lore quantity to mask the absence of run-to-run strategic growth.

## Content / longevity consequence

The existing long-term failures remain unchanged: persistent bank/runs/discoveries do not alter later decisions; six anomaly selections exhaust all six discoveries; collection lacks pursuit agency; discovery identities have no durable presentation or authored revelation structure; additional banked salvage has no marginal consequence.

The active-chain gate still exposes a potentially strong repeatable tension: **preserve accumulated state versus sacrifice it for a different objective**. If both sides become genuinely viable, that tension can support repeated runs with very little added content. If preservation remains structurally favored, meta progression built on top of it would shorten strategic lifespan by making one commitment increasingly obvious.
