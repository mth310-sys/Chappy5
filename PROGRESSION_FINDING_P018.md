# P-018 — Persistent progression must not subsidize active-chain preservation before switching cost is cleared

- **Director:** Progression & Content Analysis Director
- **Status:** BLOCKED_DESIGN_GATE / ACTIVE_CHAIN_SWITCH_COST_DEPENDENCY
- **Severity (1-5):** 4
- **Confidence:** HIGH for corrected benchmark dependency / MEDIUM-HIGH for progression-design consequence
- **Verification Type:** OBSERVED + SIMULATED + DESIGN ANALYSIS
- **Last updated:** 2026-08-26 13:36 JST

## Evidence

Executive EX-018 supersedes the pre-TQ-018 EX-017 promotion evidence. After per-Run RNG isolation, the visible-signal causal-exit plus mature-chain `+5` cap does **not** make a contextual policy beat fixed resonance. Under the corrected deterministic probe, fixed resonance remains ahead in all tested high-chain shapes: production `12.095225` vs best contextual `11.856475`; taper `11.905175` vs `11.733650`; cap `11.732800` vs `11.635775`. The former EX-017 contextual `+0.70%` lead is invalid as a promotion premise.

EX-018 also tested a one-step secured-bank objective policy (`bank-ev`). It remains materially below fixed resonance (`10.856575` vs `12.095225` in production), weakening the explanation that resonance dominance is mainly caused by a hand-weighted contextual evaluator. Executive's current gate is therefore the opportunity cost of abandoning an already-active resonance chain for calm/deep: how often switching is locally attractive, how much future chain value is surrendered, and whether meaningful switch states actually exist.

These are `SIMULATED` results. They are not `HUMAN_VERIFIED` evidence of fun, replay desire, or player strategy.

## Progression interpretation

This changes the long-term design constraint. P-017 correctly warned against selling high-chain growth back through meta progression, but its `ROBUSTNESS_PENDING` premise is obsolete because the cap candidate failed corrected strategic clearance. The stronger invariant now is broader:

**Until active-chain switching cost is understood and the core is strategically cleared, persistent progression must not make an existing chain easier to preserve, more expensive to abandon, or more profitable merely for staying on resonance.**

That includes permanent effects such as reduced chain-loss chance, free signal matching, chain insurance, larger continuation payout, resonance-only extraction multipliers, or upgrades whose main value is avoiding the sacrifice involved in switching to calm/deep. Any of these could amplify the exact structural pressure currently under investigation and turn a narrow core imbalance into a permanent build trap.

The useful progression direction remains decision semantics rather than raw chain power. If the core later establishes genuine switch states, a small persistent commitment may change **why or when** the player chooses to sacrifice a chain, what information they receive about that sacrifice, or what alternative objective they pursue afterward. It should not simply make preservation dominant.

## Recommended Action

1. Keep persistent progression implementation blocked until Executive/Systems resolve the active-chain switching-cost gate.
2. Supersede P-017's cap-specific promotion premise; do not treat the `+5` cap as a cleared future balance invariant.
3. Preserve the more durable constraints from P-013 through P-016: no permanent named-route affinity, no flat resonance power, and no progression tied to simulator-sensitive coefficients.
4. After strategic clearance, require the first tiny progression prototype to demonstrate at least two viable intentions that differ in **pursuit plus extraction/sacrifice timing**, not merely expected bank.
5. Reject any prototype that mainly insures an active chain against the cost of switching, unless the cleared core explicitly proves that such insurance preserves rather than collapses contextual choice.
6. Do not add enemies, items, stages, discovery names, or lore quantity to mask the absence of run-to-run strategic growth.

## Content/longevity consequence

The current long-term failures remain unchanged: persistent bank/runs/discoveries do not alter later decisions; six anomaly selections exhaust all six discoveries; collection lacks pursuit agency; discovery identities have no durable presentation or authored revelation structure; additional banked salvage has no marginal consequence.

The active-chain gate is nevertheless useful for eventual longevity design because it identifies a possible repeatable tension already inside the core: **preserve accumulated state versus sacrifice it for a different immediate or future objective**. If the core can make both sides genuinely viable, that tension can support repeated runs with a small amount of persistent choice and without content inflation. If preservation remains overwhelmingly dominant, building progression on top of it would shorten strategic lifespan by making one commitment increasingly obvious.
