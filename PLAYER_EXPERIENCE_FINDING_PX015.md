# Player Experience Finding PX-015 — reduced resonance compounding needs a reward-feel gate, not just a balance gate

Updated: 2026-08-26 07:11 JST
Target: latest `main` ECHO DRIFT / preserved `HUMAN_CANDIDATE_01`
Director: Player Experience Analysis Director

- Status: CANDIDATE_PX_GATE
- Severity(1-5): 3
- Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH
- Verification Type: OBSERVED + SIMULATED
- Evidence: Production `game.js` still presents resonance as `同系統を繋ぐほど回収が加速` and, on a matching resonance route, exposes the exact continuation bonus generated by `1 + (run.chainLen+1)*2`. GS-012 independently supports a narrow balance candidate that halves only this compounding slope to `1 + chainLen`: in its bounded future-aware probe the candidate reduced resonance selection from about 48.6% to 39.0% and allowed mixed future-aware play (≈11.03 bank/run) to beat fixed resonance (≈10.40), while production remains strategically blocked by resonance pressure. The same lever that improves strategic diversity is therefore also the lever that creates the visible feeling of a chain accelerating. Halving it can improve choice quality while simultaneously weakening the route's most legible escalating reward signal.
- Player Experience Impact: From a player-facing perspective, the desired outcome is not merely `resonance is picked less`. Resonance should still produce a noticeable sense that maintaining a signal chain matters and that a successful match is more exciting than an ordinary route resolution. If the reduced slope is adopted and the chain becomes numerically healthy but emotionally flat, the game may trade a systems exploit for a weaker payoff loop. Conversely, preserving the current *2 solely because the larger number looks exciting would retain the structural dominance identified by GS-011/GS-012. The correct PX gate is therefore to separate reward magnitude from reward presentation: first make the economy strategically healthy, then verify whether the smaller but still increasing bonus is perceived as acceleration.
- Recommended Action: Do not change `HUMAN_CANDIDATE_01`. If Executive later promotes the `1 + chainLen` candidate into a controlled playable, keep the route identity and visible continuation bonus legible and run a focused A/B human check against the frozen candidate. The minimum new question for that balance revision should be: `共鳴が続いた時、回収が加速している感じは残っていたか？` Do not add this question to the current three-question first-session package because the current build still uses *2. If the reduced candidate is strategically superior but feels weak, prefer a small non-economic feedback improvement on the existing radar/log/number reaction over restoring the excessive compounding coefficient.

## Interpretation boundary

This finding does not claim the reduced candidate will feel worse; no human has played it. GS-012 is `SIMULATED` evidence about strategy, not `HUMAN_VERIFIED` evidence about excitement. PX-015 records the acceptance criterion that should accompany any future production balance change: **mixed play must improve without erasing the felt reward of maintaining resonance.**
